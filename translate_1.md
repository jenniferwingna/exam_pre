**DISCUSSION PAPER: COMMUNITY CANTEENS IN INTEGRATED YOUTH CENTRES**

**讨论文件：综合青少年中心的社区饭堂**

---

**PURPOSE**
This paper (a) identifies two major problems in introducing community canteens in integrated youth centres and sets out their implications; and (b) recommends measures to address them.

**目的**
本文件(a)指出在综合青少年中心推行社区饭堂所面对的两大问题，并说明其影响；(b)建议相应的应对措施。

---

**BACKGROUND**
The canteens have three objectives: to provide affordable nutrition, to strengthen connection with the neighbourhood, and to identify needs at an early stage. The third objective distinguishes the scheme from an ordinary meal service. It is also the easiest to lose sight of, because meals can be counted but identification cannot. The scheme should therefore be assessed on two points: whom it reaches that existing services have not reached, and what happens to them afterwards.

**背景**
饭堂有三项目标：提供可负担的营养膳食、加强邻里联系，以及及早识别需要。第三项目标使本计划有别于一般膳食服务，但也最容易被忽略，因为膳食数量可以统计，识别成效则难以量度。因此，评估本计划应着眼两点：计划接触到哪些现有服务未能接触的人士，以及这些人士其后的情况。

On this basis, the trial results give cause for concern. The trial reached elders, who are already well served by existing meal services. It barely reached carers and families. Very few of the needs identified led to support. The two problems below explain why.

以此衡量，试行计划的结果令人关注。计划接触到的是长者，而长者已是现有膳食服务覆盖最好的群体。照顾者和家庭则几乎没有接触到。试行期间识别的需要，亦极少转化为支援。以下两个问题说明了原因。

---

**PROBLEM 1 — PREMISES AND HOURS DESIGNED FOR A DIFFERENT PURPOSE**
Youth centres have spare capacity mainly when young people are at school. This suits elders. It does not suit carers, who are occupied with caring or work during the day, or school-age children with special needs. The groups the scheme most wants to reach are therefore available only when the centres are busiest. Physical constraints add to the problem. Three-quarters of the selected centres do not have kitchens suitable for daily meal production. The trial also relied on youth workers to serve meals.

**问题一：场地及开放时间原为另一用途而设**
青少年中心主要在青少年上学时有空档。这个时段适合长者，但不适合日间要照顾家人或工作的照顾者，也不适合有特殊需要的学童。因此，本计划最希望接触的群体，只有在中心最繁忙的时段才有空。实际条件使问题更为严重：四分之三的获选中心没有适合每日制备膳食的厨房，而试行计划又依赖青少年工作者供餐。

*Implications.* First, coverage would be skewed. As currently designed, the scheme would become a network of lunch clubs for elders. It would duplicate existing elderly services and leave carers and families largely unreached. Second, the 12-month target may lead centres to cook in unsuitable kitchens. A single food safety incident involving frail diners would damage confidence in the whole programme, not only in the centre concerned. Third, there is a less visible cost to youth work. Every hour youth workers spend serving meals is an hour taken from young people. The scheme needs the goodwill of the youth sector to share premises, and the sector may come to regard the canteens as an imposition. The conflict would become direct if centres extended canteen hours into the evening to reach families.

*影响。* 第一，服务覆盖会出现偏差。按现有设计推行，本计划将变成一批长者午膳中心，与现有长者服务重叠，而照顾者和家庭基本上不会接触到。第二，为在12个月内达标，中心可能在不适合的厨房烹煮食物。一旦发生涉及体弱用膳者的食物安全事故，受影响的将是整项计划的公信力，而不仅是有关中心。第三，对青少年工作造成的代价较不明显。青少年工作者每花一小时供餐，便少一小时服务青少年。本计划需要青少年界别的配合才能共用场地，而该界别可能逐渐视饭堂为额外负担。若中心为接触家庭而将饭堂时间延至晚间，矛盾便会直接浮现。

---

**PROBLEM 2 — IDENTIFYING NEED WITHOUT A PATHWAY TO SUPPORT**
The identification objective assumes a chain: someone notices a need, records it, refers it, and a service responds. In the trial, every link in the chain was weak. Staff had no agreed way to ask about or record concerns. Referrals went to elderly, rehabilitation and carer services, each with its own intake and waiting arrangements. Only one case in eleven received help within a month. Stigma also weakens the chain at the start. If the canteen is seen as a place for people who cannot afford to eat, those most reluctant to accept help will stay away.

**问题二：识别需要，却没有通往支援的途径**
识别需要的目标假设存在一条完整的链条：有人察觉需要，加以记录和转介，再由服务单位回应。在试行计划中，这条链条的每个环节都很薄弱。职员没有既定的方法去询问或记录问题。转介个案分别送往长者、康复及照顾者服务，各服务有各自的收案和轮候安排。每十一宗个案中，只有一宗在一个月内获得协助。污名化亦在起点削弱这条链条：如果饭堂被视为让负担不起膳食者前往的地方，最不愿接受协助的人便不会前来。

*Implications.* People who disclose difficulties but see no follow-up will lose trust, not only in the canteen but in welfare services generally. They will be less likely to come forward again, and may be worse off than before. Stigma would also mean that the canteens serve people who are already connected, which is contrary to their purpose. In addition, evaluation tends to rely on what is easy to count. A scheme that feeds many but helps few may be reported as a success. A later review may then find a low referral rate and question the funding, although the fault lies in the design and not in a lack of need.

*影响。* 披露困难却未见跟进的人，不但会对饭堂失去信任，对整体福利服务也会如此，日后更不愿主动求助，处境可能比以前更差。污名化亦意味着饭堂服务的是原本已有社会联系的人，与计划目的相违。此外，评估往往倚重易于统计的数据。一项让很多人用膳、却帮到很少人的计划，可能被汇报为成功。其后的检讨若发现转介率偏低，并质疑拨款是否恰当，问题其实出在设计，而非没有需求。

---

**RECOMMENDATIONS**

**建议**

*Separate cooking from serving (Problem 1).* Meals should be prepared in licensed central kitchens run by experienced NGOs or social enterprises. They should then be delivered to centres for reheating and serving. This removes the main food safety risk. It also allows centres without full kitchens to open canteens.

*烹煮与供餐分开（问题一）。* 膳食应在持牌中央厨房制备，厨房由经验丰富的非政府机构或社会企业营运，膳食制成后送往各中心加热及供应。此举可消除主要的食物安全风险，亦让没有完备厨房的中心可以开设饭堂。

*Staff canteens separately and turn the overlap into an asset (Problem 1).* Each canteen should have its own small operating team, funded through the scheme, so that no youth work hours are diverted. Young people could volunteer as helpers on a structured basis. This would add an intergenerational element and give the youth sector a stake in the scheme.

*另设人手营运饭堂，并将两者的重叠转化为优势（问题一）。* 每个饭堂应设有自己的小型营运团队，经费由本计划提供，以免占用青少年工作时间。青少年可按有系统的安排担任义工助手。此举可为计划增添跨代元素，亦让青少年界别成为持份者。

*Match sessions to target groups (Problem 1).* Weekday lunches should serve elders. A small number of centres with spare evening or weekend capacity should run family sessions for carers and children with special needs. These centres should be selected according to local need, and not spread thinly across all sites.

*按目标群体安排时段（问题一）。* 平日午膳服务长者。少数在晚间或周末有余裕的中心，应开办家庭时段，服务照顾者及有特殊需要的儿童。这些中心应按地区需要挑选，而非平均分散至所有地点。

*Build the referral pathway before opening (Problem 2).* Each canteen should have a short needs checklist, a named link social worker, and an agreed referral route into district services with a target response time. Referrals should be tracked to completion, and not merely recorded.

*开业前建立转介途径（问题二）。* 每个饭堂应备有简短的需要检视清单、指定的联络社工，以及通往地区服务的既定转介途径，并订明目标回应时间。转介个案应追踪至完成，而不仅是记录在案。

*Design against stigma (Problem 2).* Canteens should be open to all residents, with a simple sliding price scale. They should be presented as community dining and not as welfare. Mixed use protects dignity and makes attendance unremarkable.

*从设计上减少污名化（问题二）。* 饭堂应向全体居民开放，采用简单的浮动收费，并以社区共膳而非福利服务的形象推出。混合使用可保障尊严，令前来用膳变得平常。

*Measure what matters (Problems 1 and 2).* In addition to the number of meals served, indicators should include the share of diners from carer and family groups, the number of users previously unknown to services, and the number of referrals completed within the target time.

*衡量真正重要的事项（问题一及二）。* 除供应的膳食数量外，指标还应包括：来自照顾者及家庭群体的用膳者比例、以往不为服务单位所知的使用者人数，以及在目标时间内完成的转介个案数目。

---

**IMPLEMENTATION AND MONITORING**
It is suggested that the 12-month target be treated as a phased rollout. Central-kitchen supply and referral arrangements should be put in place first. Canteens would then open in batches, with family sessions piloted in a few districts. A review should be conducted at six months and should report on the indicators in paragraph 13. Further expansion should depend on the findings. The main risk is that the rollout will be slower than announced. This is preferable to scaling up a model that does not reach its intended users.

**推行及监察**
建议将12个月的目标视为分阶段推行。应先落实中央厨房供应及转介安排，然后分批开设饭堂，并在数个地区试办家庭时段。六个月后应进行检讨，并就第13段的指标作出汇报，进一步扩展则应视乎检讨结果而定。主要风险是推行进度较公布的为慢。相比把一个未能接触目标使用者的模式扩大推行，这样做较为可取。

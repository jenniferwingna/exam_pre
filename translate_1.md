> **DISCUSSION PAPER: COMMUNITY CANTEENS IN INTEGRATED YOUTH CENTRES**

**讨论文件：综合青少年中心内的社区饭堂**

> **PURPOSE**
> This paper (a) sets out two major problems facing the introduction of community canteens in integrated youth centres and explains their implications; and (b) recommends feasible measures to address them.

**目的**
本文件(a)列出在综合青少年中心推行社区饭堂所面对的两大问题，并阐述其影响；以及(b)建议切实可行的应对措施。

> **BACKGROUND**
> The canteens have three objectives: affordable nutrition, connection with the neighbourhood, and early identification of need. The third distinguishes the scheme from an ordinary meal service, and it is the one most easily lost, because meals are easy to count and identification is not. The scheme should therefore be judged by whom it reaches that services did not reach before, and by what happens to them afterwards.

**背景**
饭堂有三项目标：提供价格可负担的营养膳食、加强与社区邻里的联系，以及及早识别有需要人士。第三项目标使本计划有别于一般膳食服务，却也最容易被淡化，因为膳食数量易于统计，识别成效则难以量度。因此，评估本计划应着眼于它接触到哪些以往服务未能接触的人士，以及这些人士其后的情况。

> On that test, the trial is a warning rather than a success. It reached elders, the group already best served by existing meal services, but barely reached carers and families, and very few of the needs it noticed led to support. The two problems below explain why.

以此衡量，试行计划与其说是成功，不如说是一个警示。计划接触到的主要是长者，而长者正是现有膳食服务覆盖得最好的群体；照顾者和家庭则几乎没有接触到；试行期间发现的需要，亦极少转化为支援。以下两个问题正好说明原因。

> **PROBLEM 1 — PREMISES AND HOURS DESIGNED FOR A DIFFERENT PURPOSE**
> Youth centres are free precisely when young people are at school. That window suits elders, but not carers, who are tied to caring or work during the day, nor school-age children with special needs. The groups the scheme most wants to reach are available only when the centres are full. Physical constraints compound the mismatch: three-quarters of the selected centres lack kitchens fit for daily meal production, and the trial depended on youth workers to serve meals.

**问题一：场地及开放时间原为另一用途而设**
青少年中心恰恰在青少年上学的时段空置。这个时段适合长者，却不适合照顾者（他们日间要照顾家人或工作），也不适合有特殊需要的学童。本计划最希望接触的群体，只有在中心使用率最高的时候才有空。实际条件的限制令错配问题更为严重：获选中心中有四分之三欠缺适合每日制备膳食的厨房，而试行计划又依赖青少年工作者负责供餐。

> *Implications.* The immediate implication is skewed coverage. Scaled up as designed, the scheme would become a network of lunch clubs for elders, duplicating existing elderly services while leaving carers and families largely untouched. Second, pressure to meet the 12-month target may push centres to cook in unsuitable kitchens; a single food safety incident involving frail diners would damage confidence in the whole programme, not one centre. Third, and less visible, is the cost to youth work. Every hour youth workers spend serving meals is taken from young people, and the youth sector, whose goodwill the scheme needs in order to share premises, will come to see the canteens as an imposition. If centres then extend canteen hours into the evening to reach families, the conflict becomes direct.

*影响。* 最直接的影响是服务覆盖出现偏差。如按现有设计推展，本计划将变成一个长者午膳中心网络，既与现有长者服务重复，又几乎没有触及照顾者和家庭。其次，为在12个月内达标而承受的压力，可能促使中心在不适合的厨房烹煮食物；一旦发生涉及体弱用膳者的食物安全事故，受损的将是整项计划的公信力，而不止一间中心。第三，也是较不明显的一点，是对青少年工作造成的代价。青少年工作者每花一小时供餐，便是从青少年身上挪走一小时；而本计划需要青少年界别的善意配合才能共用场地，该界别将逐渐视饭堂为强加的负担。若中心其后把饭堂时间延至晚间以接触家庭，矛盾便会变得直接。

> **PROBLEM 2 — IDENTIFYING NEED WITHOUT A PATHWAY TO SUPPORT**
> The identification objective assumes a chain: someone notices a need, records it, refers it, and a service responds. In the trial every link was weak. Staff had no agreed way to ask about or record concerns; referrals went to elderly, rehabilitation and carer services, each with its own intake and waiting arrangements; and only one case in eleven received help within a month. Stigma weakens the chain at its start: if the canteen is seen as a place for people who cannot afford to eat, those most reluctant to accept help will stay away.

**问题二：识别需要，却没有通往支援的途径**
识别需要这项目标假设存在一条环环相扣的链条：有人察觉需要、加以记录、作出转介，然后由服务单位回应。在试行计划中，每个环节都很薄弱。职员没有既定的方法去询问或记录所关注的问题；转介个案分别送往长者、康复及照顾者服务，各服务有各自的收案及轮候安排；而每十一宗个案中，只有一宗在一个月内获得协助。污名化则在链条的起点就削弱其效能：若饭堂被视为让负担不起膳食者前往的地方，最不愿接受协助的人便会却步。

> *Implications.* People who disclose difficulties and see nothing follow will lose trust not only in the canteen but in welfare services generally, and will be less likely to come forward again; the scheme could leave them worse off than before. Stigma means the canteens will serve the already connected, the opposite of their purpose. And because evaluation defaults to what is easy to count, a scheme that feeds many but helps few may be reported as a success, until a later review finds a low referral rate and questions the funding, when the fault lies in design rather than in need.

*影响。* 向职员透露困难、却不见任何跟进的人，不仅会对饭堂失去信任，对整体福利服务亦会如此，日后更不愿再主动求助；本计划反而可能令他们的处境比以前更差。污名化意味着饭堂最终服务的是原本已有社会联系的人，与计划目的背道而驰。此外，由于评估往往倾向采用易于统计的指标，一项让很多人吃饭、却帮到很少人的计划，可能被汇报为成功；直至日后检讨发现转介率偏低，并质疑拨款是否恰当，而问题其实出在设计，而非需求不存在。

> **RECOMMENDATIONS**
> *Separate cooking from serving (Problem 1).* Meals should be prepared in licensed central kitchens run by experienced NGOs or social enterprises and delivered to centres for reheating and serving. This removes the main food safety risk and allows canteens to open in centres without full kitchens.

**建议**
*将烹煮与供餐分开（针对问题一）。* 膳食应由持牌中央厨房制备，该等厨房由经验丰富的非政府机构或社会企业营运，膳食制成后送往各中心加热及供应。此举可消除主要的食物安全风险，亦让没有完备厨房的中心也能开设饭堂。

> *Staff canteens separately and turn the overlap into an asset (Problem 1).* Each canteen should have its own small operating team, funded through the scheme, so that no youth work hours are diverted. Young people could volunteer as helpers on a structured basis, giving the scheme an intergenerational dimension and the youth sector a stake in it.

*另设人手营运饭堂，并把两者的重叠化为优势（针对问题一）。* 每个饭堂应设有自己的小型营运团队，经费由本计划提供，使青少年工作时间不致被挪用。青少年可按有系统的安排担任义工助手，既为计划增添跨代元素，亦让青少年界别成为持份者。

> *Match sessions to target groups (Problem 1).* Weekday lunches should serve elders. A small number of centres with spare evening or weekend capacity should run family sessions for carers and children with special needs, selected by local need rather than spread thinly across all sites.

*按目标群体安排时段（针对问题一）。* 平日午膳服务长者。少数在晚间或周末尚有余裕的中心，可开办家庭时段，服务照顾者及有特殊需要的儿童；这些中心应按地区需要挑选，而非平均分散至所有地点。

> *Build the referral pathway before opening (Problem 2).* Each canteen should have a short needs checklist, a named link social worker and an agreed referral route into district services with a target response time. Referrals should be tracked to completion, not merely recorded.

*开业前建立转介途径（针对问题二）。* 每个饭堂应备有简短的需要检视清单、指定的联络社工，以及通往地区服务的既定转介途径，并订明目标回应时间。转介个案应追踪至完成，而非仅作记录。

> *Design against stigma (Problem 2).* Canteens should be open to all residents on a simple sliding price scale and presented as community dining rather than welfare. Mixed use protects dignity and makes attendance unremarkable.

*从设计上防止污名化（针对问题二）。* 饭堂应向全体居民开放，采用简单的浮动收费制，并以社区共膳而非福利服务的形象示人。混合使用可保障尊严，令前来用膳变得平常不过。

> *Measure what matters (Problems 1 and 2).* Alongside meals served, indicators should include the share of diners from carer and family groups, the number of users previously unknown to services, and referrals completed within the target time.

*衡量真正重要的事项（针对问题一及二）。* 除供应膳食数量外，指标还应包括：来自照顾者及家庭群体的用膳者比例、先前未为服务单位所知的使用者人数，以及在目标时间内完成的转介个案。

> **IMPLEMENTATION AND MONITORING**
> It is suggested that the 12-month target be treated as a phased rollout: central-kitchen supply and referral arrangements first, then openings in tranches, with family sessions piloted in a few districts. A six-month review should report on the indicators in paragraph 13, and further expansion should depend on them. The main risk is a slower rollout than announced; this is preferable to scaling up a model that misses its intended users.

**推行及监察**
建议把12个月的目标视为分阶段推行：先落实中央厨房供应及转介安排，再分批开设饭堂，并在数个地区试办家庭时段。六个月后应进行检讨，就第13段所载的指标作出汇报，而进一步扩展应视乎这些指标而定。主要风险是推行进度较公布的为慢；相比把一个未能接触目标使用者的模式扩大推行，这样做较为可取。

勞工、就業與人力

Labour, Employment and Manpower
← 議題庫
指標措施方向取態比較出題目的矛盾現況問題影響論點與反駁方案挑戰游說句式詞彙

一句話方向

兩條線同時走：一方面以「本地優先」收緊輸入勞工（分級審批、第二級提高人手比例和延長本地招聘期）並嚴打黑工；另一方面以新法例把數碼平台外送員納入法定工傷補償、推進強積金「全自由行」，並以技能提升局、再就業津貼和青年職位計劃釋放本地勞動力。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府應立法保障數碼平台外送員的權益。試分析外送員的現況，就立法範圍提出建議，分析執行上的挑戰及處理方法，並游說平台公司支持。

出處：五年規劃第五篇第二十三章「健全勞工保障」（FYP 5.51–5.54）；施政報告第七章第九節（§382–389）、§276–279（技術人才與技能提升局）、§286（三萬青年職位）、§363（人力推算）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Two tracks at once: tightening imported labour under “local first” (tiered vetting, stricter manning ratios and longer local recruitment in Tier 2) and a crackdown on illegal work; and, on the other side, new legislation giving delivery platform workers statutory injury compensation, MPF “Full Portability”, and releasing local labour through skills upgrading, re-employment allowances and youth jobs.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say the Government should legislate to protect the rights of digital platform delivery workers. Analyse their situation, propose the scope of legislation, analyse the implementation challenges and responses, and persuade platform companies to support it.

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

輸入勞工「一套標準」審批→按行業空缺情況分級審批

政府不再只問「有沒有短缺」，而是按工種的本地應徵情況決定審批嚴格程度；並會動態把更多工種納入第二級。

One set of rules for all imported labour→Tiered vetting by sector

The question is no longer only whether there is a shortage, but how strict to be by job category given local applications, with more categories added dynamically.

平台工人處於「自僱」灰色地帶→以專門法例先提供工傷補償

不改變「僱員」定義，而是就最迫切的風險立法——這是先易後難、避免陷入身份爭議的做法。

Platform workers in a self-employment grey zone→A dedicated law for injury compensation first

Rather than redefine “employee”, legislate for the most urgent risk: an easy-first approach that avoids the status debate.

最低工資兩年一檢→一年一檢，追上通脹

五年規劃把「工資增幅追上通脹、經濟好時分享成果」寫成原則。

Minimum wage reviewed every two years→Annual review, keeping pace with inflation

The Plan writes in the principle that low pay should keep up with inflation and share in growth.

失業後再培訓→在職技能提升與「技能為本」

僱員再培訓局升格為技能提升局，重點由協助失業者轉向提升整體勞動人口的技能，並回應AI帶來的轉型。

Retraining after job loss→In-work upskilling on a skills-based framework

The retraining board becomes a skills upgrading body, shifting from helping the unemployed to raising the skills of the whole workforce and responding to AI.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

「本地優先、有限度輸入、保障底線」：政府不會開放勞工市場，也沒有引入全面集體談判或標準工時的方向；取態是以行政審批控制輸入、以個別立法補漏（外送員工傷、強積金追討）、以培訓和津貼釋放本地勞動力（婦女、長者、青年）。

Government stance

“Local first, limited importation, a floor of protection”: no opening of the labour market and no move towards collective bargaining or standard working hours. The approach is administrative control of imports, targeted legislation to plug gaps (delivery injuries, MPF recovery) and training and subsidies to release local labour (women, older people, youth).

收緊輸入勞工：保障本地工人，還是窒礙行業？

支持的理據

餐飲業樓面和廚房仍有本地人應徵，收緊可防僱主以外勞壓低工資；實體招聘會和六星期招聘期可以驗證「真實短缺」。

質疑的理據

中小企招聘成本和時間增加；勞動人口持續下降，部分商戶可能縮減營業或結業；以本地中位工資作下限，未必反映個別行業的真實情況。

Tighter imports: protecting local workers or strangling business?

Case for

Local people still apply for F&B kitchen and floor jobs; tighter rules stop employers using imports to depress wages, and job fairs plus six weeks of recruitment test whether shortages are genuine.

Case against

SMEs face higher hiring costs and delays; with a shrinking workforce some may cut hours or close; the local median wage floor may not reflect conditions in particular trades.

外送員立法：只補工傷夠不夠？

支持的理據

工傷是外送員最迫切、最有共識的風險；專門法例避開「僱員定義」的長期爭論，可較快落實。

質疑的理據

平台工人還有收入不穩、演算法派單、帳戶被停用而無申訴渠道等問題；只補工傷可能令其他保障被擱置，平台亦可能把保費轉嫁工人或消費者。

Legislating for delivery workers: is injury cover enough?

Case for

Injury is the most urgent and least contested risk; a dedicated law avoids the long dispute over employee status and can be delivered faster.

Case against

Platform workers also face volatile income, algorithmic dispatch and account deactivation without appeal; covering injury alone may shelve other protections, and platforms may pass premiums to workers or consumers.

三萬青年職位：公帑補貼薪酬是否恰當？

支持的理據

缺乏經驗的青年最難入職；六個月薪酬補貼降低僱主試用成本，幫青年建立履歷。

質疑的理據

可能出現「替代效應」——僱主以受補貼青年取代原有職位；補貼期滿後留任率未知；公帑成效需以留任數據證明。

30 000 youth jobs: should public money subsidise wages?

Case for

Inexperienced young people find it hardest to get a first job; a six-month subsidy lowers employers’ trial cost and builds a CV.

Case against

Employers may substitute subsidised youth for existing posts; retention after the subsidy ends is unknown; value for money must be shown by retention data.

考生取態建議與紅線

「本地優先」是不可挑戰的前提；可以發揮的是「如何更準確認定短缺」（數據平台、以職位空缺率觸發分級）和「如何把保障延伸到新型就業」。紅線：不要主張全面開放輸入勞工或取消本地招聘要求；也不要主張立即立法標準工時或集體談判（政府沒有這個方向）——寫「研究」「分階段」「先行業指引」較穩妥。

Recommended stance and red lines

“Local first” is the non-negotiable premise; your room is in how to identify shortages more accurately (the data platform, vacancy rates triggering tiers) and how to extend protection to new forms of work. Red lines: do not argue for fully opening imports or dropping local recruitment, and do not propose immediate legislation on standard hours or collective bargaining — “study”, “phase in” and “start with sector guidelines” are safer.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

外勞以「配額（依賴比率上限）＋外勞稅」管理，不同行業上限不同；專業准證以COMPASS積分審批；《平台工人法》（2025年1月生效）要求平台為外送員和司機提供工傷補償，並逐步加入公積金供款，但保留其自僱身份。

以價格（外勞稅）而非逐宗審批控制輸入；平台工人獲法定保障而身份不變。

✓ 可借鑑

《平台工人法》證明可以不改變僱傭身份而提供工傷保障，與§385方向一致；外勞稅令輸入勞工成本高於本地工人，自動體現本地優先。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡外勞佔勞動力比例遠高於香港，外勞稅是其財政及技能資助的一部分；香港引入外勞稅要立法並面對商界反對，亦會改變現行以本地中位工資為下限的制度邏輯。

Singapore

Foreign labour is managed by quotas (dependency ratio ceilings) and levies that vary by sector; professional passes are scored under COMPASS; the Platform Workers Act (January 2025) requires platforms to provide injury compensation and phased CPF contributions for delivery riders and drivers while keeping them self-employed.

Imports are controlled by price (the levy) rather than case-by-case approval; platform workers get statutory protection without a change of status.

✓ Transferable

The Platform Workers Act shows injury protection without changing employment status, matching §385; a levy that makes imported labour dearer than local labour builds “local first” in automatically.

✗ Not transferable

Foreign workers are a far larger share of Singapore’s workforce and the levy funds skills programmes; in Hong Kong a levy would need legislation, face business opposition and change the logic of the median-wage floor.

韓國

以「僱傭許可制」（2004年）由政府與來源國政府簽約引入外勞，按行業設年度配額；外送員等特殊類型工作者逐步納入工傷保險，並取消了「只為單一平台工作」的專屬性要求。

政府間協議減少中介剝削；但外勞轉換僱主受限，被批評為不自由。

✓ 可借鑑

政府間協議管理來源國和中介收費——香港外傭與補充勞工的中介收費問題可參考；取消「專屬性」要求亦說明工傷保障須照顧同時為多個平台接單的外送員。

✗ 不可照搬

僱傭許可制屬大規模、長期輸入，配額由中央每年釐定；香港的政策定位是「有限度、補充性」，規模和政治前提不同。

South Korea

The Employment Permit System (2004) admits foreign workers through government-to-government agreements with annual sector quotas; delivery riders and other special-type workers have been brought into industrial accident insurance, and the exclusivity requirement has been dropped.

G2G agreements reduce agency exploitation, but limits on changing employer are criticised as unfree.

✓ Transferable

G2G agreements to control agencies and fees are relevant to Hong Kong’s FDH and imported-labour agency problems; dropping exclusivity shows injury cover must work for riders on several platforms at once.

✗ Not transferable

The EPS is large-scale and long-term with annual central quotas; Hong Kong’s policy is limited and supplementary, a different scale and political premise.

內地

2022年起在七個省市試行「新就業形態就業人員職業傷害保障」，由平台按單繳費，不以勞動關係為前提；其後擴大試點範圍。

以「按單計費」解決騎手同時在多個平台接單的問題，保障覆蓋迅速擴大。

✓ 可借鑑

保費按每單計算並由平台繳付，最適合多平台接單的外送員——香港新法例可參考「每單保費」的設計，避免責任在平台之間推卸。

✗ 不可照搬

內地以社會保險體系承保；香港沒有社會保險制度，工傷補償靠僱主向私營保險公司投保，須由保險業設計相應產品並設監管。

Mainland China

Since 2022, seven provinces and cities have piloted occupational injury protection for new forms of employment, with platforms paying per order and no requirement for an employment relationship; the pilot has since been widened.

Per-order premiums solve the problem of riders working for several platforms, and coverage expanded quickly.

✓ Transferable

Per-order premiums paid by the platform suit multi-platform riders; Hong Kong’s law could borrow this to stop platforms passing responsibility between them.

✗ Not transferable

The Mainland uses its social insurance system; Hong Kong has none, relying on employers’ private insurance, so the insurance industry must design products under regulation.

日本

2019年「工作方式改革」首次以法律設加班上限（原則上每月45小時、每年360小時）；2024年《自由工作者保護法》要求發包方以書面列明條件並準時付款。

長工時略有改善；對自由工作者的保障從「交易公平」而非僱傭權利入手。

✓ 可借鑑

為新型就業先訂「交易規則」（書面條件、準時付款、停用帳戶須給理由並可申訴），可補足§385只處理工傷的空白。

✗ 不可照搬

日本加班上限建基於企業工會和勞資協定傳統；香港沒有集體談判制度，直接立法規管工時爭議極大，不能照搬。

Japan

The 2019 work-style reform set a statutory overtime cap for the first time (in principle 45 hours a month, 360 a year); the 2024 freelance protection act requires written terms and timely payment from those commissioning work.

Long hours eased slightly; protection for freelancers starts from fair dealing rather than employment rights.

✓ Transferable

Fair-dealing rules for new work — written terms, timely payment, reasons and appeal for deactivation — would fill the gap left by §385’s focus on injury.

✗ Not transferable

Japan’s overtime cap rests on enterprise unions and labour-management agreements; Hong Kong has no collective bargaining, so legislating hours directly would be highly contentious.

政策目的

Policy objective

在勞動人口下降的結構下維持經濟產能，同時保障本地工人的工資與職業發展，並讓工作與家庭責任可以並存。

To maintain productive capacity as the workforce shrinks, while protecting local workers' wages and career development, and making it possible to hold a job alongside family responsibilities.

核心矛盾

Core tension

僱主說請不到人，工會說僱主不願加薪；兩者都可能同時成立。核心難題是：如何客觀認定「真實短缺」，而不是把不願調整待遇的成本轉嫁給輸入勞工政策。

Employers say they cannot recruit; unions say employers will not pay more. Both can be true at once. The core difficulty is how to determine a genuine shortage objectively, rather than allowing an unwillingness to adjust terms to be exported into labour-importation policy.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

受人口高齡化影響，勞動人口未來數年逐步遞減；勞福局即將公布的人力推算中期更新顯示創科、航空、醫療及建造業人手依然緊絀，並首次分析AI對勞動市場的影響。本屆政府已完成取消強積金「對沖」、最低工資改為一年一檢、連續性合約由「418」改為「468」。平台經濟擴張令外送員保障成為焦點。

Current position

Ageing means the workforce will shrink over the next few years; the Labour and Welfare Bureau’s mid-term manpower projection update shows continued shortages in I&T, aviation, healthcare and construction, and analyses AI’s effect on the labour market for the first time. This term has abolished the MPF offsetting arrangement, moved the statutory minimum wage to annual review and changed the continuous-contract threshold from “418” to “468”. The growth of platform work has made delivery workers’ protection a focus.

長期結構

勞動人口因人口老化而收縮，部分行業（護理、建造、運輸、餐飲）短缺尤其明顯；女性與長者的勞動參與率仍有提升空間，但受託兒、照顧責任與工作安排僵化所限；彈性工作安排與家庭友善僱傭措施近年成為政策焦點。

Long-run structure

The workforce is contracting as the population ages, with shortages most visible in care, construction, transport and catering. Participation among women and older people still has room to rise but is held back by child care, caring duties and inflexible working arrangements. Flexible working and family-friendly employment measures have become a policy focus in recent years.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1短缺認定缺乏可信機制

若沒有客觀、公開、可覆核的短缺認定標準，每一個輸入配額都會變成政治爭議，計劃本身也失去正當性。

1There is no credible mechanism for determining shortage

Without an objective, published and reviewable test of shortage, every quota becomes a political dispute and the scheme itself loses legitimacy.

2輸入勞工削弱行業自我培訓的誘因

若可以較低成本從外補充人手，僱主投資本地培訓的動機下降，長遠造成技能基礎萎縮與路徑依賴。

2Imported labour weakens the incentive to train

If manpower can be topped up from outside at lower cost, employers' incentive to invest in local training falls, eroding the skills base over time and creating path dependence.

3未被使用的本地勞動力仍然龐大

婦女、長者、殘疾人士的勞動參與率偏低，障礙主要不是意願，而是託兒、照顧責任、工時僵化與年齡歧視。

3A large pool of local labour remains untapped

Participation among women, older people and people with disabilities is low, and the barriers are not chiefly willingness but child care, caring duties, rigid hours and age discrimination.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1以輸入代替結構改革，短期紓緩、長期依賴，最終同時失去人手與技能。

1Substituting importation for structural reform relieves the immediate pressure but builds dependence, and eventually costs both the manpower and the skills.

2若輸入勞工的居住與待遇出現問題，會迅速升級為國際聲譽事件，代價遠超政策本身。

2If the accommodation or treatment of imported workers goes wrong, the issue escalates quickly into a reputational one, at a cost far beyond the policy itself.

3彈性工作若只由個別僱主自願推行，最需要的低薪與輪班工種反而最不可能受惠，形成新的不平等。

3If flexible working is left to voluntary employer action, the low-paid and shift-based jobs that need it most are least likely to get it, creating a new inequality.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

勞動人口下降是結構性的，本地培訓補不上時序缺口。

站反方？這樣駁

時序缺口是政策自己造成的——過去二十年沒有及時擴充培訓、沒有提高參與率。以缺口為由持續輸入，會令同一個缺口永遠存在，因為沒有人有動機去填它。

FOR 1

The contraction of the workforce is structural and local training cannot close the timing gap.

Counter

The timing gap is of policy's own making: training was not expanded and participation was not raised over two decades. Importing because of the gap keeps the gap permanent, since nobody then has an incentive to close it.

支持 2

彈性工作安排能直接釋放婦女與照顧者的勞動力，成本低於任何補貼。

站反方？這樣駁

彈性工作只對可以彈性的職位有效。低薪、輪班、前線工種佔本地勞動力相當比例，而短缺最嚴重的正是這些行業——彈性安排在這裡幾乎無法實施，等於幫不到最需要的人。

FOR 2

Flexible working directly releases the labour of women and carers at a cost below that of any subsidy.

Counter

Flexible working helps only where the job can flex. Low-paid, shift-based and front-line work makes up a large share of the workforce and is where shortages bite hardest — precisely where flexibility is hardest to deliver.

支持 3

有序輸入配合嚴格條件，比默許非法勞工更能保障本地工人。

站反方？這樣駁

這是把兩個問題混為一談。非法勞工應該靠執法處理，而不是靠合法化來處理；以「反正已經存在」為由開放，會削弱執法本身的正當性。

FOR 3

Orderly admission on strict conditions protects local workers better than tolerating irregular employment.

Counter

This conflates two problems. Irregular employment is a matter for enforcement, not for legalisation; opening the door because the practice already exists undermines the legitimacy of enforcement itself.

反對／制約 1

輸入勞工可能壓低工資水平，特別是議價能力最弱的工種。

站支持方？這樣駁

壓低工資是設計的結果，不是輸入的必然。把輸入工資設在本地中位數或以上、要求可審計的本地優先招聘紀錄，價格誘因就消失了。反過來，長期短缺同樣會壓低本地工人的處境——因為行業會萎縮。

AGAINST 1

Imported labour can depress wages, particularly in the occupations with the least bargaining power.

Counter

Wage depression is a design outcome, not an inevitability. Setting imported wages at or above the local median and requiring auditable local-first recruitment removes the price incentive. A prolonged shortage depresses local workers too, because the sector contracts.

反對／制約 2

彈性工作在部分行業（輪班、前線、生產）技術上難以實施。

站支持方？這樣駁

難不等於不可。輪班行業可以用自選更表、換更平台、部分時間制與跨店調配達到同樣效果。把「難」直接等同「不做」，正是這些行業長期請不到人的原因之一。

AGAINST 2

Flexible working is technically difficult in shift-based, front-line and production settings.

Counter

Difficult is not impossible. Shift sectors can achieve the same effect through self-rostering, shift-swap platforms, part-time posts and cross-site deployment. Treating difficulty as a reason not to try is itself part of why these sectors cannot recruit.

反對／制約 3

強制規定會增加中小企的合規成本，可能反而抑制聘用。

站支持方？這樣駁

把法定要求限於程序——須考慮並書面回覆——而非結果，合規成本極低。而現時的自願模式已經證明不足：有能力的大企業做了，最需要的地方沒有做。

AGAINST 3

Mandating arrangements raises compliance costs for smaller employers and may suppress hiring.

Counter

Confine the statutory duty to process — to consider and reply in writing — rather than outcome, and compliance costs little. The voluntary model has already shown its limits: large employers who can afford it act, and the places that need it most do not.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊與示範

公布家庭友善僱傭措施的良好做法與成效數據，以約章與表揚制度帶動自願採用。

Information and demonstration

Publish good practice and outcome data on family-friendly employment, and drive voluntary uptake through a charter and recognition scheme.

誘因

對實施彈性安排或提供託兒支援的僱主給予稅務或資助誘因，並優先適用於中小企。

Incentives

Offer tax or funding incentives to employers who introduce flexible arrangements or child-care support, targeted first at smaller firms.

配套服務

大幅擴充資助託兒與課餘照顧，尤其零至三歲及非辦公時間時段——這是釋放婦女勞動力最直接的槓桿。

Enabling services

Expand subsidised child care and after-school care substantially, especially for the under-threes and outside office hours: this is the most direct lever on female participation.

規管底線

以立法訂定最低標準（例如僱員可要求彈性安排並獲書面回覆的權利），而非規定一刀切的安排。

A regulatory floor

Legislate a minimum standard — for example a right to request flexible arrangements and receive a reasoned written reply — rather than prescribing a uniform arrangement.

輸入制度設計

以空缺率、工資增長與招聘期等客觀數據認定短缺，交由獨立三方委員會覆核；訂明本地優先招聘期與可審計紀錄；輸入工資不低於本地中位數以消除價格誘因；配額與培訓投入掛鈎；設期限與定期檢討。

Design of the admission scheme

Determine shortage from objective data — vacancy rates, wage growth, time-to-fill — reviewed by an independent tripartite committee; require an auditable local-first recruitment period; set imported wages no lower than the local median to remove the price incentive; tie quotas to training investment; and impose a sunset with periodic review.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

工會對任何輸入安排都會強烈反對。

化解

把保障本地工人的條款寫在前面而非附註：優先招聘期、工資下限、可審計紀錄、三方覆核、期限。

Challenge 1

Unions will oppose any admission arrangement strongly.

Mitigation

Put the protections for local workers in the body of the scheme, not in a footnote: a local-first period, a wage floor, auditable records, tripartite review and a sunset.

挑戰 2

中小企未必有能力實施彈性安排。

化解

提供現成的人手排班工具與過渡資助，並把法定要求限於程序（須考慮並書面回覆），而非結果。

Challenge 2

Smaller employers may lack the capacity to implement flexible arrangements.

Mitigation

Provide off-the-shelf rostering tools and transitional funding, and confine any statutory duty to process — to consider and reply in writing — rather than to outcome.

挑戰 3

託兒服務本身同樣面對人手短缺。

化解

以社區保姆、學校共用空間、與長者中心跨代共址等較輕的模式先擴容。

Challenge 3

Child-care services face manpower shortages of their own.

Mitigation

Expand first through lighter models: community child-minders, shared school premises and intergenerational co-location with elderly centres.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

工會

先講本地優先與工資下限，再講配額；提供可查核的執行機制，而非承諾。

Unions

Lead with local-first recruitment and the wage floor before any mention of quotas, and offer a verifiable enforcement mechanism rather than an assurance.

商會與僱主

以確定性換條件：清晰規則、可預期的處理時間，換取培訓投入與合規紀錄。

Business and employers

Trade certainty for conditions: clear rules and predictable processing in exchange for training investment and compliance records.

婦女與照顧者團體

託兒供應與時段是實質議題，彈性安排若無託兒配套等於空談。

Women's and carers' groups

Child-care supply and hours are the substantive issue; flexible working without child care is an empty promise.

立法會

以三方委員會與定期檢討回應問責關注，並承諾公開行業別數據。

The legislature

Answer accountability concerns with a tripartite committee and periodic review, and commit to publishing sector-level data.

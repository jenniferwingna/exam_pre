人口老化與安老服務

Ageing Population and Elderly Care

一句話方向

方向不是單純「多建院舍」，而是三件事同時做：把資助照顧的重心移向「錢跟人走」的長者券；把部分院舍供應交給業界自資營運和大灣區；以試行輸入外籍家居照顧員補足居家照顧的人手缺口。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府試行輸入外籍家居照顧員，是應對居家安老人手短缺的有效方法。試分析現時居家照顧的人手狀況，提出優化此試行計劃的措施，分析你的建議在執行上的挑戰及處理方法，並嘗試游說勞工界支持。

出處：五年規劃第五篇第二十四章「共建豐盛樂齡社會」（FYP 5.55–5.61、專欄37）；施政報告第七章第十節（§389–400）；《應對社會高齡化對策報告》（2026年9月18日）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

The direction is not simply “build more homes”. Three things move together: shifting subsidised care towards vouchers where the money follows the user; handing part of residential supply to self-financed operators and to the Greater Bay Area; and piloting imported foreign domestic carers to fill the home-care manpower gap.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say the pilot to admit foreign domestic carers is an effective answer to the shortage of home-care manpower. Analyse the current manpower situation, propose improvements to the pilot, analyse the implementation challenges of your proposals and how to handle them, and try to persuade labour groups to support it.

對策報告

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

服務為本：政府撥款給機構，按名額提供服務→錢跟人走：逐步提高長者券的比重

長者券讓長者自選服務提供者，營辦者要爭取使用者。好處是選擇與競爭，風險是資訊不對稱和「揀客」。題目若問資助模式，這是主軸。

Funding places run by NGOs→Money follows the user: a growing share of vouchers

Vouchers let elders choose a provider, so operators compete for users. The gain is choice and competition; the risks are information asymmetry and cream-skimming. Any question on funding models turns on this shift.

政府興建、津助機構營運→政府興建、業界自資營運（試行）

政府出處所、營運者自負盈虧，等於把「供應」與「資助」分開：硬件由政府提供，資助則經院舍券跟人走。

Government builds, subvented NGO operates→Government builds, operator self-finances (pilot)

The Government supplies premises and the operator carries the business risk. Supply and subsidy are separated: hardware from the state, subsidy through vouchers that follow the resident.

本地院舍是唯一選項→大灣區成為照顧與醫療的延伸腹地

由可攜現金援助、資助購買內地醫保到醫療券延伸，政策開始把跨境養老當作正式選項而非例外。

Local homes as the only option→The GBA as an extended hinterland for care and treatment

Portable cash assistance, subsidised Mainland medical insurance and extended vouchers together treat cross-boundary retirement as a mainstream option rather than an exception.

照顧人手靠本地招聘及外傭兼任→輸入具護老技能的外籍家居照顧員

外傭制度原本是「家務助理」；首次把「照顧技能」寫入入境安排，等於承認居家照顧需要一個專業化的人手層級。

Local hiring plus FDHs doubling as carers→Imported foreign domestic carers with recognised care skills

The FDH regime was designed for domestic help. Writing care skills into an admission scheme for the first time concedes that home care needs a professional tier of workers.

被動等候長者求助→以數據主動識別高風險長者

社福數據平台把醫療、房屋、社福數據串連，由「未求助即無服務」轉為「未出事先介入」；代價是私隱和數據管治的問題。

Waiting for elders to ask→Using data to find high-risk elders early

Linking health, housing and welfare data moves the system from “no request, no service” to intervening before a crisis, at the price of privacy and data-governance questions.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府堅持「居家安老為本，院舍照顧為後援」，以「精準」取代「普及」：資源集中在體弱、獨居及雙老家庭；以長者券引入競爭；以市場（自資營運、私人發展項目內的院址）和大灣區擴大供應；以輸入外籍照顧員補人手。財政上要求財庫局「密切留意」高齡化對財政的影響——即不會無上限擴張經常開支，亦沒有全民退休保障式的普及津貼。

Government stance

The Government holds to “ageing in place as the core, with institutional care as back-up” and prefers targeting to universality: resources go to frail elders, those living alone and elderly couples; vouchers introduce competition; the market (self-financed operation, premises in private developments) and the GBA expand supply; imported carers fill manpower gaps. The Treasury is told to watch the fiscal impact of ageing, which signals no open-ended growth in recurrent spending and no universal pension-style benefit.

輸入外籍家居照顧員：解決人手，還是製造新問題？

支持的理據

居家照顧需要全日支援，本地護理員不足而且多不願留宿；外籍照顧員能讓體弱長者延遲入住院舍，而延遲入院本身就節省公帑。以外傭制度為框架，行政成本低、市場熟悉。

質疑的理據

一、壓低本地護理員的議價能力，勞工界必然反對；二、「較高護老技能」如何認證？若只是在來源地短期培訓，質素與外傭無異卻收取較高工資；三、僱主是家庭，體弱長者的家屬未必有能力管理照顧員，出事時責任誰負？

Importing foreign domestic carers: filling the gap or creating new problems?

Case for

Home care is a round-the-clock need; local care workers are scarce and few will live in. Foreign carers can delay admission to homes, which itself saves public money. Building on the FDH framework keeps administrative cost low and the market familiar.

Case against

First, it weakens local care workers’ bargaining power and labour groups will oppose it. Second, how is “more advanced training” certified? A short course abroad may leave quality no better than an FDH at a higher wage. Third, the employer is a family that may not be able to manage a carer, and liability when things go wrong is unclear.

提高長者券比重：是賦權，還是把責任推給家庭？

支持的理據

長者券讓長者和家人自行選擇，營辦者要爭取使用者，服務質素和效率會因競爭提升；同一筆公帑可買到更多服務。

質疑的理據

選擇權的前提是有能力選擇。體弱、認知障礙或缺乏家人支援的長者最難比較服務——最需要個案管理的人，偏偏最難用好長者券；營辦者亦可能偏好較易照顧的個案。

A larger voucher share: empowerment, or shifting responsibility to families?

Case for

Vouchers let elders and families choose; operators must win users, so quality and efficiency improve, and the same public money buys more service.

Case against

Choice presumes capacity to choose. Frail elders, those with dementia or without family support find it hardest to compare providers: those who most need case management are least able to use vouchers, and operators may prefer easier cases.

跨境養老：擴大選擇，還是把長者「送走」？

支持的理據

大灣區內地城市的土地、人手和成本較低，長者可在熟悉的語言文化環境入住院舍，又能保留香港福利；對輪候中的長者是實在的選擇。

質疑的理據

跨境養老適合有親友在內地、身體尚可的長者；對體弱長者而言，就醫、探訪、糾紛處理和監管都跨越兩套制度。若以此紓緩本地輪候，等於把供應責任轉移出境。

Cross-boundary retirement: more choice, or sending elders away?

Case for

Land, staff and costs are lower in Mainland GBA cities; elders can live in a familiar language and culture and keep Hong Kong benefits. For those on the waiting list it is a real option.

Case against

It suits elders with relatives across the boundary and reasonable health. For frail elders, treatment, visits, disputes and regulation all straddle two systems. Using it to ease local queues exports the duty of supply.

考生取態建議與紅線

最穩妥的取態是「支持方向，改良執行」：肯定居家安老和長者券的方向，把分數花在執行細節——長者券要配個案經理和服務比較平台；外籍照顧員要有技能認證、僱主支援和投訴渠道；跨境養老要有監管互認和回流安排。紅線：不要把跨境養老寫成「把長者送回內地」，也不要主張全民退休保障或無上限增加經常開支——兩者都與政府既定取態及量入為出原則衝突，閱卷者會視為缺乏財政意識。

Recommended stance and red lines

The safest stance is “support the direction, improve the delivery”: endorse ageing in place and vouchers, then spend your marks on implementation — vouchers need case managers and a comparison platform; foreign carers need skills certification, employer support and a complaints channel; cross-boundary care needs regulatory recognition and a route home. Red lines: do not describe cross-boundary care as “sending elders back to the Mainland”, and do not argue for universal retirement protection or open-ended recurrent spending; both run against the Government’s stated position and fiscal prudence, and markers will read them as a lack of fiscal awareness.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

以「社區為先」：2020年推出強制性長期護理保險 CareShield Life（1980年或以後出生者30歲起納入，嚴重殘疾時終身按月發放）；家庭聘用外籍家傭照顧長者可獲外勞稅寬減，照顧者培訓亦有資助；以「活躍樂齡中心」網絡承擔預防與轉介。

長期護理的財務風險由個人保險分擔，而非全由稅收承擔；受訓的外籍家傭是居家照顧的主力。

✓ 可借鑑

以「外勞稅寬減＋照顧培訓資助」鼓勵家庭聘用受訓的外籍照顧者，與§395方向一致；香港可參考其培訓課程和認證方式，並把培訓資助與聘用掛鈎。

✗ 不可照搬

CareShield Life 建基於中央公積金的強制儲蓄和扣款基建。香港強積金是退休儲蓄，不能提取作護理費；另設強制護理保險等於新增供款，是重大政治決定，不能以「借鑑」一筆帶過。

Singapore

Community first. CareShield Life (2020) is a compulsory long-term care insurance for those born in 1980 or later, joined from age 30 and paying monthly for life on severe disability. Families hiring a migrant domestic worker to care for an elder pay a reduced levy, and caregiver training is subsidised. Active Ageing Centres handle prevention and referral.

The financial risk of long-term care is shared through individual insurance rather than carried by taxes alone, and trained foreign domestic workers are the backbone of home care.

✓ Transferable

Levy concessions plus caregiver-training grants to encourage families to hire trained foreign carers match the direction of §395; Hong Kong can borrow the curriculum and certification and tie training subsidies to employment.

✗ Not transferable

CareShield Life rests on the CPF’s compulsory savings and deduction infrastructure. The MPF is a retirement fund that cannot be drawn for care; a compulsory care insurance would be a new levy and a major political decision, not something to borrow in passing.

日本

2000年起實施「介護保險」：40歲以上強制供款，保費與稅款各佔一半，使用者自付一至三成；由照顧經理（care manager）按統一評估制訂照顧計劃；以日常生活圈為單位推動「地域包括照顧體系」。

服務普及、評估標準化；但平均保費自2000年以來上升逾一倍，護理人手缺口仍以數十萬計，已開放「特定技能」簽證輸入外籍護理員。

✓ 可借鑑

照顧經理和統一評估：香港長者券要避免「有券不懂用」，需要一個代表長者的個案經理；日本亦證明輸入外籍護理員必須配合語言和技能考核。

✗ 不可照搬

社會保險模式要求長期供款，並把保費與服務權利掛鈎；香港低稅制下沒有社會保險傳統。日本保費持續上升亦說明，財政壓力只是轉移，沒有消失。

Japan

Long-term care insurance since 2000: compulsory contributions from age 40, funded half by premiums and half by taxes, users paying 10–30 per cent; care managers draw up care plans from a single assessment; care is organised around daily-life areas under the Community-based Integrated Care System.

Coverage is universal and assessment standardised, but average premiums have more than doubled since 2000, the care workforce gap runs to hundreds of thousands, and foreign care workers are admitted under the Specified Skilled Worker visa.

✓ Transferable

Care managers and a single assessment: if Hong Kong vouchers are not to go unused, elders need a case manager acting for them. Japan also shows that importing care workers needs language and skills tests.

✗ Not transferable

Social insurance requires lifelong contributions tied to entitlement; Hong Kong’s low-tax system has no such tradition. Japan’s rising premiums show the fiscal pressure is moved, not removed.

台灣地區

「長照2.0」（2017年）以稅收而非保險融資（包括菸稅、遺產及贈與稅、房地合一稅的部分收入），建立A（社區整合型服務中心）、B（複合型服務中心）、C（巷弄長照站）三級社區網絡；家庭亦大量聘用外籍看護工。

社區服務點快速增加；但家庭外籍看護工長期不在勞動基準法的全面保障之內，工時和逃逸問題持續。

✓ 可借鑑

稅收融資加社區三級網絡，是與香港邏輯最接近的模式：可借「巷弄站」概念，把長者鄰舍中心、關愛隊和社區客廳整合成步行可達的服務點。

✗ 不可照搬

把居家照顧長期寄託在缺乏勞動保障的外籍看護身上，形成灰色市場和照顧質素問題。香港試行外籍家居照顧員，正要避免這一點：工時、休息和投訴機制必須寫進合約。

Taiwan region

Long-Term Care 2.0 (2017) is tax-funded rather than insurance-funded, drawing on tobacco, estate and property-transaction taxes, and builds a three-tier network: A (integrated centres), B (multi-service centres) and C (neighbourhood care stations). Households also rely heavily on foreign live-in caregivers.

Community service points multiplied quickly, but foreign live-in caregivers have long sat outside full labour-standards protection, and hours and absconding remain problems.

✓ Transferable

Tax funding plus a three-tier community network is closest to Hong Kong’s logic: the neighbourhood-station idea could join up Neighbourhood Elderly Centres, Care Teams and community living rooms into walkable service points.

✗ Not transferable

Relying on foreign live-in carers without adequate labour protection created a grey market and quality problems. Hong Kong’s pilot must avoid exactly this by writing hours, rest and complaints into the contract.

韓國

2008年實施老人長期療養保險；2024年首爾試行經僱傭許可制引入約100名菲律賓家務及照顧人員，按韓國最低工資受聘。

以最低工資計算，每月開支對一般家庭偏高，需求集中在較富裕地區，並出現離崗事件——輸入照顧人手的成敗取決於成本結構和僱傭安排，而非單純人手供應。

✓ 可借鑑

試行前先做成本和需求評估：若外籍家居照顧員工資高於外傭，必須說清楚誰負擔得起、是否配合長者券或照顧者津貼，否則試行只會令中產受惠。

✗ 不可照搬

韓國以最低工資定價，是因為其外勞受本地最低工資法保障；香港外傭另有「規定最低工資」制度，兩地的工資機制和法律框架不同，其定價結論不能直接套用。

South Korea

Long-term care insurance for older people since 2008; in 2024 Seoul piloted around 100 Filipino domestic and care workers under the employment permit system, paid the Korean minimum wage.

At minimum-wage rates the monthly cost was high for ordinary families, demand clustered in wealthier districts and some workers left their posts: success depends on cost structure and employment arrangements, not just supply.

✓ Transferable

Assess cost and demand first: if foreign carers earn more than FDHs, say who can afford them and whether vouchers or carer allowances apply, or the pilot will benefit only the middle class.

✗ Not transferable

Korea priced at the minimum wage because its foreign workers are covered by local minimum-wage law; Hong Kong FDHs have a separate Minimum Allowable Wage, so the pricing conclusions do not transfer.

政策目的

Policy objective

讓長者盡可能在熟悉的社區安老，把院舍與醫院留給最有需要的人，同時控制公共開支的長期增長。既定方針是「居家安老為本、院舍照顧為後援」。

To enable older people to age in place for as long as possible, reserving residential and hospital care for those who most need it, while containing the long-run growth in public expenditure. The standing policy is "ageing in place as the core, institutional care as back-up".

核心矛盾

Core tension

需求隨人口結構以幾何級數上升，而土地、人手與財政只能以算術級數增加。更棘手的是：便宜的選項（社區照顧）使用率偏低，昂貴的選項（院舍、急症）卻大排長龍。

Demand rises geometrically with the age structure of the population, while land, manpower and public money grow arithmetically. The sharper problem is that the cheap option — community care — is under-used, while the expensive options — residential homes and accident and emergency — are over-subscribed.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

按高齡化對策報告的推算，2046年65歲及以上人口約274萬，佔總人口超過三分之一。資助長期護理服務名額在2025年底約69 500個；五年規劃要求2030年底增至約77 500個。勞動人口正逐步遞減，護理是人手最緊絀的行業之一。政府已把「照顧者」（尤其以老護老、以老護殘）納入精準扶貧的目標群組，低收入護老者生活津貼亦加至每月3,300元。

Current position

The ageing strategies report projects about 2.74 million people aged 65 or over by 2046, more than a third of the population. Subsidised long-term care places stood at about 69 500 at the end of 2025; the Plan requires about 77 500 by the end of 2030. The workforce is shrinking and care is among the tightest sectors. Carers, especially elders caring for elders or for disabled relatives, are now a target group of the Commission on Targeted Poverty Alleviation, and the living allowance for low-income carers has been raised to $3,300 a month.

長期結構

長者人口比例持續上升，撫養比率惡化；家庭規模縮小，傳統由子女承擔照顧的模式難以為繼；照顧者本身亦日益年長（老護老）。護理人手在全球範圍內短缺，不是靠增加撥款就能解決。

Long-run structure

The share of the population aged 65 and above continues to climb and the dependency ratio is deteriorating. Households are smaller, so the traditional assumption that adult children will provide care no longer holds, and carers are themselves ageing. Care manpower is in short supply worldwide, so this is not a problem that funding alone can solve.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1服務存在但用不到（可達缺口）

申請程序繁複、資訊分散、名額與地點不對應需求；加上文化因素——長者抗拒「被照顧」的標籤，子女視使用服務為失職——令規劃容量與實際使用率長期脫節。

1Services exist but are not reached (access gap)

Application procedures are complex, information is scattered, and places are not located where need is. Cultural factors compound this: older people resist the label of being "cared for", and adult children read the use of services as a failure of filial duty. Planned capacity and actual take-up therefore drift apart.

2服務設計與照顧者的真實需要不符

現行日間服務多在辦公時間提供，但照顧壓力的高峰在夜間、周末及突發時刻；缺乏緊急喘息安排，令照顧者無法維持受僱工作，最終被迫辭職或把長者送入院舍。

2Service design does not match what carers actually need

Day services are largely delivered in office hours, whereas the peaks of caring pressure fall at night, at weekends and in emergencies. Without emergency respite, carers cannot hold down paid work and are eventually forced either to leave employment or to place their relative in residential care.

3人手：不是錢的問題，是人的問題

護理員流失率高、職業階梯不清、社會地位不高；培訓學額擴充的效果要三至五年後才顯現，而需求是即時的。單靠加薪只會在機構之間互相挖角，不會增加總量。

3Manpower: not a money problem but a people problem

Turnover among care workers is high, the career ladder is unclear and the occupation carries low status. Expanding training places takes three to five years to show results, while demand is immediate. Pay rises alone simply move staff between operators without adding to the total pool.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

人口結構已經決定需求必然上升，提早投資比延後投資便宜。

站反方？這樣駁

提早投資的前提是知道要投資在什麼上。在服務模式未定型、使用率只有一半的情況下大幅擴容，等於把一個未經驗證的模式放大，變成更貴的錯誤。

FOR 1

The age structure has already determined that demand will rise; investing early is cheaper than investing late.

Counter

Investing early presupposes knowing what to invest in. Expanding capacity while the model is unproven and take-up sits at half is to scale up an untested design and make the error more expensive.

支持 2

社區照顧的單位成本遠低於院舍與住院，擴大社區服務長遠是省錢而非花錢。

站反方？這樣駁

單位成本比較只在「社區真的取代了院舍」時成立。若社區服務只是額外加一層而院舍需求未減，總開支是相加而非相減——現行的使用率正好說明替代並未發生。

FOR 2

The unit cost of community care is far below residential and hospital care, so expanding community services saves money over time rather than spending it.

Counter

The unit-cost comparison holds only if community care actually substitutes for residential care. If it merely adds a layer while residential demand is unchanged, the two costs add rather than offset — and current take-up suggests substitution is not happening.

支持 3

銀髮需求可以轉化為產業機會——樂齡科技、院舍管理、照顧培訓都是可出口的能力。

站反方？這樣駁

產業化需要有購買力的市場，而長者的購買力分佈極不平均。把公共照顧責任包裝成產業機會，風險是最付不起的一群被排除在市場之外。

FOR 3

Silver demand can be converted into industrial capability — gerontechnology, care-home management and carer training are all exportable.

Counter

An industry needs a market with purchasing power, and purchasing power among older people is very unevenly spread. Recasting a public duty of care as an industrial opportunity risks excluding precisely those least able to pay.

反對／制約 1

安老屬經常開支，一旦設立難以縮減，與量入為出原則構成長期張力。

站支持方？這樣駁

剛性的是承諾，不是設計。服務券、分級定額、日落條款與期滿重新立法，都能讓開支隨需求與成效調整。何況不作為的開支同樣剛性——院舍與急症的成本只會更硬。

AGAINST 1

Elderly services are recurrent expenditure: once established they are hard to withdraw, which sits in permanent tension with the principle of keeping expenditure within the limits of revenue.

Counter

What is rigid is the commitment, not the design. Vouchers, tiered rates, sunset clauses and renewal by fresh legislation all allow expenditure to move with need and performance. Inaction is no less rigid: residential and emergency costs are harder still.

反對／制約 2

人手全球短缺，撥款增加未必轉化為服務增加。

站支持方？這樣駁

人手短缺正是改設計的理由，不是放棄的理由。分級職系、樂齡科技、家屬培訓與樞紐—衞星安排都能在人手不變下擴大覆蓋。以「請不到人」為由不做，等於讓短缺自動決定政策。

AGAINST 2

Care manpower is scarce worldwide, so additional funding does not automatically translate into additional service.

Counter

A manpower shortage is a reason to redesign, not to abandon. Tiered grades, gerontechnology, family training and hub-and-spoke arrangements all widen coverage without more staff. To do nothing because staff are scarce is to let the shortage set the policy.

反對／制約 3

公共承擔擴大可能產生道德風險，削弱家庭原有的照顧責任。

站支持方？這樣駁

替代效應的前提在現實中並不成立：今日的家庭承擔的照顧已遠超其能力。制度介入所替代的不是家庭責任，而是照顧者的健康與收入；照顧者倒下之後，公共成本只會更高。

AGAINST 3

A wider public commitment may create moral hazard and erode the caring responsibility families currently carry.

Counter

The substitution premise does not hold in practice: families already carry more care than they can bear. What public provision displaces is not family duty but the carer's health and income — and when the carer collapses, the public cost is higher still.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊與外展

由被動等候申請改為外展式個案經理主動接觸；與屋苑管理處、家庭醫生、長者中心建立固定轉介點。

Information and outreach

Replace passive application with outreach by case managers, and build standing referral points with estate management offices, family doctors and elderly centres.

誘因

服務券讓使用者選擇營運者，迫使營運者按需求調整服務時段與內容。

Incentives

Vouchers let users choose the operator, forcing operators to adjust hours and content to what users actually want.

重新設計

試行夜間、周末及緊急喘息服務；把服務單位與地區康健中心、日間中心共址以降低成本與抗拒。

Service redesign

Pilot night, weekend and emergency respite; co-locate units with district health centres and day centres to cut cost and reduce resistance.

供應側

擴大培訓學額並設立清晰職業階梯；有限度輸入護理員，同時把配額與本地培訓投入掛鈎。

Supply side

Expand training places and establish a clear career ladder; admit care workers from outside in a limited way, with quotas tied to the operator's own investment in local training.

科技

遠程監察、跌倒偵測、用藥提醒——把人手用在必須有人的地方。

Technology

Remote monitoring, fall detection and medication reminders, so that staff time is spent where a human presence is indispensable.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

外展與個案經理本身需要人手，短期內會與服務前線競爭同一批人。

化解

先以現有社工重新分配職務並輔以行政助理，避免新增專業崗位。

Challenge 1

Outreach and case management themselves need staff and will compete with front-line services for the same people in the short run.

Mitigation

Redeploy existing social workers with administrative support rather than creating new professional posts.

挑戰 2

服務券可能被營運者用來挑選較易照顧的個案（cream-skimming）。

化解

按被照顧者依賴程度分級定價，令高需要個案同樣有利可圖。

Challenge 2

Vouchers may be used by operators to select easier cases (cream-skimming).

Mitigation

Tier the voucher value by the dependency level of the user so that high-need cases remain financially viable.

挑戰 3

輸入護理員必然引起本地工會反對。

化解

以客觀短缺數據作認定基礎，訂明本地優先招聘期與可審計紀錄，並把輸入配額與培訓投入掛鈎。

Challenge 3

Admitting care workers from outside will draw union opposition.

Mitigation

Base the determination of shortage on objective data, require an auditable local-first recruitment period, and tie quotas to training investment.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

財政司／庫務

強調單位成本比較與延後入住院舍所省下的開支，並提出分階段與檢討機制。

Treasury

Lead with unit-cost comparisons and the expenditure avoided by delaying residential placement, and offer phasing plus a review point.

立法會議員

以各區服務名額與輪候時間的均衡分佈回應，並承諾公開按區指標。

Legislators

Respond with the even distribution of places and waiting times across districts, and commit to publishing indicators by district.

工會與前線護理員

先講職業階梯、培訓資助與不減本地職位，再談輸入。

Unions and front-line carers

Lead with the career ladder, subsidised training and a guarantee on local posts before discussing admission of workers from outside.

家屬與照顧者

強調喘息服務與個案經理的單一聯絡點，減少他們自己奔走的負擔。

Families and carers

Emphasise respite provision and a single point of contact through a case manager, so that they are not left to navigate the system themselves.

可直接用的句式

Ready sentences

安老服務的成敗，不在於興建了多少設施，而在於最需要的人有沒有用到。

The test of elderly services is not how many facilities are built but whether those who most need them actually use them.

表面上這是供應不足的問題，實質上是可達性與服務設計的問題。

What presents itself as a shortage of supply is in substance a problem of accessibility and service design.

任何涉及長期經常開支的建議，均須說明其財政可持續性，否則不成建議。

Any proposal carrying long-term recurrent expenditure must address its own fiscal sustainability, or it is not a proposal at all.

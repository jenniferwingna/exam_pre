一句話方向

北都由「藍圖」進入「收成期」，發展主線改為「以大學城帶動」：新田、洪水橋、打鼓嶺三個大學城以「一城五元素」布局；熟地五年900公頃屬約束性指標；以《北部都會區發展條例草案》、片區開發、按實補價和環評數據庫提速；以招商優惠政策包和5%／半稅吸引企業；並以城鄉共融、交通先行和「住大些」回應生活質素。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，北部都會區應優先引入產業和大學，而非住屋，以避免成為「睡城」。你是否同意？試分析現況，提出措施，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說發展商參與片區開發。

出處：五年規劃第三篇「加快北部都會區建設 引領空間格局優化」（第十一至十三章、專欄25–27）、主要指標11–12；施政報告第五章「加快建設北部都會區」（§183–223）、§229

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

The Northern Metropolis moves from blueprint to harvest, with university towns as the main thread: San Tin, Hung Shui Kiu and Ta Kwu Ling are planned around “one town, five elements”; 900 hectares of spade-ready land in five years is a binding target; the Northern Metropolis Development Bill, large-scale land disposal, Pay for What You Build and a database-assisted EIA speed things up; preferential policy packages and 5 per cent or half-rate tax attract firms; and urban-rural integration, transport first and larger flats address quality of life.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some argue the Northern Metropolis should prioritise industry and universities over housing to avoid becoming a dormitory town. Do you agree? Analyse the situation, propose measures, analyse the challenges and responses, and persuade developers to take part in large-scale land disposal.

§204–205

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

以住屋為主的新市鎮→以大學城和產業為主線的「宜居宜業宜遊」都會

過去新市鎮以安置人口為主；北都把教育、科技、產業放在住屋之前或同步。

New towns built around housing→A metropolis led by universities and industry

Past new towns housed people first; the north puts education, technology and industry ahead of, or alongside, housing.

政府收地、平整、賣地→片區開發、按實補價、交回土地抵銷地價

引入市場資金承擔平整和基建，政府角色由「全包」轉為「規則制定者」。

Government resumes, forms and sells land→Large-scale disposal, Pay for What You Build, land surrender

Private capital takes on site formation and infrastructure; the Government shifts from doing everything to setting rules.

一般法例程序→北都專屬法例

以專屬法例精簡城規、收地、審批，並設時限和上報機制。

General statutory procedures→Dedicated legislation

Planning, resumption and approvals streamlined with time limits and escalation.

一視同仁的批地與稅制→按企業貢獻度身訂造的優惠

優惠政策包和5%／半稅是香港傳統「簡單低稅、不設個別優惠」的重大轉變。

Uniform land and tax rules→Incentives tailored to each firm’s contribution

Tailored packages and 5 per cent or half-rate tax are a major departure from Hong Kong’s simple, uniform tax tradition.

城鄉發展對立→城鄉共融

以基金和指引讓村民參與鄉郊旅遊，減少發展阻力。

Urban development against the countryside→Urban-rural integration

A fund and guidelines give villagers a stake in rural tourism, easing resistance to development.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府以「有為政府」主導北都：制度創新（專屬法例、片區開發）、財政和稅務誘因、政府全資公司推動產業園區；程序上主張「精簡重複、保留底線」；強調「政、產、學、研、投」協同，並把北都定位為融入大灣區的主平台和發展新質生產力的新引擎。

Government stance

The Government leads the Northern Metropolis as a proactive state: institutional innovation (dedicated law, large-scale disposal), fiscal and tax incentives, and wholly owned companies driving industrial parks; on procedure it cuts duplication while keeping safeguards; it stresses collaboration among government, industry, academia, research and investors, and casts the north as the main platform for GBA integration and a new engine of productive forces.

北都專屬法例精簡程序：提速，還是削弱保障？

支持的理據

傳統程序由規劃到入伙逾十年；專屬法例消除重複程序、設時限，令土地更快到位；環評數據庫以科技提升質素（有案例減少近七成受影響生態敏感區）。

質疑的理據

收地補償和城規程序縮短，可能削弱受影響居民的申訴和公眾參與；生態評估時間縮短令保育團體憂慮。

Dedicated legislation: speed or weaker safeguards?

Case for

Conventional procedures take over a decade from planning to intake; removing duplication and setting deadlines gets land ready faster, and the database improves EIA quality — one case cut affected sensitive areas by nearly 70 per cent.

Case against

Shorter compensation and planning procedures may weaken affected residents’ recourse and public participation; conservation groups worry about compressed ecological assessment.

招商優惠政策包與5%／半稅：吸引企業，還是破壞公平？

支持的理據

國際招商競爭激烈，鄰近地區都有度身訂造優惠；以企業投入和貢獻為準則，並由委員會審批。

質疑的理據

違背「簡單低稅、一視同仁」的傳統；本地中小企得不到同等優惠；稅基受影響；企業可能只「掛名」取得優惠。

Incentive packages and 5 per cent tax: winning investment or breaching fairness?

Case for

Competition for investment is fierce and neighbours all offer tailored deals; eligibility rests on investment and contribution, vetted by a committee.

Case against

It departs from simple, uniform low tax; local SMEs do not get the same; the tax base erodes; firms may register nominally to capture benefits.

以大學城為主線：遠見，還是高風險？

支持的理據

大學帶來人才、研究和國際聯繫，是創科產業的根基；北都大學城可成為大灣區「人才高地」。

質疑的理據

院校財政能力不一，擴展成本高；學生來源（本地學齡人口下降）依賴非本地生；若產業未到位，大學城會與經濟脫節。

University towns as the main thread: vision or risk?

Case for

Universities bring talent, research and international links — the foundation of an innovation economy — and can make the north a talent hub for the GBA.

Case against

Institutions’ finances vary and expansion is costly; with fewer local school-leavers, enrolment depends on non-local students; without industry, the towns may be cut off from the economy.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持北都方向；答題強調「就業與住屋同步」「交通先行」「程序精簡但保留公眾參與和生態底線」「優惠要透明、有實質營運要求和回撥條款」。紅線：不要否定北都——它是國家和五年規劃的旗艦項目；也不要以環保為由主張停止發展，應以生態補償、保護區和數據化環評平衡。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support the Northern Metropolis and stress jobs and homes together, transport first, streamlining that keeps public participation and ecological floors, and incentives that are transparent with substance requirements and clawback. Red lines: do not reject the Northern Metropolis — a flagship of the national plan and the Plan; and do not call for halting development on environmental grounds — balance it with ecological compensation, protected areas and data-driven assessment.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡（裕廊集團與緯壹科技城）

由法定機構裕廊集團（JTC）統一規劃、開發和管理工業用地與產業園區；緯壹科技城把研究機構、大學和企業集中於同一區，並配合住宅和生活設施。

產業用地和企業落戶由單一機構負責，效率高；緯壹成為生物醫藥和資訊科技集群。

✓ 可借鑑

以政府全資公司作「總開發商」統一規劃、招商和管理——正是新田科技城公司、洪水橋產業園公司的模式；緯壹「研、學、住」混合設計可用於大學城。

✗ 不可照搬

JTC掌握大量國有土地並長期持有；北都涉及私人業權、原居民權益和收地補償，土地整合的時間和成本高得多。

Singapore (JTC and one-north)

The statutory board JTC plans, develops and manages industrial land and parks; one-north clusters research institutes, universities and firms with homes and amenities.

One agency handles industrial land and investors efficiently; one-north became a biomedical and IT cluster.

✓ Transferable

Wholly government-owned master developers for planning, investment promotion and management — exactly the San Tin and Hung Shui Kiu companies; one-north’s mix of research, learning and living suits the university towns.

✗ Not transferable

JTC controls large amounts of state land long-term; the north involves private titles, indigenous villagers’ rights and compensation, making land assembly slower and costlier.

日本（筑波研究學園都市）

1960至80年代把大量國立研究機構和筑波大學遷往筑波，建立科學城。

研究機構集中，但長期被批評與產業聯繫弱、生活配套和交通不足，直至2005年筑波快線通車後才明顯改善。

✓ 可借鑑

反面教訓：大學城必須與產業和交通同步，否則成為「只有研究、沒有城市」；北都「交通先行」和「一城五元素」正是回應。

✗ 不可照搬

筑波以中央政府遷移國立機構為主；香港大學城依賴院校自願進駐和其財政能力，推動方式不同。

Japan (Tsukuba Science City)

From the 1960s to the 1980s Japan moved national research institutes and the University of Tsukuba to build a science city.

Research was concentrated, but for years the city was criticised for weak industry links and poor amenities and transport, improving markedly only after the Tsukuba Express opened in 2005.

✓ Transferable

A cautionary tale: a university town without industry and transport becomes research without a city; transport-first and “five elements” respond to this.

✗ Not transferable

Tsukuba relocated national institutions by central decision; Hong Kong’s university towns depend on institutions choosing to come and being able to afford it.

韓國（板橋科技谷與世宗市）

板橋科技谷位於首爾近郊，吸引大量資訊科技和遊戲企業；世宗市作為行政首都遷移，人口增長和企業進駐較慢，公務員長期兩地通勤。

區位和交通決定成敗：靠近人才和市場的板橋成功，行政遷移的世宗較慢。

✓ 可借鑑

北都吸引企業須與市區和深圳有快速連接——北環線、港深西部鐵路是關鍵；可用「靠近兩地人才」作為招商論據。

✗ 不可照搬

世宗市以行政命令遷移政府部門；香港不會以行政命令遷移企業，只能以誘因和配套吸引。

South Korea (Pangyo and Sejong)

Pangyo Techno Valley near Seoul drew IT and gaming firms; Sejong, the relocated administrative capital, grew slowly in people and business, with officials commuting for years.

Location and transport decide success: Pangyo near talent and markets thrived, while administratively relocated Sejong lagged.

✓ Transferable

The north needs fast links to the urban core and Shenzhen — the Northern Link and Western Rail Link are key — and can pitch proximity to talent on both sides.

✗ Not transferable

Sejong moved ministries by decree; Hong Kong cannot move firms by order and must attract them with incentives and amenities.

深圳前海

前海深港現代服務業合作區以15%企業所得稅優惠、港人就業創業支援、容許選用香港法律等制度創新吸引企業。

企業註冊數量大增；但實際營運規模和「空殼公司」問題曾受關注。

✓ 可借鑑

稅務優惠須與實質營運要求（員工、辦公地點、營業額）掛鈎，防止「掛名」——§211的5%／半稅條例草案可加入實質活動要求和回撥條款。

✗ 不可照搬

前海15%稅率相對內地25%屬優惠；香港利得稅本已較低（16.5%），5%／半稅的邊際吸引力和對稅基的影響需另行評估。

Shenzhen Qianhai

The Qianhai co-operation zone attracts firms with a 15 per cent corporate income tax rate for encouraged industries, support for Hong Kong people to work and start businesses, and the option of Hong Kong law.

Registrations surged, though actual operating scale and shell companies drew concern.

✓ Transferable

Tax incentives should require real substance — staff, premises, turnover — to prevent nominal registration; the §211 bill could include substance tests and clawback.

✗ Not transferable

Qianhai’s 15 per cent is generous against the Mainland’s 25; Hong Kong’s profits tax is already low at 16.5 per cent, so the marginal pull and revenue cost of 5 per cent or half-rate need separate assessment.

政策目的

Policy objective

把北都建成「南金融、北創科」格局中的高等教育和創科增長引擎，同時提供住屋（五年落成7萬個單位）和就業，令新界北不再只是「睡城」，並作為香港融入大灣區的主平台。

To make the Northern Metropolis the higher-education and innovation engine of a “finance in the south, I&T in the north” layout, while delivering housing (70 000 flats in five years) and jobs so the north is no longer a dormitory, and making it Hong Kong’s main platform for integration with the Greater Bay Area.

核心矛盾

Core tension

北都要同時做到「快」（熟地五年900公頃屬約束性指標，北都專屬法例精簡程序）和「好」（產業、大學、生態、鄉郊共融）。提速必然壓縮諮詢、環評和收地程序；而產業和大學能否真正進駐，取決於市場和院校，並非政府單方面可以決定。

The Northern Metropolis must be both fast — 900 hectares of spade-ready land in five years is a binding target, and dedicated legislation streamlines procedures — and good, with industry, universities, ecology and urban-rural integration. Speed compresses consultation, environmental assessment and land resumption, while whether firms and universities actually come depends on markets and institutions, not on the Government alone.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

北都面積和未來人口約佔全港三分之一。至2025-26年度累計產出約120公頃熟地；五年規劃要求2026-27至2030-31年度產出900公頃（約束性），住宅落成由11 000個增至70 000個。九個新發展區全數開動；河套香港園區2025年12月開園；洪水橋／厦村片區開發項目2026年8月批出；《北部都會區發展條例草案》爭取年內通過。

Current position

The Northern Metropolis accounts for about a third of Hong Kong’s land and future population. About 120 hectares of spade-ready land had been produced by 2025-26; the Plan requires 900 hectares in 2026-27 to 2030-31 (binding) and housing completions to rise from 11 000 to 70 000. All nine new development areas are under way; the Loop’s Hong Kong Park opened in December 2025; the Hung Shui Kiu/Ha Tsuen large-scale land disposal was awarded in August 2026; the Northern Metropolis Development Bill is due to pass this year.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

大規模土地儲備令政府「掌握土地供應主導權」，可同時滿足住屋和產業需要。

站反方？這樣駁

土地儲備只是供應潛力；若產業和就業不到位，大量熟地和住宅會令北都成為睡城，「主導權」變成財政包袱。

FOR 1

A large land bank lets the Government lead land supply and meet housing and industrial needs together.

Counter

A land bank is only potential supply; without jobs, abundant land and housing make the north a dormitory and the “lead” becomes a fiscal burden.

支持 2

以大學城和河套帶動「產學研」，把科研成果留在香港，並與深圳形成互補。

站反方？這樣駁

大學城帶動產學研需要時間和院校財力；把大學當作產業引擎，可能高估了研究轉化的速度。

FOR 2

University towns and the Loop drive industry–academia–research links, keeping research outcomes in Hong Kong and complementing Shenzhen.

Counter

University-led clusters take time and money from institutions; treating universities as industrial engines may overestimate how fast research turns into business.

支持 3

片區開發、按實補價等創新模式引入市場資金，減輕政府前期投資壓力。

站反方？這樣駁

市場資金的前提是市場有利可圖；樓市下行時片區開發吸引力下降，政府最終仍要承擔基建。

FOR 3

Large-scale land disposal and “Pay for What You Build” bring in private capital and ease the Government’s up-front burden.

Counter

Private capital comes only when profitable; in a downturn large-scale disposal loses appeal and the Government still ends up funding infrastructure.

反對／制約 1

精簡程序可能削弱環評和公眾諮詢，影響濕地和鄉郊生態。

站支持方？這樣駁

精簡的是重複程序；以環境數據庫優化走線，有案例把受影響生態敏感區減少近七成，說明提速和保育可以同時達到。

AGAINST 1

Streamlining may weaken EIA and consultation, harming wetlands and rural ecology.

Counter

Streamlining removes duplication; the environmental database has cut affected sensitive areas by nearly 70 per cent in one case, showing speed and conservation can go together.

反對／制約 2

招商引資需要批地、地價和稅務優惠，有「用公帑補貼企業」的公平性爭議。

站支持方？這樣駁

優惠以企業投入和貢獻為準則，並可設實質營運要求和回撥條款；鄰近地區都有類似政策，不做等於放棄競爭。

AGAINST 2

Attracting firms needs land, premium and tax concessions, raising fairness questions about subsidising businesses.

Counter

Incentives depend on investment and contribution, with substance requirements and clawback; neighbouring economies all offer them, and doing nothing means opting out of the competition.

反對／制約 3

賣地收入下跌時，大量基建投資加重財政壓力；借貸融資的可持續性受質疑。

站支持方？這樣駁

基建分期、以片區開發和保險資金融資，可分散財政風險；北都帶來的長遠土地和稅收亦是回報。

AGAINST 3

When land revenue falls, heavy infrastructure spending strains public finances and questions arise over borrowing.

Counter

Phased infrastructure financed through large-scale disposal and insurance capital spreads fiscal risk, and the north’s long-term land and tax revenue is the return.

一句話方向

教育同時面對「兩頭」：基礎教育因學齡人口下降而收縮——以「滙流升級計劃」鼓勵資助中小學合併、「做大做強」；高等教育則擴張——北都三個大學城、研究院學額增三成、非本地生10萬人。中間是質素和價值觀：教師須持執業證書、數字及AI教育、愛國主義及國家安全教育，以及職專教育和「技能為本」培訓。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，面對學齡人口下降，政府應積極推動資助中小學合併。試分析現況，就如何推行「滙流升級計劃」提出建議，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說辦學團體參與。

出處：五年規劃第五篇第十八章「發展優質教育」（FYP 5.2–5.10）、第十九章（青年，FYP 5.11–5.15）、第三篇第十一章（北都大學城）；施政報告第七章第一、二節（§269–287）、§25–26（國民及國家安全教育）、§185–194（北都大學城）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Education faces both ends at once: basic education is shrinking with the school-age population, so the “Confluence and Elevation” project encourages subsidised schools to merge and grow stronger; higher education is expanding — three university towns in the Northern Metropolis, 30 per cent more research postgraduate places and 100 000 non-local students. In between come quality and values: practising certificates for teachers, digital and AI education, patriotic and national security education, and vocational and skills-based training.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say that, with a falling school-age population, the Government should actively promote mergers of subsidised schools. Analyse the situation, propose how to implement the Confluence and Elevation project, analyse the challenges and responses, and persuade school sponsoring bodies to take part.

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

政府把合併由「殺校」重新包裝為「滙流升級」，重點是資源整合和質素，而非單純關校。

Mergers are reframed from school closures to “confluence and elevation”, stressing consolidation and quality rather than closure.

From lifelong registration to a licence that depends on meeting training requirements — stronger professional accountability.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

基礎教育以「質素與規模」取代「保留每一間學校」，但以鼓勵和支援合併為手段，而非強制；教師專業以執業證書和培訓要求加強問責；價值觀教育（愛國主義、國家安全、中華文化）是必要部分；高等教育是國際化、創科和人口政策的交匯點。

Government stance

In basic education, quality and scale replace keeping every school open, but through encouraging and supporting mergers rather than compulsion; teacher accountability tightens through practising certificates and training requirements; values education — patriotism, national security, Chinese culture — is an integral part; higher education sits where internationalisation, innovation and population policy meet.

學校合併：提升質素，還是削弱社區和教師？

支持的理據

學生人數下降，小型學校難以提供多元課程和活動；合併可整合師資與資源，令學生受惠；以誘因鼓勵、辦學團體自願，屬溫和方式。

質疑的理據

學校是社區的一部分，合併後家長接送距離增加；教師面對裁減或調職；辦學團體歷史與傳統難以融合；誘因不足時合併只會發生在最弱的學校。

School mergers: better quality or weaker communities and teachers?

Case for

With fewer pupils, small schools struggle to offer a broad curriculum; mergers pool teachers and resources to students’ benefit; voluntary mergers with incentives are a moderate approach.

Case against

Schools anchor communities and mergers lengthen journeys; teachers face redundancy or redeployment; sponsoring bodies’ traditions are hard to combine; with weak incentives only the weakest schools will merge.

教師執業證書：提升專業，還是增加行政和寒蟬？

支持的理據

醫生、律師、社工均有執業規管；證書配培訓要求可保持專業水平，並處理失德個案。

質疑的理據

教師工作量已重，培訓要求增加壓力；證書續期標準若不清晰，可能令教師擔心；需要清晰、公平的程序。

Practising certificates: professionalism or more paperwork?

Case for

Doctors, lawyers and social workers all hold practising licences; certificates with training keep standards up and deal with misconduct.

Case against

Teachers are already overloaded and training adds pressure; unclear renewal criteria create anxiety; procedures must be clear and fair.

北都大學城：帶動發展，還是「只有大學、沒有城市」？

支持的理據

以大學為主線匯聚教育、科技、產業和人才，形成產學研生態；結合新發展區可同步規劃住屋和交通。

質疑的理據

大學城若缺乏產業和生活配套，會成為孤立校園；院校財政能力不一；與現有校園的分工未明。

Northern Metropolis university towns: engine of growth or campuses without a city?

Case for

Universities anchor education, technology, industry and talent into an ecosystem, planned alongside housing and transport in new development areas.

Case against

Without industry and amenities they become isolated campuses; institutions’ finances vary; the division of labour with existing campuses is unclear.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持「做大做強」和專業化方向，把重點放在執行：合併的誘因設計、教師過渡安排、家長交通、學校傳統融合；執業證書的程序公平和培訓支援。紅線：不要反對國民及國家安全教育（可討論教學方法和資源）；也不要把合併寫成「殺校」——用官方的「滙流升級」「資源整合」語言。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support scale and professionalisation and focus on delivery: incentives for mergers, teacher transition, pupils’ journeys, blending school traditions; fair procedures and training support for certificates. Red lines: do not oppose national or national security education — discuss pedagogy and resources instead; and do not call mergers “school killing” — use the official language of confluence, elevation and resource consolidation.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

因出生率下降，2019年起多輪合併中小學及初級學院（2019年一次過宣布合併共28間學校）；2024年以「全科目編班」取代分流制度；以「技能創前程」（SkillsFuture）推動終身學習，2024年起為40歲或以上人士提供4 000坡元進修補助。

合併由教育部統一規劃、按地區人口進行；技能創前程提升在職人士進修參與。

✓ 可借鑑

以「地區人口推算」公開合併準則，令決定可預期；技能創前程的「按年齡加碼補助」可作為香港檢討持續進修基金的參考。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡公立學校由教育部直接管理，合併可由上而下決定；香港大部分資助學校由辦學團體營辦，合併必須取得辦學團體同意，只能以誘因鼓勵。

Singapore

Because of falling births, rounds of primary, secondary and junior college mergers from 2019 (28 schools in a single announcement); full subject-based banding replaced streaming in 2024; SkillsFuture drives lifelong learning, with a S$4,000 top-up for those aged 40 and over from 2024.

Mergers were planned centrally by district demographics; SkillsFuture raised adult learning participation.

✓ Transferable

Publishing merger criteria based on district demographics makes decisions predictable; age-based top-ups under SkillsFuture are a reference for reviewing Hong Kong’s Continuing Education Fund.

✗ Not transferable

Singapore’s schools are run by the ministry and can be merged top-down; most Hong Kong subsidised schools belong to sponsoring bodies whose consent is needed, so only incentives will work.

日本

少子化令公立學校持續關閉或合併，推動「小中一貫校」（九年一貫學校）；2020年起「GIGA學校構想」為每名中小學生提供一部電腦。

一貫校保留了社區學校的同時擴大規模；GIGA令數碼學習普及，但教師運用能力參差。

✓ 可借鑑

「一貫校」式合併（小學和中學合併成一條龍）可作為香港合併的另一種形式，保留社區學校；數字教育必須配教師培訓，與§270的AI教學助理相呼應。

✗ 不可照搬

日本市町村教育委員會擁有地方學校規劃權；香港學校規劃集中在教育局，並受辦學團體制度影響，合併程序和持份者不同。

Japan

Falling births have closed or merged public schools steadily, with integrated nine-year compulsory schools; the GIGA School initiative from 2020 gave every pupil a device.

Integrated schools keep a local school while gaining scale; GIGA spread digital learning, though teachers’ skills vary.

✓ Transferable

Through-train mergers of primary and secondary schools are an alternative form that keeps a local school; digital education needs teacher training, echoing the AI teaching assistants in §270.

✗ Not transferable

Japan’s municipal boards of education control local school planning; Hong Kong planning sits with the EDB and is shaped by sponsoring bodies.

韓國

學齡人口「斷崖」令地方大學面臨收生危機；2023年起推行「Glocal大學30」計劃，向約30所地方大學各提供約1,000億韓圜，條件是進行結構改革（包括合併）和與地方產業協作。

以大額資助換取改革，推動部分大學合併和專業化；但成效仍待觀察。

✓ 可借鑑

「資助換改革」：香港「滙流升級計劃」可把額外資源與合併後的具體質素指標掛鈎；大學城亦可要求院校與產業協作作為用地條件。

✗ 不可照搬

韓國的危機源自大學數目過多和首都圈集中；香港資助大學數目有限、國際生需求強，問題性質不同，不能照搬其大學縮減邏輯。

South Korea

A demographic cliff threatens regional universities; the Glocal University 30 programme from 2023 gives about 30 regional universities around KRW 100 billion each, conditional on restructuring (including mergers) and partnership with local industry.

Big grants in exchange for reform prompted some mergers and specialisation, though results are still unclear.

✓ Transferable

Money for reform: extra resources under Confluence and Elevation could be tied to quality indicators after merger, and university-town land could require industry partnerships.

✗ Not transferable

Korea’s crisis stems from too many universities and concentration in the capital region; Hong Kong has few funded universities and strong international demand, so its shrinkage logic does not apply.

台灣地區

少子化令私立大專院校收生不足，2022年訂立《私立高級中等以上學校退場條例》，設專案輔導和退場基金，保障師生權益。

為學校退場提供法律程序和學生轉學、教職員安置的保障。

✓ 可借鑑

「退場也要有秩序」：香港推動合併時，可同步訂立教師過渡安排和學生保障的清晰程序，減少不確定性。

✗ 不可照搬

台灣的退場條例針對財務危機的私立學校；香港資助學校由公帑支持，問題是規模效益而非破產，工具和語言都不同。

Taiwan region

Falling enrolments hit private colleges; a 2022 law on the exit of private upper-secondary and tertiary institutions set up special guidance and an exit fund to protect students and staff.

It provides a legal process for closures with protection for student transfers and staff redeployment.

✓ Transferable

Orderly exit: as Hong Kong promotes mergers it can set clear procedures for teacher transition and student protection, reducing uncertainty.

✗ Not transferable

Taiwan’s law targets financially failing private schools; Hong Kong’s subsidised schools are publicly funded and the issue is scale, not insolvency.

★★★中文卷

有意見認為，面對學齡人口下降，政府應積極推動資助中小學合併。試分析現況，就如何推行「滙流升級計劃」提出建議，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說辦學團體參與。

為何可能：2026/27學年全新推出，涉及教師、家長、辦學團體、社區，是典型「新措施＋持份者」題。

  1. 現況學齡人口下降、部分學校收生不足；小型學校課程和資源受限。

政策目的

Policy objective

在本地學齡人口結構性下跌的同時，把高等教育擴容並國際化，建設國際專上教育樞紐；推動「教育、科技、人才一體化」，使教育規劃對準產業需要；並以職專與學術雙軌並行，為不同能力的青年提供上流路徑。

To expand and internationalise higher education into an international post-secondary education hub while the local school-age population declines structurally; to integrate education, technology and talent so that educational planning targets industry needs; and to run vocational and academic tracks in parallel so young people of different abilities have routes upward.

核心矛盾

Core tension

同一個教育系統，兩端的方向相反：小學與中學在收縮，大學在擴張。收縮端涉及殺校、教師過剩與社區反對；擴張端涉及非本地生比例、宿舍與本地生學額的觀感。政府需要同時處理「太多學位」與「太少學位」兩種政治壓力，而且兩者在公眾眼中很容易被連結成「用資源招外地生、卻讓本地學校關門」。

The same system is moving in opposite directions at either end: primary and secondary schools are contracting while universities expand. Contraction brings closures, surplus teachers and community opposition; expansion brings questions about non-local ratios, hostels and the perception of local places. The Government faces pressure over too many places and too few at once, and in public eyes the two are easily joined into a charge of spending on outside students while local schools close.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

學齡人口下降令部分小學收生不足（2026/27學年有官津小學不獲開辦小一班）；另一端，香港擁有五所世界百強大學，教育競爭力全球第三。教育局已成立「留學香港專班」；職業訓練局推動應用科學大學發展；僱員再培訓局已升格為技能提升局。青年政策以《青年發展藍圖》為基礎，已落實逾290項措施。

Current position

A falling school-age population leaves some primary schools under-enrolled — some aided and government schools could not open Primary One classes in 2026/27. At the other end, Hong Kong has five universities in the global top 100 and ranks third in the world for education competitiveness. The EDB has a Study in Hong Kong unit, the VTC is developing universities of applied sciences, and the Employees Retraining Board has become Upskill Hong Kong. Youth policy rests on the Youth Development Blueprint, with over 290 measures delivered.

長期結構

二○二六／二七學年有 15 間官津小學在小一入學統籌辦法下獲派學生不足 16 人而不獲資助開辦小一班級（俗稱「派零班」），數目創近年新高。另一端，香港是全球唯一擁有五所世界百強大學的城市；資助專上院校非本地生學額上限由百分之四十上調至百分之五十；教育局成立「留學香港專班」並推出「Hong Kong: Your World-class Campus」宣傳。教資會目標是二○二六／二七學年資助大學學生中百分之三十五修讀 STEAM、百分之六十修讀與「八大中心」相關學科。職訓局轄下香港資訊科技學院二○二五／二六學年錄取超過 2 100 名新生；優質教育基金預留二十億元推動中小學數字教育，並於二○二六年發表《中小學數字教育藍圖》。

Long-run structure

For the 2026-27 school year, 15 aided and government primary schools were allocated fewer than 16 Primary One pupils and were not funded to operate a Primary One class, the highest number in recent years. At the other end, Hong Kong is the only city with five universities in the world’s top 100; the non-local student quota at funded institutions has been raised from 40 to 50 per cent; the Education Bureau has set up a Study in Hong Kong task force and launched the "Hong Kong: Your World-class Campus" campaign. The UGC targets 35 per cent of funded students in STEAM and 60 per cent in disciplines related to the "eight centres" by 2026-27. The VTC’s Hong Kong Institute of Information Technology admitted over 2,100 new students in 2025-26, and $2 billion has been earmarked from the Quality Education Fund for digital education, with a Blueprint for Digital Education in Primary and Secondary Schools to be published in 2026.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1收縮與擴張的觀感連結

基礎教育收縮與高教擴張分屬不同體系與撥款，但公眾會把兩者並列比較，形成「資源外流」的敘事，令兩端的政策都更難推行。

1Contraction and expansion read together

Basic education contraction and higher education expansion sit in different systems and budgets, but the public sets them side by side, producing a narrative of resources flowing outward that makes policy at both ends harder.

2教育規劃與產業需要的時間差

一個學位課程由開辦到畢業生進入市場需時四至六年，而產業需求在兩三年內已可改變。按產業需要分配學額，存在「培訓出來時需求已經轉移」的風險。

2Timing gap between planning and industry need

A degree takes four to six years from launch to graduates entering the market, while industry demand can shift in two or three. Allocating places by industry need risks training for demand that has moved on.

3職專的社會認受性

即使設立應用科學大學，家長與僱主對職專出路的判斷仍以學位為參照。職專畢業生的起薪與晉升若無實質改善，名稱改變不會改變選擇。

3Social standing of vocational routes

Even with applied science universities, parents and employers still judge vocational outcomes against degrees. Without real change in starting pay and promotion, a new name will not change choices.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1財政影響：高教擴張涉及土地、校舍與宿舍的大額資本投入，而非本地生學費可部分回收成本；基礎教育收縮則可釋放經常開支，但短期內須承擔合併與教師轉職的過渡成本。

1Fiscal: higher education expansion requires heavy capital for land, buildings and hostels, partly recovered through non-local fees; basic education contraction releases recurrent spending but carries transition costs of mergers and teacher redeployment in the short term.

2社會影響：學校是地區的公共空間與社會網絡節點，合併的影響遠超教育本身，涉及家長社群、校友網絡與地區身分。

2Social: a school is a public space and a node of community networks, so mergers affect far more than education, touching parent communities, alumni networks and district identity.

3人才影響：非本地畢業生若能留港工作，可部分抵銷勞動人口下跌；若畢業即離開，則等同以本地資源培訓他人的人才。留港率是整個政策成敗的關鍵指標。

3Talent: if non-local graduates stay to work, they partly offset the shrinking workforce; if they leave on graduation, local resources have trained others’ talent. The retention rate is the decisive measure of the whole policy.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

非本地生帶來學費收入、國際網絡與人才來源，在本地適齡人口下降時是維持大學規模與科研質素的必要條件。

站反方？這樣駁

非本地生帶來收入，但若宿舍、師資和校園空間不同步擴張，教學質素和本地生體驗都會下降；「維持規模」不能以犧牲質素換取。

FOR 1

Non-local students bring fee income, international networks and a talent pipeline, which are necessary to sustain university scale and research quality as the local cohort shrinks.

Counter

Non-local students bring revenue, but if hostels, staff and space do not grow in step, quality and local students’ experience fall; scale cannot come at the cost of quality.

支持 2

學校合併可集中資源提升教學質素：小班雖好，但過小的學校難以維持多元課程、課外活動與專科教師配置。

站反方？這樣駁

合併提升資源的前提是合併後真的整合；若只是行政合併而校舍、團隊依舊分散，好處有限，社區卻失去一間學校。

FOR 2

School mergers concentrate resources to raise quality: small classes help, but very small schools cannot sustain a varied curriculum, activities and specialist teachers.

Counter

Mergers raise quality only if the schools truly integrate; an administrative merger with separate campuses and teams yields little while the community loses a school.

支持 3

按產業需要規劃學額可減少錯配：過去部分學科畢業生供過於求，而醫護、工程與資訊科技長期短缺，引導分配是回應真實需要。

站反方？這樣駁

人力推算本身有誤差；按今天的短缺規劃學額，畢業時需求可能已變，AI更令職業需求變化加快。

FOR 3

Planning places by industry need reduces mismatch: some disciplines have long produced surplus graduates while healthcare, engineering and IT remain short, so steering allocation responds to real need.

Counter

Manpower projections are error-prone; planning places for today’s shortages may miss tomorrow’s needs, especially as AI accelerates change.

反對／制約 1

擴大非本地生比例會令本地生感到競爭加劇，尤其在研究院與熱門學科，觀感上等同削減本地機會。

站支持方？這樣駁

資助學額仍以本地生為主，非本地生多屬額外學額或自資課程；公開學額分配數據可以回應觀感問題。

AGAINST 1

Raising the non-local share intensifies perceived competition for local students, especially in research programmes and popular disciplines, and reads as cutting local opportunity.

Counter

Subsidised places remain mainly for local students, and non-local students largely take additional or self-financed places; publishing allocation data answers the perception problem.

反對／制約 2

殺校與合併對基層與偏遠地區的衝擊最大，學童可能需長途上學，而這些家庭本已最缺乏選擇。

站支持方？這樣駁

合併可以附帶交通支援和過渡安排；而且小型學校資源不足本身亦損害基層學生的學習機會。

AGAINST 2

Closures and mergers hit low-income and remote districts hardest, forcing longer journeys on children from families who already have the fewest choices.

Counter

Mergers can come with transport support and transition arrangements, and under-resourced small schools themselves hurt disadvantaged pupils.

反對／制約 3

大學的價值不止於就業，按產業需要分配學額會削弱人文與基礎學科，長遠損害社會的批判思考與文化底蘊。

站支持方？這樣駁

按需要規劃只涉及增量和部分學額，人文和基礎學科可以通過跨學科和通識課程保留；兩者並非二擇其一。

AGAINST 3

Universities are about more than jobs; allocating places by industry need weakens the humanities and basic sciences and harms critical thinking and cultural depth in the long run.

Counter

Needs-based planning affects increments and some places; humanities and basic disciplines can be kept through interdisciplinary and general education — it is not either-or.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊工具：公開學額與畢業生去向數據

按學科公開畢業生就業率、起薪與非本地生留港率，讓學生、家長與公眾以數據而非印象判斷。

Information: publish places and graduate outcomes

Publish employment rates, starting salaries and non-local retention by discipline so students, parents and the public judge on data rather than impression.

規劃工具：學額分配與產業掛鈎

透過教資會三年期規劃，把部分新增學額與策略產業掛鈎，同時保留基礎學科的底線配額。

Planning: linking allocation to industry

Use the UGC triennial planning cycle to tie part of new provision to strategic industries, while keeping a floor for basic disciplines.

過渡工具：合併升級與豁免

以合併、一次性豁免與教師轉職支援處理學齡人口下跌，避免集中式殺校。

Transition: mergers and exemptions

Manage falling rolls through mergers, one-off exemptions and teacher redeployment, avoiding concentrated closures.

空間工具：校舍轉用

把騰空校舍規劃為長者中心、託兒、社區或職專培訓用途，化解地區反對並善用公共資產。

Space: reuse of school premises

Plan vacated premises for elderly centres, childcare, community use or vocational training, defusing district opposition and using public assets well.

結構工具：資歷互認與職專升格

推動應用科學大學、副學位互認與行業認證，從制度上提升職專資歷的市場價值。

Structural: recognition and upgrading

Advance applied science universities, sub-degree recognition and industry certification to raise the market value of vocational qualifications institutionally.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

非本地生擴招引發本地生與家長對學額被擠佔的不滿。

化解

明確承諾本地生資助學額不減，非本地生以超額形式取錄並自付全費；同時公布非本地畢業生留港就業數據，把論述由「搶學位」轉為「補人才」。

Challenge 1

Expanding non-local admissions provokes resentment among local students and parents about displaced places.

Mitigation

Commit explicitly that funded local places will not fall, with non-local students admitted over quota at full fees, and publish data on non-local graduates staying to work, moving the argument from taking places to filling talent gaps.

挑戰 2

學校合併遭辦學團體、校友與地區人士反對。

化解

提前三至五年公布地區學齡人口推算，讓辦學團體有時間自行規劃；合併方案同步提出校舍轉用計劃，並設立教師轉職與提早退休的支援安排。

Challenge 2

Mergers meet opposition from sponsoring bodies, alumni and district figures.

Mitigation

Publish district school-age projections three to five years ahead so sponsors can plan themselves; pair each merger with a reuse plan for the premises and support for teacher redeployment and early retirement.

挑戰 3

按產業需要規劃學額存在預測失準的風險。

化解

以三年期滾動檢討而非一次性定額，並優先投放於跨學科與可轉移技能的課程，降低單一產業需求轉變帶來的錯配。

Challenge 3

Planning places by industry need risks forecasting error.

Mitigation

Use rolling three-year review rather than fixed quotas, and prioritise interdisciplinary programmes with transferable skills to limit mismatch when a single industry’s demand shifts.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

本地學生與家長

談資助學額不減與升學路徑多元化，並以實際數據說明非本地生是超額取錄，不佔用本地資助學位。

Local students and parents

Speak of funded local places being protected and pathways widening, showing with figures that non-local students are admitted over quota and do not take funded local places.

辦學團體與校長

談自主規劃空間：提早公布人口推算、提供合併誘因與過渡資源，讓辦學團體成為決策者而非被動承受者。

School sponsors and principals

Speak of room to plan: early projections, incentives to merge and transition resources, so sponsors are decision-makers rather than recipients.

教師與教育團體

談專業與生計：教師轉職、再培訓與提早退休安排要先於合併公布，避免把人口問題轉化為勞資對立。

Teachers and education unions

Speak of profession and livelihood: redeployment, retraining and early retirement arrangements must be announced before mergers, so a demographic problem does not become an industrial dispute.

僱主與行業組織

談參與而非只談需求：邀請行業參與課程設計與資歷認證，並承諾提供實習與聘用，令產業導向的學額規劃有實際出路。

Employers and industry bodies

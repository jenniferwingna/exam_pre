環境、氣候與城市韌性

Environment, Climate and Urban Resilience
← 議題庫
指標措施方向取態比較出題目的矛盾現況問題影響論點與反駁方案挑戰游說句式詞彙

一句話方向

環境是五年規劃中少數設「約束性」指標的範疇：碳強度、零碳能源比例、PM2.5、水質都必須達標。路徑以供應側和基建為主——輸電供港和第132區填海把零碳能源在2035年前提升至六至七成；I·PARK邁向「零廢堆填」；電動車與快充網絡，2035年或之前停止新登記燃油私家車（包括混能）——而非以收費改變行為。同時以防洪、海岸防禦和供水網更新提升城市韌性。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，都市固體廢物收費擱置後，政府應以其他方法推動減廢回收。試分析現況，提出措施，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說物業管理業界配合。

出處：五年規劃第五篇第二十五章「建設綠色宜居城市」（FYP 5.62–5.70、專欄38–39）、主要指標19–22（全屬約束性）；施政報告第七章第十一節（§401–417）、§421–422（氣候適應與供水網）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Environment is one of the few areas with binding Plan targets — carbon intensity, zero-carbon energy, PM2.5 and water quality must all be met. The route is mainly supply-side and infrastructure: imported power and the Area 132 reclamation to take zero-carbon energy to 60–70 per cent before 2035; I·PARK towards zero landfill; electric vehicles and fast chargers, with no new fuel-powered private car registrations, hybrids included, by 2035 — rather than charging to change behaviour. Flood control, coastal defence and water-main renewal build resilience alongside.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say that with MSW charging shelved, the Government should promote waste reduction and recycling by other means. Analyse the situation, propose measures, analyse the challenges and responses, and persuade the property management sector to co-operate.

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

以收費改變行為減廢→以設施供應和回收網絡減廢

都市固體廢物收費已擱置；政府改以I·PARK、24小時智能回收、廚餘桶等「供應側」工具推動，承認收費在香港的執行和社會接受度問題。

Charging to change behaviour→Facilities and recycling networks

With MSW charging shelved, the Government relies on supply-side tools — I·PARK, 24-hour smart recycling, food waste bins — acknowledging the enforcement and acceptance problems of charging in Hong Kong.

本地發電為主→區域能源合作加本地策略設施

輸電供港和第132區新設施並行，以國家清潔能源體系補足本地土地限制。

Mainly local generation→Regional energy co-operation plus local strategic facilities

Imported power and new facilities at Area 132 proceed together, using the national clean-energy system to offset local land limits.

環境目標作願景→約束性指標

碳強度、零碳能源、PM2.5、水質四項均列為約束性，是五年規劃中問責力度最高的一類。

Environmental goals as aspirations→Binding indicators

Carbon intensity, zero-carbon energy, PM2.5 and water quality are all binding — the most accountable category in the Plan.

電動化只及私家車→擴展至商用車，並訂燃油車停售時間表

2035年或之前停止新登記燃油私家車（包括混能）；公共小巴率先探討電動化。

Electrification of private cars only→Commercial vehicles too, with a phase-out date

No new fuel-powered private cars, hybrids included, by 2035; public light buses first among commercial vehicles.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府以「供應側＋基建」推動減碳和減廢，避免直接加重市民負擔的收費措施；能源方面強調「零碳、能源安全、穩定電價」三者平衡，並以國家資源（輸電、閃充技術、標準互認）補足本地限制。生態保育以「保育紅線」和保護區比例作承諾，同時容許北都等發展以科技縮短環評。

Government stance

The Government pursues decarbonisation and waste reduction through supply and infrastructure, avoiding charges that directly burden households; in energy it balances zero carbon, security of supply and stable tariffs, using national resources — imported power, charging technology, mutual standards — to offset local limits. On ecology it commits to conservation red lines and protected-area shares while letting development such as the Northern Metropolis shorten environmental assessment with technology.

減廢：靠設施還是靠收費？

支持的理據

收費在台北、首爾等地證明有效，亦符合「污染者自付」；沒有價格訊號，市民缺乏減量誘因；I·PARK只處理廢物，不會減少廢物。

質疑的理據

香港高樓大廈以垃圾房集中收集，按量收費難以追溯到個別住戶；社會對「加重負擔」反彈大；先建回收設施、培養習慣再談收費更務實。

Waste: facilities or charges?

Case for

Charging has worked in Taipei and Seoul and follows the polluter-pays principle; without a price signal there is little incentive to cut waste; I·PARK treats waste but does not reduce it.

Case against

Hong Kong’s high-rises collect waste centrally, so volume charging cannot be traced to households; resistance to new burdens is strong; building recycling habits and facilities first is more practical.

輸電供港：提升零碳比例，還是增加供電風險？

支持的理據

內地清潔能源規模大、成本低，可快速提高零碳比例並有助穩定電價；香港土地有限，難以大規模發展本地可再生能源。

質疑的理據

供電來源集中需要備用容量和電網韌性；電價機制和兩電管制協議如何配合；本地發電設施的角色和就業。

Imported power: faster decarbonisation or supply risk?

Case for

Large, low-cost Mainland clean energy can raise the zero-carbon share quickly and help stabilise tariffs; Hong Kong lacks land for large local renewables.

Case against

Concentrated supply requires reserve capacity and grid resilience; tariffs and the Scheme of Control agreements must fit; the future role of local plants and jobs.

2035年停止新登記燃油私家車（包括混能）：進取還是脫離現實？

支持的理據

電動車已佔新登記逾七成；清晰時間表給市場和車主明確預期；快充網絡同步擴展。

質疑的理據

舊樓和村屋充電設施不足；中低收入車主的轉換成本；二手車市場和維修業的過渡。

Ending new fuel-car registrations by 2035: bold or unrealistic?

Case for

EVs are already over 70 per cent of new registrations; a clear date gives certainty; fast charging expands in step.

Case against

Old buildings and village houses lack charging; costs for lower-income owners; transition for the used-car and repair trades.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持減碳和零廢目標，把重點放在「落實工具」。題目若涉收費，要承認政府已擱置都市固體廢物收費，建議可放在強化回收網絡、生產者責任計劃和獎勵式措施（積分、回贈），而非直接重推收費。紅線：不要把輸電供港寫成「依賴內地」的負面論述——談「多元來源、備用容量、電網韌性」；也不要以環保為由全盤反對北都發展，可主張以生態補償和保護區平衡。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support the decarbonisation and zero-landfill goals and focus on delivery tools. If charging comes up, acknowledge that MSW charging has been shelved and put your weight on recycling networks, producer responsibility and reward schemes rather than reviving the charge. Red lines: do not frame imported power as “dependence on the Mainland” — talk about diverse sources, reserve capacity and grid resilience; and do not oppose Northern Metropolis development on environmental grounds wholesale — argue for ecological compensation and protected areas.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

台北

2000年實施「垃圾費隨袋徵收」：市民須購買專用垃圾袋，資源回收免費；配合「垃圾不落地」，由垃圾車定時定點收集。

人均垃圾量大幅下降、回收率上升，是亞洲按量收費的成功例子。

✓ 可借鑑

收費與「免費回收」同步推出，給市民清晰替代途徑；長時間宣傳和準備。

✗ 不可照搬

台北以街道和垃圾車定點收集為基礎，按袋收費容易執行；香港高樓以垃圾房集中收集，難以追溯到住戶，這正是香港收費計劃擱置的主因之一。

Taipei

Pay-as-you-throw by bag since 2000: residents buy official bags while recycling is free; combined with no-street-bins collection by scheduled trucks.

Per-capita waste fell sharply and recycling rose: Asia’s best-known pay-as-you-throw success.

✓ Transferable

Launch charging together with free recycling so people have a clear alternative; prepare and publicise for a long time.

✗ Not transferable

Taipei’s kerbside truck collection makes bag charging easy to enforce; Hong Kong’s refuse rooms in high-rises make households untraceable — a main reason its scheme was shelved.

韓國

1995年起垃圾按量收費（指定垃圾袋）；2013年全國推行廚餘按重量收費，住宅大廈以RFID智能廚餘桶按住戶計重收費。

廚餘回收率極高，大部分轉化為飼料、堆肥或沼氣。

✓ 可借鑑

RFID智能廚餘桶：香港公屋已推「一座一桶」，技術基礎已具備，下一步可研究以積分獎勵（而非收費）鼓勵分類。

✗ 不可照搬

韓國在多年收費制度和法律強制分類下推行；香港廚餘回收仍屬自願，突然引入按重量收費會重演都市固體廢物收費的阻力。

South Korea

Volume-based waste fees with designated bags since 1995; nationwide weight-based food waste charging since 2013, with RFID smart bins in apartment blocks billing each household by weight.

Food waste recycling is very high, mostly turned into feed, compost or biogas.

✓ Transferable

RFID bins: Hong Kong’s public estates already have one bin per block, so the technology is there; rewarding sorting with points rather than fees could come next.

✗ Not transferable

Korea built this on years of charging and mandatory sorting; Hong Kong’s food waste recycling is voluntary, and sudden weight charging would repeat the resistance to MSW charging.

新加坡

碳稅由每公噸5坡元（2019年）升至25坡元（2024–25年），2026年起45坡元；擁車證配額限制車輛增長；垃圾以焚化為主，實馬高島是唯一堆填區。

碳價訊號清晰；土地限制令其必須以焚化和回收取代堆填。

✓ 可借鑑

清晰、分階段上調的碳價路線圖給企業預期：香港可研究針對大型排放源的碳定價，並與國家碳市場銜接。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡碳稅主要向大型工業排放源徵收；香港排放以發電為主，碳價會直接轉嫁電價，與「穩定電價」的施政目標衝突。

Singapore

A carbon tax rising from S$5 a tonne (2019) to S$25 (2024–25) and S$45 from 2026; vehicle quotas limit car growth; waste is mostly incinerated, with Semakau its only landfill.

A clear carbon price signal; land scarcity forces incineration and recycling over landfill.

✓ Transferable

A clear, stepped carbon price path gives business certainty; Hong Kong could study carbon pricing for large emitters linked to the national market.

✗ Not transferable

Singapore taxes large industrial emitters; Hong Kong’s emissions come mainly from power, so a carbon price would pass straight into tariffs, clashing with the goal of stable prices.

深圳／廣東

深圳公交和的士基本全面電動化；廣東的核電、水電等清潔能源是區域能源合作的基礎；深圳2020年起實施生活垃圾強制分類。

電動化速度快；強制分類提升回收率。

✓ 可借鑑

由公共交通和商用車先行電動化——與§412公共小巴電動化工作小組一致；區域能源合作支持零碳目標。

✗ 不可照搬

深圳由政府主導的公交集團統一採購電動巴士；香港專營巴士和小巴屬私營，須以資助和牌照條件推動，時間表不能照搬。

Shenzhen and Guangdong

Shenzhen’s buses and taxis are almost fully electric; Guangdong’s nuclear and hydro power underpin regional energy co-operation; Shenzhen has mandated household waste sorting since 2020.

Rapid electrification and higher recycling through mandatory sorting.

✓ Transferable

Electrify public transport and commercial fleets first, as §412 does for minibuses; regional energy co-operation supports zero-carbon targets.

✗ Not transferable

Shenzhen’s state bus groups bought EVs centrally; Hong Kong’s buses and minibuses are private and must be moved by subsidies and licence conditions, so the timetable cannot be copied.

政策目的

Policy objective

在二○五○年前達致碳中和以對接國家「雙碳」目標，二○三五年前達致零廢堆填；同時把「安全」與「韌性」由工程概念提升為城市規劃原則，應對極端天氣與重大事故。

To achieve carbon neutrality before 2050 in line with the national dual carbon goals and zero landfill by 2035, and to raise safety and resilience from an engineering concern to a principle of urban planning in the face of extreme weather and major incidents.

核心矛盾

Core tension

減廢與減碳的成本是即時、可見、落在個人身上的；效益是遲來、分散、落在整體身上的。都市固體廢物收費的擱置證明了這個不對稱：政策在技術上可行，在政治上不可行。政府因此轉向基建與誘因，但沒有價格訊號，行為改變的速度必然慢。

The cost of cutting waste and carbon is immediate, visible and personal; the benefit is delayed, diffuse and collective. The shelving of municipal solid waste charging proved this asymmetry: technically workable, politically unavailable. The Government has turned instead to infrastructure and incentives, but without a price signal behaviour changes only slowly.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

人均溫室氣體排放降至2024年的4.41公噸。都市固體廢物棄置量連續四年下降（2025年平均每日10 326公噸，較2021年減9.1%），回收率由28%升至34%。I·PARK1今年全面投入運作（每日處理3 000公噸）。都市固體廢物收費計劃已於2025年宣布無限期擱置，本年施政報告沒有重提。電動車逾18萬輛，新登記私家車每10輛逾7輛為電動車；充電車位逾17萬個。

Current position

Per-capita greenhouse gas emissions fell to 4.41 tonnes in 2024. Municipal solid waste disposal has fallen four years running (10 326 tonnes a day in 2025, 9.1 per cent below 2021) and the recovery rate has risen from 28 to 34 per cent. I·PARK1 is fully operational this year (3 000 tonnes a day). The MSW charging scheme was shelved indefinitely in 2025 and is not revived this year. There are over 180 000 electric vehicles, more than seven in ten new private cars are electric, and over 170 000 parking spaces have chargers.

長期結構

每日棄置於堆填區的都市固體廢物中，約三成是廚餘。I·PARK 1 於二○二五年底落成，I·PARK 2 將向立法會申請撥款。由二○二六／二七年度起啟動五年計劃優化回收設施，包括把「綠在區區」逐步轉型為全天候自助回收設施、推動公共屋邨「一座一桶」智能廚餘回收桶。環保園的現代化紙漿生產設施二○二六年全面運作，全港首間大型電動車電池回收設施預計二○二六年上半年啟用。新登記私家車中每十輛約有七輛為電動車。粵港已於二○二五年一月成立「無廢灣區」建設專題小組。

Long-run structure

About 30 per cent of municipal solid waste sent to landfill each day is food waste. I·PARK 1 was completed at the end of 2025 and funding for I·PARK 2 will be sought from the Legislative Council. A five-year programme beginning in 2026-27 upgrades recycling facilities, converting GREEN@COMMUNITY into round-the-clock self-service points and extending smart food waste bins to one per block in public housing estates. The modern pulp production facility at EcoPark reaches full operation in 2026 and the first large-scale electric vehicle battery recycling facility is expected in the first half of 2026. About seven in ten newly registered private cars are electric. Guangdong and Hong Kong set up a Zero Waste Bay Area task group in January 2025.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1缺乏價格訊號

垃圾徵費擱置後，棄置行為沒有任何邊際成本，回收完全依賴自願。基建再方便，也難以逆轉「棄置免費、回收要花時間」的成本結構。

1No price signal

With charging shelved, disposal carries no marginal cost and recycling depends entirely on goodwill. However convenient the facilities, they cannot reverse a cost structure in which disposal is free and recycling takes time.

2減碳成本的分配未有共識

發電燃料組合轉型的成本最終反映於電費，而電費對低收入家庭的影響最大；在沒有配套補貼的討論之前，任何加快減碳的建議都會卡在分配問題上。

2No agreement on who pays for decarbonisation

The cost of changing the generation fuel mix ultimately appears in tariffs, which weigh most on low-income households; without a parallel discussion of relief, proposals to accelerate stall on distribution.

3韌性長期以工程標準處理

防洪、斜坡、建築物料與工地安全分屬不同部門與條例，宏福苑火災顯示問題出在銜接處：物料規管、工程監督、招標制度與消防檢查各自運作，沒有一方對整體結果負責。

3Resilience treated as an engineering standard

Flood control, slopes, building materials and site safety sit with different departments and ordinances. The Wang Fuk Court fire showed the failure was at the joins: material regulation, works supervision, tendering and fire inspection each operated separately with no one answerable for the whole.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1財政影響：轉廢為能設施與防災基建屬大額資本開支，回報以避免的損失計算，難以在傳統的成本效益框架中證明，需要以風險為本的論證方式。

1Fiscal: waste-to-energy and disaster-prevention facilities are large capital items whose return is measured in losses avoided, which is hard to demonstrate in a conventional cost-benefit frame and calls for a risk-based case.

2社會影響：環保措施的成本落在個人日常行為上，效益落在整體與未來世代，這種不對稱使得公眾支持在民意調查中很高、在實際執行中很低。

2Social: the cost of environmental measures falls on daily individual behaviour while the benefit accrues collectively and to future generations; this asymmetry makes public support high in surveys and low in practice.

3管治影響：宏福苑事件後，公眾對「制度銜接」的要求高於對「個別部門表現」的要求，這改變了問責的性質，也是部門首長責任制的直接由來。

3Governance: after Wang Fuk Court the public asks about how the system joins up rather than how individual departments perform, changing the nature of accountability and giving rise directly to the accountability system for heads of departments.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

成本前置比事後補救便宜得多：防洪與防災基建的投入相對於一次重大災害的直接損失與重建成本，比例懸殊。

站反方？這樣駁

「前置比補救便宜」只在投資用得其所時成立；若工程按舊標準設計、選址錯誤，前置投資同樣會浪費。

FOR 1

Paying early is far cheaper than paying later: investment in flood and disaster prevention is small against the direct loss and reconstruction cost of a single major event.

Counter

Prevention is cheaper only if the money is well spent; works designed to old standards or in the wrong places waste it too.

支持 2

綠色轉型同時是產業機會：可持續航空燃料、氫能、電池回收與綠色建築都是新的產業鏈，把環保開支轉化為經濟活動。

站反方？這樣駁

綠色產業機會是否落在香港，取決於土地、人才和市場；可持續航空燃料生產基地設在東莞，正說明產業鏈不一定在本地。

FOR 2

The green transition is also an industrial opportunity: sustainable aviation fuel, hydrogen, battery recycling and green building are new supply chains that turn environmental spending into economic activity.

Counter

Whether green industries land in Hong Kong depends on land, talent and markets; the SAF plant is in Dongguan, showing the chain need not be local.

支持 3

國家「雙碳」目標已經給定，香港作為高度發達的城市不可能只享受成果而不承擔份額，這是政治上不可迴避的論據。

站反方？這樣駁

承擔國家目標是應有之義，但路徑選擇（速度、成本分配）仍可討論；「政治上不可迴避」不等於任何成本都合理。

FOR 3

The national dual carbon goals are settled, and a highly developed city cannot take the benefit without carrying a share; politically this argument cannot be avoided.

Counter

Sharing national goals is right, but the path — pace and who pays — is still open; being politically unavoidable does not make every cost reasonable.

反對／制約 1

減碳成本轉嫁至電費，對低收入家庭的影響最直接，而他們的碳足印本來就最低，屬於逆向分配。

站支持方？這樣駁

逆向分配可以用補貼和累進電價設計處理（例如電費紓緩、能源效益資助），而不是放慢減碳。

AGAINST 1

Passing decarbonisation costs into tariffs hits low-income households hardest, though their carbon footprint is already the smallest: a regressive outcome.

Counter

Regressive effects can be handled by design — targeted relief, tiered tariffs, efficiency grants — rather than by slowing decarbonisation.

反對／制約 2

香港排放量佔全球極小比例，單方面加快減碳對全球氣候的邊際影響有限，卻要付出實質的本地成本。

站支持方？這樣駁

香港排放量小，但作為國際城市和綠色金融中心，減碳表現直接影響融資、營商和城市競爭力；空氣質素改善亦是本地即時得益。

AGAINST 2

Hong Kong’s emissions are a very small share of the global total, so unilateral acceleration has limited marginal effect on the climate while imposing real local costs.

Counter

Hong Kong’s emissions are small, but as an international and green-finance centre its performance affects financing, business and competitiveness, and cleaner air is an immediate local gain.

反對／制約 3

減廢的行為改變必須以價格為基礎，而價格工具已經證明在政治上不可行，因此目前的方案在效果上難以達標。

站支持方？這樣駁

價格工具不是唯一工具：生產者責任、回收便利、獎勵積分、智能廚餘桶都能改變行為；先建設施和習慣，再談價格，順序更可行。

AGAINST 3

Behavioural change in waste requires a price, and the price instrument has proved politically unavailable, so the present approach is unlikely to reach the target.

Counter

Price is not the only lever: producer responsibility, convenient recycling, reward points and smart food-waste bins also change behaviour; facilities and habits first, pricing later, is a more workable sequence.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊工具：公開各區回收率與棄置量

以地區為單位公開數據，形成同儕比較的壓力，成本極低而能推動屋苑與物管主動改善。

Information: publish district-level rates

Publishing recovery and disposal figures by district creates peer comparison at very low cost and prompts estates and managing agents to act.

基建工具：全天候自助回收與一座一桶

把回收由「要特地去」變成「順路就做」，是在沒有價格訊號下唯一可靠的行為改變手段。

Facilities: round-the-clock and one bin per block

Turning recycling from a special trip into something done on the way is the only reliable behavioural lever in the absence of a price signal.

誘因工具：生產者責任與回贈

把成本內部化到生產與零售端，並以回贈鼓勵回收，避免直接向市民收費的政治阻力。

Incentives: producer responsibility and rebates

Internalise cost at the producer and retailer end and reward returns, avoiding the political resistance of charging households directly.

規管工具：建築物料與工地安全標準

把阻燃標準、抽樣檢測與第三方專業人士要求寫入法例，是宏福苑之後政治阻力最低而效果最直接的一級。

Regulation: materials and site safety standards

Writing flame-retardant standards, sampling and third-party professional requirements into law is, after Wang Fuk Court, the rung with the least resistance and the most direct effect.

價格工具：都市固體廢物收費

技術上仍然有效，但須先完成回收配套、選定先行對象並設定長適應期，否則重蹈覆轍。

Price: municipal solid waste charging

Still effective in principle, but only after the recycling network is complete, an initial cohort is chosen and a long adjustment period is set, or the earlier failure repeats.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

沒有價格訊號，回收率的提升會在達到某一水平後停滯。

化解

先以智能廚餘桶與自助回收設施把基建鋪滿並累積數據，再以特定樓宇類型（例如新建屋苑或單一業權物業）試行按量收費，用實證而非預測說服公眾。

Challenge 1

Without a price signal, recovery rates will plateau.

Mitigation

First complete the network of smart food waste bins and self-service points and accumulate data, then pilot volume-based charging in defined building types such as newly completed estates or single-ownership properties, persuading the public with evidence rather than projection.

挑戰 2

提高能源標準與轉換燃料組合會推高電費，引發民生反彈。

化解

把電費影響分拆公布並設立針對低收入住戶的定額補貼，使減碳成本與扶助措施同時出台；同時以節能改造資助降低住戶的實際用電量，抵銷單位電價的上升。

Challenge 2

Raising energy standards and changing the fuel mix pushes tariffs up and provokes a cost-of-living backlash.

Mitigation

Publish the tariff impact separately and pair it with a flat subsidy for low-income households, so the cost and the relief arrive together, while retrofit funding lowers actual consumption to offset the higher unit price.

挑戰 3

韌性涉及多個部門與條例，責任分散導致無人對整體結果負責。

化解

以跨部門的常設機制統一風險評估與早期預警，並透過部門首長責任制把「銜接處的失誤」明確歸屬；在工程項目中引入獨立於承建商的第三方專業人士，堵塞監督真空。

Challenge 3

Resilience spans departments and ordinances, so responsibility is diffuse and no one answers for the whole.

Mitigation

Unify risk assessment and early warning through a standing cross-departmental mechanism, use the accountability system for heads of departments to assign failures at the joins, and require a third-party professional independent of the contractor to close the supervision gap.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

物業管理與業主立案法團

談可執行性：回收設施的位置、清運頻次與費用分擔要具體，法團最怕的是責任落在自己身上而資源沒有到位。

Managing agents and owners’ corporations

Speak of what can actually be done: where facilities go, how often they are cleared and who pays. Their fear is responsibility without resources.

電力公司

談投資回報與規管協議：燃料組合轉型涉及長期資本投入，准許回報與燃料調整條款的安排決定他們的配合程度。

Power companies

Speak of returns and the scheme of control: changing the fuel mix requires long-term capital, and the permitted return and fuel clause arrangements determine their co-operation.

建造業界

談公平競爭：加強物料規管與第三方監督會提高成本，但同時淘汰以低價違規投標的對手，對守規的承建商是保護而非負擔。

The construction industry

Speak of a level field: tighter material regulation and third-party supervision raise costs but remove competitors who win by cutting corners, protecting compliant contractors rather than burdening them.

基層住戶

談實質支出而非環保理念：電費補貼、節能改造與免費回收設施要先講清楚，環保論述才聽得進去。

Lower-income households

Speak of actual outgoings before principle: tariff relief, retrofit support and free recycling facilities must be set out first for the environmental case to land.

在缺乏價格訊號的情況下，行為改變只能依靠把回收變成比棄置更方便的選項。

Without a price signal, behavioural change depends on making recycling more convenient than disposal.

防災投入的回報以避免的損失計算，因此應以風險為本而非傳統成本效益方式論證。

The return on disaster prevention is measured in losses avoided, so the case should be made on a risk basis rather than by conventional cost-benefit analysis.

制度的失誤多數出現在部門與部門的銜接處，而非任何單一部門之內。

Systemic failures usually occur where departments meet, not within any single department.

Tourism, Mega Events and Culture-Sports-Tourism Integration

一句話方向

旅遊政策由「數人頭」轉向「提升增加值和留港時間」：旅遊業增加值目標由862億元增至2030年1,260億元，非內地過夜旅客比例增至40%。手段是「盛事＋旅遊」、「一程多站」、票尾經濟和遊艇經濟；並首次在熱門郊野景點試行網上預約和人數上限——承認「承載力」是旅遊政策的一部分。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府應在熱門郊野景點實施網上預約、人數上限甚至收費，以保護生態和提升遊覽體驗。你是否同意？試分析現況，提出建議，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說行山團體支持。

出處：五年規劃第二篇第十章第三節（FYP 2.67–2.70、專欄23–24）；施政報告第七章第六節（§339–361）及第七節「支持本地經濟」（§362–371）；主要指標14

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Tourism policy is moving from counting heads to raising value added and length of stay: tourism value added to rise from $86.2 billion to $126 billion by 2030, and non-Mainland visitors to reach 40 per cent of overnight arrivals. The tools are “mega events + tourism”, multi-destination travel, the ticket-stub economy and the yacht economy — and, for the first time, online booking and visitor caps at a popular country-park site, admitting that carrying capacity is part of tourism policy.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say the Government should introduce online booking, visitor caps and even charges at popular country-park sites to protect ecology and improve the experience. Do you agree? Analyse the situation, make proposals, analyse the challenges and responses, and persuade hiking groups to support them.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府定位香港為「亞洲盛事之都」，以「＋旅遊」模式把文化、體育、金融、生態都變成旅遊產品；以恆常撥款支持旅發局；在熱門自然景點開始接受「限流」，但未涉及入場收費。對本地經濟以支援中小企轉型和拆牆鬆綁為主，本年沒有推出消費券一類的普遍補貼。

Government stance

The Government brands Hong Kong the Events Capital of Asia and turns culture, sport, finance and ecology into tourism products; it gives the HKTB recurrent funding; it now accepts visitor limits at natural hotspots but not entry charges. For the local economy it prefers SME upgrading and deregulation, with no general subsidy such as consumption vouchers this year.

熱門郊野景點預約、限額甚至收費：保育，還是限制市民親近自然？

支持的理據

熱門地點人流超出承載力，帶來安全和生態風險；預約制平衡保育與體驗；長遠可研究小額收費以支付管理成本。

質疑的理據

郊野公園是市民的公共空間，限額或收費影響基層使用；網上預約對長者不友善；遠郊執法人手有限。

Booking, caps or charges at country-park hotspots: conservation or keeping people out?

Case for

Crowds exceed carrying capacity and create safety and ecological risks; booking balances conservation and experience; small charges could later fund management.

Case against

Country parks are public space and caps or fees hit low-income users; online booking disadvantages the elderly; enforcement in remote areas is thin.

盛事經濟：帶動消費，還是短暫熱鬧？

支持的理據

盛事吸引過夜旅客、提升城市品牌；票尾優惠把消費帶到社區；恆常撥款令旅發局可長遠部署。

質疑的理據

盛事經濟效益評估不透明；居民承受交通和噪音影響；以非本地藝人為主的目標可能擠壓本地藝人的演出機會。

Mega events: lasting spending or a passing buzz?

Case for

Events draw overnight visitors and lift the city brand; ticket-stub offers spread spending; recurrent funding allows long-term planning.

Case against

Economic impact assessments are opaque; residents bear traffic and noise; a target for non-local headliners may crowd out local performers.

「一程多站」：香港是門戶，還是過境站？

支持的理據

海外旅客經港入內地的比例上升，香港憑航空網絡和國際化服務成為「南大門」，可帶動酒店和零售。

質疑的理據

若旅客只轉機或短留，消費有限；內地放寬外國旅客入境，亦可能令旅客直接飛往內地城市。

Multi-destination travel: gateway or transit stop?

Case for

More overseas visitors enter the Mainland through Hong Kong, whose air links and international services make it the southern gateway, supporting hotels and retail.

Case against

If visitors only connect or stay briefly, spending is limited; Mainland visa relaxation may also let visitors fly direct to Mainland cities.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持「提升增加值、盛事＋旅遊」方向；可主張以數據評估盛事成效、以承載力管理熱門景點。收費議題可取「先預約、後研究小額收費並豁免長者和學生」。紅線：不要把內地旅客寫成負面「問題」，也不要提出「限制內地旅客」一類建議——題目問承載力時，應談時間和地點分流，而非旅客來源。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support value added and events-led tourism; argue for data-based evaluation of events and capacity management at hotspots. On charging, a good line is booking first, then study small fees with exemptions for elders and students. Red line: never frame Mainland visitors as a problem or propose limiting them — on capacity, talk about spreading visitors by time and place, not by origin.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

2010年起兩個綜合度假村帶動會展與旅遊；以獨家演出安排（例如2024年國際巨星的東南亞站只在新加坡舉行）吸引區內旅客；旅遊局以「優質旅遊」為目標，重消費而非人數。

盛事帶動酒店、零售和航空；但獨家安排引起鄰國不滿。

✓ 可借鑑

以數據評估盛事回報（酒店入住、消費）並把「國際旅客比例」寫進資助條件——與§351恆常撥款和啟德60%非本地藝人目標相配合。

✗ 不可照搬

綜合度假村以賭場收入支撐非博彩設施；香港不設賭場，盛事資助靠公帑和企業贊助，財政結構完全不同。

Singapore

Two integrated resorts since 2010 drive MICE and tourism; exclusive concert arrangements (such as a global star’s 2024 Southeast Asian leg held only in Singapore) draw regional visitors; the tourism board targets quality tourism — spending over headcount.

Events lift hotels, retail and aviation, though exclusivity annoyed neighbours.

✓ Transferable

Evaluate events with data (hotel stays, spending) and tie funding to the share of international visitors — consistent with recurrent funding in §351 and the 60 per cent non-local artist target.

✗ Not transferable

The resorts cross-subsidise from casinos; Hong Kong has none, so events rely on public money and sponsorship — a different financial base.

日本

應對過度旅遊：富士山登山路線設入山費和每日人數上限（2025年入山費提高至4 000日圓）；京都在熱門區域限制私人通道並以旅客專用巴士分流。

高峰人流有所緩和，收費收入用於管理和安全。

✓ 可借鑑

「預約＋人數上限＋小額收費」三件套：§351(iv)已試行預約和上限，下一步可研究收費支付管理和保育成本。

✗ 不可照搬

富士山屬國家公園登山路線、以旅客為主；香港郊野公園主要服務本地市民日常康樂，收費涉及公共空間使用權，政治敏感度高得多。

Japan

Tackling overtourism: Mount Fuji trails have an entry fee and daily caps (raised to ¥4,000 in 2025); Kyoto restricts access to private lanes in hotspots and runs tourist-only buses to spread demand.

Peak crowding eased and fees fund management and safety.

✓ Transferable

Booking, caps and small fees as a package: §351(iv) already pilots the first two; charges to fund management could come next.

✗ Not transferable

Fuji is a visitor-dominated national park trail; Hong Kong’s country parks serve residents’ everyday recreation, so charging touches rights to public space and is far more sensitive.

澳門特區

「1＋4」經濟適度多元發展（綜合旅遊休閒，加上大健康、現代金融、高新技術、會展商貿及文化體育）；2022年修訂博彩法，要求承批公司投資非博彩項目；以盛事和「旅遊＋」把旅客帶到社區。

非博彩收入比例上升，但經濟仍高度依賴博彩。

✓ 可借鑑

把旅客導入社區小店：澳門以盛事帶客到社區的做法，與香港「票尾經濟」「由場館輻射至社區」一致。

✗ 不可照搬

澳門以博彩稅支撐財政和盛事；香港經濟多元、不以博彩為財源，資助來源與規模不同。

Macao SAR

The “1+4” diversification strategy — integrated tourism and leisure plus health, modern finance, high technology, and conventions, trade, culture and sport; a 2022 gaming law revision requires concessionaires to invest in non-gaming projects; events and “tourism plus” bring visitors into neighbourhoods.

Non-gaming revenue has grown, but the economy still depends heavily on gaming.

✓ Transferable

Bringing visitors to neighbourhood shops matches Hong Kong’s ticket-stub economy and venue-to-community spending chain.

✗ Not transferable

Macao funds events from gaming taxes; Hong Kong’s diversified economy has no such source, so funding and scale differ.

韓國

以韓流內容（電影、音樂、劇集）帶動「內容旅遊」，推廣拍攝地點；以電子旅行許可便利入境。

拍攝地成為旅遊點，帶動首爾以外地區旅遊。

✓ 可借鑑

「電影＋旅遊」——與§341–342港產片取景路線、境外電影拍攝支援組一致，可用本地電影IP帶動社區旅遊。

✗ 不可照搬

韓流影響力源自長期產業生態和龐大內需市場；香港電影業規模縮小，不能期望單靠旅遊推廣重振，要與電影資助改革並行。

South Korea

Hallyu content — film, music, drama — drives “content tourism” to filming locations; electronic travel authorisation eases entry.

Filming sites became attractions and spread tourism beyond Seoul.

✓ Transferable

Film plus tourism matches the routes built on Hong Kong films and the new support unit for visiting productions (§341–342); local film IP can bring visitors into districts.

✗ Not transferable

Hallyu rests on a long-built industry and a big home market; Hong Kong film has shrunk and cannot be revived by tourism promotion alone — it needs funding reform alongside.

政策目的

Policy objective

由追求旅客人次，轉為追求過夜比例、人均消費與重訪率；並以文化、體育、旅遊三者協同，把香港由「購物中轉站」重新定位為「體驗目的地」。

To shift from visitor arrivals to overnight share, per-capita spending and repeat visits, and through the integration of culture, sports and tourism to reposition Hong Kong from a shopping stopover to an experience destination.

核心矛盾

Core tension

人次已經回來，錢沒有回來。旅客結構偏向即日往返與低消費，而本地居民同時北上消費，零售與餐飲兩頭受壓。政府能做的是盛事與產品，但決定消費的是匯率、內地經濟與比價心理，這三項都不在政府手上。

Arrivals have returned; spending has not. The visitor mix leans towards same-day, low-spending trips while residents themselves spend across the boundary, squeezing retail and catering from both sides. What the Government controls is events and products; what determines spending is the exchange rate, the Mainland economy and price comparison, none of which it controls.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

2025年訪港旅客4 990萬人次，按年增長12%；預計2026年入境旅遊相關總消費約2,381億元。經香港前往內地的海外旅客比例2026年上半年逾兩成。啟德體育園至今舉辦逾170場盛事、累計逾260萬觀眾人次，2025年主場館票務銷量和收入均為亞洲第一。本地零售和餐飲面對市民消費模式轉變（包括北上消費），政府以「支援中小企十措施」協助轉型。

Current position

Visitor arrivals reached 49.9 million in 2025, up 12 per cent; inbound tourism spending is expected to be about $238.1 billion in 2026. Over a fifth of overseas visitors in the first half of 2026 went on to the Mainland via Hong Kong. Kai Tak Sports Park has hosted over 170 events with 2.6 million attendees, and its main stadium led Asia in ticket sales and revenue in 2025. Local retail and catering face changing consumption patterns, including residents spending across the boundary, and ten SME measures aim to help them adapt.

長期結構

二○二五年訪港旅客近五千萬人次，按年增長百分之十二，其中非內地旅客增長百分之十五；訪港郵輪一百八十九航次，按年增加百分之二十六。會展旅客二百五十一萬人次，其中一百四十三萬為過夜旅客，帶來約一百零七億元消費。啟德體育園開幕未滿一年，主場館門票銷量達亞洲第一、全球第三。二○二五年上半年約四分之一過夜旅客表示來港是為了體驗自然、文化與地道生活。中東旅客平均消費比一般旅客高一倍，二○二八年全球穆斯林旅客預計達 2.5 億人。

Long-run structure

Visitor arrivals reached almost 50 million in 2025, up 12 per cent year on year, with non-Mainland visitors up 15 per cent; 189 cruise ship calls were recorded, up 26 per cent. MICE visitors numbered 2.51 million, of whom 1.43 million stayed overnight, generating some $10.7 billion in spending. Within a year of opening, ticket sales at the Kai Tak Sports Park main stadium ranked first in Asia and third worldwide. In the first half of 2025 about a quarter of overnight visitors cited nature, culture and local experience as their reason for coming. Middle Eastern visitors spend about twice the average, and global Muslim travellers are projected to reach 250 million by 2028.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1人均消費下跌

旅客人次回升而消費未同步，原因是即日往返比例高、比價心理強、匯率使香港相對昂貴。以人次作為政策目標會掩蓋這個結構問題。

1Falling per-capita spending

Arrivals have recovered but spending has not, because same-day trips dominate, visitors compare prices closely, and the exchange rate makes Hong Kong relatively expensive. Using arrivals as the policy target conceals this structural problem.

2產品同質與供給僵化

景點以主題樂園與購物為主，新產品開發受土地、牌照與跨部門審批限制，一個新的郊野體驗路線可能涉及漁護、地政、食環、消防四個部門。

2Undifferentiated products and rigid supply

Attractions centre on theme parks and shopping; new products are constrained by land, licensing and cross-departmental approval, so one countryside trail may involve four departments.

3承載力與民生的衝突

旅遊旺區的交通、街道與商舖結構被旅客需求重塑，居民的日常購物與通勤成本上升，而收益歸於業主與少數行業，形成得益與代價的錯配。

3Carrying capacity against daily life

In busy districts traffic, streets and shop mix are reshaped by visitor demand, raising residents’ shopping and commuting costs while gains accrue to landlords and a few trades, creating a mismatch between who benefits and who pays.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1經濟影響：旅遊業就業以基層為主，人次與職位的關係較人均消費更直接；純粹追求高端客源可能提升總收益卻減少職位。

1Economic: tourism employment is concentrated at entry level, and jobs track arrivals more directly than spending; pursuing high-end visitors alone may raise revenue while reducing jobs.

2社會影響：旅客與居民共用的空間越多，摩擦越集中於少數地區，而這些地區往往是基層居住區，政治成本高於經濟收益。

2Social: the more space visitors and residents share, the more friction concentrates in a few districts, often lower-income ones, where the political cost exceeds the economic gain.

3財政影響：盛事的公共投入以場地與保安為主，屬一次性資本與經常開支混合；若無明確的效益評估方法，將難以在財政緊絀時繼續申請撥款。

3Fiscal: public spending on events is mostly venues and security, mixing one-off capital with recurrent cost; without a clear method of assessing returns, further funding will be hard to secure when finances are tight.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

盛事帶來的不只是門票收入，還有國際曝光與城市形象，這些外溢效益無法由私營市場單獨提供，屬典型的公共投資理由。

站反方？這樣駁

「國際曝光」難以量化，正因如此最容易被用來合理化任何開支；若沒有統一評估方法，外溢效益只是口號。

FOR 1

Events bring more than ticket revenue: international exposure and city image are spillovers the private market will not supply alone, which is the classic case for public investment.

Counter

International exposure is hard to measure, which is exactly why it is used to justify anything; without a standard evaluation method, spillovers are a slogan.

支持 2

旅遊業是少數能大量吸納低學歷勞動力的行業，支持旅遊等同支持基層就業，其分配效果優於多數產業政策。

站反方？這樣駁

吸納低學歷勞動力的同時，也可能把他們鎖在低薪、不穩定的崗位；在勞動人口下降時，人手應流向生產力較高的行業。

FOR 2

Tourism is one of the few sectors that absorbs low-qualification labour at scale, so supporting it supports entry-level employment, with better distributional effects than most industrial policy.

Counter

Absorbing low-skilled workers can also lock them into low-paid, unstable jobs; with a shrinking workforce, labour should move to more productive sectors.

支持 3

香港的自然與文化資源長期未被開發，發展生態與文化旅遊的邊際成本低，而且能把旅客由旺區分流至郊野與地區。

站反方？這樣駁

把旅客分流到郊野和地區，等於把承載力壓力分散到最脆弱的生態和社區；邊際成本低只是因為保育和管理成本未被計算。

FOR 3

Hong Kong’s natural and cultural assets are largely undeveloped, so ecological and cultural tourism carry low marginal cost and disperse visitors away from crowded districts.

Counter

Sending visitors to the countryside and districts spreads pressure onto the most fragile ecologies and communities; the marginal cost looks low only because conservation and management costs are left out.

反對／制約 1

盛事的經濟效益長期被高估：主辦方報告多以總消費計算而未扣除替代效應，本地居民的消費只是由其他項目轉移而來。

站支持方？這樣駁

替代效應確實存在，但過夜旅客和非本地觀眾的消費是淨增加；答案是改用統一方法、只計算新增消費，而不是否定盛事。

AGAINST 1

The economic return of events is systematically overstated: organiser reports count gross spending without netting out substitution, as residents merely shift spending from elsewhere.

Counter

Displacement is real, but spending by overnight visitors and non-local audiences is net new; the answer is a standard method counting only additional spending, not rejecting events.

反對／制約 2

旅遊業就業質素低、上流機會有限，以公帑扶持等同鎖定一批勞動力於低薪行業，長遠不利產業升級。

站支持方？這樣駁

旅遊業同樣有專業化路徑（專項專牌導遊、盛事管理、酒店管理）；政策可以把資助與培訓和薪酬提升掛鈎。

AGAINST 2

Tourism jobs are low quality with limited mobility; public support locks labour into low-wage work and works against upgrading in the long run.

Counter

Tourism has professional pathways too — specialist guides, event management, hotel management — and support can be tied to training and better pay.

反對／制約 3

承載力壓力落在居民身上而收益落在業主身上，若不同步處理租金與街道管理，旅遊越旺，居民反感越深。

站支持方？這樣駁

承載力問題正是預約制、人流分流和「票尾經濟」要處理的：把消費帶到社區小店，令居民也分享收益。

AGAINST 3

Residents bear the congestion while landlords take the gain; unless rents and street management are addressed at the same time, the busier tourism becomes, the more residents resent it.

Counter

Capacity is exactly what booking, crowd dispersal and the ticket-stub economy address, bringing spending to neighbourhood shops so residents share the gains.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊工具：實時人流發布與分流導引

以實時人流數據引導旅客避開高峰地點與時段，成本極低且不涉限制自由，是承載力問題的第一級處理方式。

Information: real-time crowd data

Publish real-time crowding to steer visitors away from peak places and times: very low cost, no restriction of movement, and the first rung for carrying-capacity problems.

產品工具：特色旅遊與一程多站

發展城市漫步、賽馬、美食、郵輪、跳島遊，並與內地省市合作推「一程多站」，以新供給疏導既有需求。

Product: distinctive and multi-destination itineraries

Develop city walks, horse racing, culinary, cruise and island-hopping products and work with Mainland provinces on multi-destination itineraries, using new supply to relieve existing demand.

行政工具：拆牆鬆綁

精簡臨時公眾娛樂場所牌照、露天餐飲與郊野設施的審批流程，把跨部門審批改為單一窗口與限期回覆。

Administrative: streamlining

Simplify licensing for temporary places of public entertainment, outdoor dining and countryside facilities, replacing multi-department approval with a single window and response deadlines.

財政工具：多年期盛事基金

設立多年期承諾的盛事申辦與培育基金，使香港有能力參與三至五年周期的國際賽事爭辦。

Fiscal: a multi-year events fund

Establish a multi-year fund for bidding and incubating events so Hong Kong can compete in international bidding cycles of three to five years.

規管工具：住宿稅或旺區收費

屬最重的一級，需要證明承載力已達臨界並設計收益回饋機制，在零售疲弱期推行的政治成本極高。

Regulatory: accommodation levy or district charge

The heaviest rung, requiring evidence that capacity is at its limit and a hypothecation design; the political cost while retail is weak is very high.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

盛事效益缺乏公認的計算方法，撥款時難以證明，事後亦難以檢討。

化解

事前訂立以過夜旅客人數、酒店入住率與非本地訂票比例為核心的指標，並委託獨立機構以扣除替代效應的方法評估，把爭論由「值不值得」轉為「達標與否」。

Challenge 1

There is no agreed method of measuring event returns, so funding is hard to justify and impossible to review afterwards.

Mitigation

Set indicators in advance around overnight visitors, hotel occupancy and the share of non-local ticket sales, and commission independent assessment that nets out substitution, turning the argument from whether it was worth it into whether the target was met.

挑戰 2

新旅遊產品涉及多個部門的牌照與用地審批，開發周期長於市場窗口。

化解

以跨部門專責小組或「一站式」審批平台處理旅遊項目，設定法定回覆時限，並容許先以試辦期營運再正式發牌。

Challenge 2

New tourism products need licences and land approvals across departments, and the development cycle outlasts the market window.

Mitigation

Handle tourism projects through a cross-departmental task force or single-window platform with statutory response times, and allow a trial operating period before full licensing.

挑戰 3

旺區居民對旅客的反感一旦形成，任何旅遊政策都會被視為犧牲居民。

化解

在旺區同步推出居民可見的補償措施，例如改善街道管理、增加非旅遊性商舖的租務支援與社區設施，並讓地區組織參與旅遊規劃的前期討論。

Challenge 3

Once resentment forms among residents of busy districts, any tourism policy is read as sacrificing them.

Mitigation

Pair tourism measures with visible benefits in the same districts, improving street management, supporting non-tourist retail tenancies and community facilities, and bringing district bodies into planning at an early stage.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

零售與餐飲業界

談客流結構而非總量：過夜旅客的餐飲消費是即日往返的數倍，分流與延長逗留時間對他們的收益比人次更重要。

Retail and catering

Speak of mix, not volume: overnight visitors spend several times more on food and drink than same-day visitors, so dispersal and longer stays matter more to them than arrivals.

旺區居民與區議員

承認代價並具體回應：交通、街道清潔與商舖結構的改善措施要與旅遊措施同時公布，否則被視為只顧旅客。

Residents and District Council members

Acknowledge the cost and answer it concretely: measures on traffic, street cleanliness and shop mix must be announced alongside tourism measures, or the policy reads as favouring visitors only.

酒店業

談淡旺季而非全年平均：盛事排期若集中於旺季，對他們幫助有限；把盛事安排在淡季才是真正的收益。

Hotels

Speak of seasonality, not annual averages: events scheduled in peak season help them little, whereas off-peak scheduling is where the real gain lies.

財政司司長辦公室

談乘數與可驗證性：任何旅遊撥款都應附退出條件與檢討時點，避免形成無限期的經常補貼。

Financial Secretary’s Office

Speak of multipliers and verifiability: any tourism funding should carry exit conditions and a review point, to avoid becoming an open-ended recurrent subsidy.

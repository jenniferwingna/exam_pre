創新科技與新型工業

Innovation, Technology and New Industrialisation
← 議題庫

一句話方向

目標是把創科由「研發強」變成「產業強」：以「三大創科園區、五大研發機構」為骨架；以新型工業化（智能生產線、菁英企業培育、國家製造業創新中心）補上香港缺了三十年的「製造」環節；以引導基金、港投公司的「耐心資本」和應用場景促進中心，解決中游轉化和「首單買家」的缺口。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，香港應大力推動新型工業化，把「製造」帶回香港。試分析香港發展新型工業化的條件與限制，提出政策建議，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說立法會支持相關撥款。

出處：五年規劃第二篇第九章「國際創新科技中心及國際高端人才集聚高地」（專欄12–18）、第三篇第十二章（專欄26）；施政報告第三章第四節（§102–144）、第五章§194–201；主要指標6–7

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

The aim is to turn research strength into industrial strength: “three I&T parks and five R&D institutions” as the skeleton; new industrialisation — smart production lines, an elite enterprise scheme, a national manufacturing innovation centre — to restore the manufacturing link missing for thirty years; and guidance funds, the HKIC’s patient capital and an application-scenario centre to close the midstream and “first buyer” gaps.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some argue Hong Kong should push hard for new industrialisation and bring manufacturing back. Analyse the conditions and constraints, propose policies, analyse implementation challenges and responses, and persuade the Legislative Council to fund them.

§193–194

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

以上游研發為主→研發＋中試＋製造的完整鏈條

河套聚焦上中游研發與中試，新田科技城提供原型、中試和量產空間；「製造」首次成為政策主角。

Upstream research first→A full chain: research, pilot, manufacturing

The Loop covers upstream and midstream work, San Tin provides space for prototypes, pilots and volume production; manufacturing becomes a protagonist for the first time.

以撥款資助為主→以資本工具帶動

引導基金、港投公司、創投配對：政府出「第一桶金」，要求私人資本跟投。

Grants→Capital tools

Guidance funds, the HKIC and venture matching: government puts in first money and requires private capital to follow.

政府只是「推動者」→政府也是「首個用家」

應用場景促進中心、醫管局醫創融匯辦公室、AI公務，把公營部門變成本地創科產品的第一個市場。

Government as promoter only→Government as first user

The application-scenario centre, the HA’s Technology Convergence Hub and AI in the civil service make the public sector the first market for local technology.

以研發開支量度→以「創新活動總開支」及製造業增加值量度

新指標反映設計、軟件、測試等創新活動，並把製造業比重寫成目標。

Measured by R&D spending→Measured by innovation expenditure and manufacturing value added

The new indicator counts design, software and testing, and makes manufacturing’s share a target.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

「有效市場＋有為政府」：政府願意出資、批地、開規管綠燈，但以母基金、配對投資、市場化營運（新田科技城公司、洪水橋產業園公司）的方式進行，不直接經營企業。答題時把政府定位為「風險分擔者與規則制定者」，比定位為「投資者」更貼近官方語言。

Government stance

“An effective market and a proactive government”: the Government will put up money, land and regulatory green lights, but through funds of funds, matched investment and market-style companies (San Tin Technopole, Hung Shui Kiu industry park), not by running firms. In answers, cast the Government as risk-sharer and rule-setter rather than investor — closer to official language.

引導基金與企業優惠：補市場失靈，還是用公帑揀贏家？

支持的理據

早期硬科技風險高、回報期長，私人資本不願獨自承擔；母基金以少量公帑帶動私人資本，屬補位而非搶位。

質疑的理據

政府缺乏判斷技術路線的能力；補貼個別企業與「公平競爭、簡單低稅」的傳統有張力；失敗時的公帑損失和問責不清。

Guidance funds and incentives: fixing market failure or picking winners?

Case for

Early hard tech is risky and slow to pay back, so private capital holds back; a fund of funds uses a little public money to pull in private capital — filling a gap, not crowding out.

Case against

Governments are poor at choosing technologies; subsidising particular firms sits uneasily with fair competition and simple low taxes; losses and accountability are unclear if bets fail.

「製造」回歸香港：有沒有比較優勢？

支持的理據

高增值、自動化的智能製造不依賴廉價勞工和大片土地；配合大灣區供應鏈，香港可做研發、中試、品牌、檢測認證和高端生產。

質疑的理據

土地、電力和人工成本高；深圳、東莞供應鏈更完整；可能只是示範性生產線，規模有限。

Bringing manufacturing back: is there a comparative advantage?

Case for

Automated high-value manufacturing needs neither cheap labour nor vast land; with GBA supply chains Hong Kong can do R&D, pilots, branding, testing and certification and high-end production.

Case against

Land, power and labour are expensive; Shenzhen and Dongguan have fuller supply chains; production may remain demonstration-scale.

改用「創新活動總開支」：更全面，還是美化數字？

支持的理據

國際指引承認設計、軟件、測試都是創新；只看研發會低估服務型經濟的創新。

質疑的理據

純研發比率只有1.13%，換指標後數字變成1.63%；目標定在「2030年後」達3%，五年內難以問責。

The new innovation indicator: more complete or flattering?

Case for

International guidance counts design, software and testing as innovation; R&D alone understates innovation in a service economy.

Case against

Pure R&D is only 1.13 per cent; the new measure shows 1.63; a 3 per cent target “after 2030” is hard to hold anyone to within five years.

考生取態建議與紅線

取「支持方向，但要求退出機制和成效評估」：引導基金要有退出條款並公開成效；企業優惠要有透明準則和「未達承諾須回撥」條款；應用場景要有公平採購規則。紅線：不要主張回到「積極不干預」全盤否定產業政策——與五年規劃「有為政府」衝突；也不要主張政府直接持股經營企業。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support the direction but demand exit mechanisms and evaluation: guidance funds need exit terms and published results; incentives need transparent criteria and clawback if commitments are missed; public procurement of local tech needs fair rules. Red lines: do not argue for a return to positive non-interventionism that rejects industrial policy — it clashes with the Plan’s proactive government; and do not propose the Government running firms directly.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

台灣地區

工業技術研究院（1973年）作為「研發—衍生企業」平台，衍生出台積電、聯電等企業；新竹科學園區（1980年）以土地、稅務和人才集聚配合。

公營研究機構承擔高風險研發再衍生企業，形成半導體產業集群。

✓ 可借鑑

研發機構應以「衍生企業、技術授權收入」作關鍵績效指標，而非只計論文——可用於香港「五大研發機構」的考核設計。

✗ 不可照搬

台灣當年有大批工程人才回流、製造業基礎和低成本土地；香港製造業早已北移、工程師人才庫小，必須依託大灣區製造能力，而非獨自建立完整產業。

Taiwan region

The Industrial Technology Research Institute (1973) acted as a research-and-spin-off platform, producing TSMC and UMC; Hsinchu Science Park (1980) added land, tax incentives and a talent cluster.

A public institute carried high-risk research and spun off firms, building a semiconductor cluster.

✓ Transferable

R&D institutes should be judged on spin-offs and licensing income, not papers — a model for assessing Hong Kong’s five institutes.

✗ Not transferable

Taiwan had returning engineers, a manufacturing base and cheap land; Hong Kong’s manufacturing moved north long ago and its engineering pool is small, so it must lean on GBA capacity rather than build a whole industry alone.

深圳

研發投入強度約佔GDP 6%，研發活動以企業為主體；以「專精特新」梯度培育中小企，形成完整硬件供應鏈。

科研到量產的週期短，企業願意投入研發。

✓ 可借鑑

「專精特新」式梯度培育正是§129菁英企業培育計劃的參考藍本；答題可強調「企業為研發主體」和政府的角色是配套。

✗ 不可照搬

深圳優勢在龐大製造腹地和工程人才；香港應與深圳錯位——研發、融資、國際標準、知識產權和檢測認證——而非複製其產業規模。

Shenzhen

R&D intensity of about 6 per cent of GDP, overwhelmingly business-led; tiered support for specialised “little giant” SMEs; a complete hardware supply chain.

A short path from lab to volume production and firms willing to invest in R&D.

✓ Transferable

Tiered support for specialised SMEs is the model for §129; stress that firms should lead R&D with government in a supporting role.

✗ Not transferable

Shenzhen’s edge is its manufacturing hinterland and engineers; Hong Kong should complement it — research, finance, international standards, IP, testing — not copy its scale.

新加坡

「研究、創新與企業計劃」（RIE2025）五年約250億坡元；經濟發展局一站式招商，補助按里程碑分期發放；政府投資機構以商業條款投資。

吸引跨國企業設立研發和高端製造基地（半導體、生物醫藥）。

✓ 可借鑑

里程碑式撥款和「單一窗口」：香港的招商優惠政策包可由同一專責團隊把批地、稅務、簽證、資助跟進到底，並按里程碑發放。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡主權基金長期持股並容忍虧損，背後有公積金和財政儲備的制度授權；香港受《基本法》第一百零七條量入為出原則約束，儲備亦承擔聯繫匯率的防衞責任。

Singapore

The RIE2025 plan commits about S$25 billion over five years; the Economic Development Board offers one-stop investment facilitation with milestone-based grants; state investors invest on commercial terms.

Multinationals set up R&D and advanced manufacturing bases in semiconductors and biomedical sciences.

✓ Transferable

Milestone grants and a single window: Hong Kong’s incentive packages could be handled end to end — land, tax, visas, grants — by one team, released against milestones.

✗ Not transferable

Singapore’s sovereign funds hold long-term stakes and tolerate losses, backed by the CPF and reserves; Hong Kong is bound by Article 107 of the Basic Law and its reserves defend the Linked Exchange Rate.

日本

以巨額補貼吸引台積電在熊本設廠，並資助本土先進晶片企業Rapidus。

熊本帶動供應鏈投資和就業，但亦推高當地地價和人手緊張；Rapidus的商業可行性仍受質疑。

✓ 可借鑑

「錨企業」帶動整條供應鏈——香港以優惠政策包吸引「鏈主」企業時，須同時規劃供應商、人才培訓和住屋配套。

✗ 不可照搬

日本以經濟安全為由投入以兆日圓計的補貼；香港財政規模和產業基礎不容許同等投入，應聚焦中試、檢測認證等配套，而非晶圓廠級補貼。

Japan

Large subsidies brought TSMC to Kumamoto, and the state backs the domestic advanced-chip venture Rapidus.

Kumamoto drew supply-chain investment and jobs but pushed up land prices and labour shortages; Rapidus’s commercial viability is still doubted.

✓ Transferable

Anchor firms pull in whole supply chains; when Hong Kong courts anchor firms, it must plan suppliers, training and housing too.

✗ Not transferable

Japan spends trillions of yen on economic-security grounds; Hong Kong cannot match that and should focus on pilots and testing rather than fab-scale subsidies.

政策目的

Policy objective

把香港從單一依賴金融與地產的服務型經濟，轉為「金融＋創科」雙引擎；把大學的科研能力變成留在香港的產業、職位與稅基。國家「十五五」規劃建議已把「國際創新科技中心」列為香港「四中心、一高地」之一。

To shift Hong Kong from a service economy reliant on finance and property to a twin-engine model of finance plus innovation and technology, and to convert the research strength of its universities into industries, jobs and a tax base that stay in Hong Kong. The national 15th Five-Year Plan Proposals name an international innovation and technology centre as one of Hong Kong’s "four centres and one hub".

核心矛盾

Core tension

香港有世界級的上游科研，卻長期缺乏中下游的土地、廠房、工程人才與本地買家，成果多數在深圳或海外落地。政府近年直接出資引導，但這與「簡單低稅、市場主導」的傳統定位存在張力：投得太少沒有作用，投得太多就變成用公帑選贏家。

Hong Kong has world-class upstream research but has long lacked the land, plant, engineering talent and domestic buyers needed midstream and downstream, so results are commercialised in Shenzhen or overseas. The Government has begun to invest directly, which sits uneasily with the tradition of a simple tax system and a market-led economy: too little intervention achieves nothing, too much means picking winners with public money.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

香港有世界級上游科研（五所大學躋身全球百強），但中下游的土地、中試設施、工程人才和本地買家長期不足。2024年純研發開支只佔GDP 1.13%；五年規劃改用涵蓋設計、軟件開發、測試等的「本地創新活動總開支」（1.63%）作主要指標。河套香港園區2025年12月開園，已有逾100家企業及機構進駐；香港已培育約20家獨角獸、約5 200家初創企業。

Current position

Hong Kong has world-class upstream research, with five universities in the global top 100, but has long lacked midstream and downstream land, pilot facilities, engineers and local buyers. Pure R&D spending was only 1.13 per cent of GDP in 2024; the Plan now uses total domestic expenditure on innovation activities, which includes design, software development and testing (1.63 per cent), as its headline indicator. The Loop’s Hong Kong Park opened in December 2025 with over 100 firms and institutions; Hong Kong has about 20 unicorns and some 5 200 start-ups.

長期結構

資源已到位：一百億元「創科產業引導基金」、三十億元「前沿科技研究支援計劃」、三十億元「人工智能資助計劃」；數碼港人工智能超算中心提供 3 000 PFLOPS 算力，全港總算力約 5 000 PFLOPS。空間亦已規劃：新田科技城連同河套香港園區提供 300 公頃創科用地、約 700 萬平方米樓面，相當於 17 個香港科學園。二○二六年將成立香港人工智能研發院與生命健康研發院，「新型工業加速計劃」項目成本門檻由三億元下調至 1.5 億元。

Long-run structure

The money is in place: a $10 billion I&T Industry-Oriented Fund, a $3 billion Frontier Technology Research Support Scheme and a $3 billion Artificial Intelligence Subsidy Scheme. The Cyberport AI Supercomputing Centre provides 3,000 PFLOPS, with total computing power in Hong Kong at some 5,000 PFLOPS. The space is planned: the San Tin Technopole with the Hong Kong Park of the Loop offers 300 hectares of I&T land and about 7 million square metres of floor area, equivalent to 17 Science Parks. The Hong Kong AI Research and Development Institute and the Institute of Life Sciences are to be established in 2026, and the cost threshold of the New Industrialisation Acceleration Scheme has been lowered from $300 million to $150 million.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1科研成果留不住

上游論文與專利世界前列，但缺乏中試線、合約製造商與第一單本地買家，團隊拿到 A 輪後多數把生產與市場放在深圳或海外，香港只留下註冊地址。

1Results do not stay

Upstream papers and patents rank highly, but with no pilot lines, contract manufacturers or first local customers, teams that raise a Series A place production and market elsewhere, leaving Hong Kong only a registered address.

2人才結構倒掛

引進的是研究人員，缺的是產線工程師、驗證工程師與品質管理人員。本地工程畢業生長期流向金融與地產相關行業，職業階梯不明。

2An inverted talent structure

What is imported is researchers; what is missing is process, validation and quality engineers. Local engineering graduates have long moved into finance and property-related work, with no clear career ladder in industry.

3公帑投入的成效難證

基礎研究的回報期超過一屆政府任期，而立法會與公眾以年度為單位問成效。這造成兩種失真：重宣布輕跟進，以及以「招商數目」代替「產業產值」作為指標。

3Outcomes are hard to prove

Basic research pays back beyond the life of an administration, while the legislature and the public ask for results annually. Two distortions follow: announcement is favoured over follow-through, and the number of firms attracted substitutes for industrial output as the measure.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1財政影響：引導基金屬投資而非經常開支，短期不增加經常性承擔，但一旦虧損將以資本項目形式反映，並可能引發對財政紀律的質疑。

1Fiscal: the fund is investment rather than recurrent expenditure, so it adds no recurrent commitment in the short term; losses would however surface as capital items and may prompt questions about fiscal discipline.

2經濟影響：若製造環節真正落地，將首次為香港帶來大量中等技術、中等薪酬的職位，直接改善收入分配結構；若只停留在研發，則加劇高低兩極。

2Economic: if manufacturing genuinely lands, Hong Kong gains a large body of mid-skill, mid-wage jobs for the first time, improving the distribution of income; if activity stops at R&D, polarisation worsens.

3區域影響：香港與深圳在創科上既合作又競爭。分工清晰（香港做國際化與規則、深圳做量產）則互補；分工不清則重複投入並互相壓價。

3Regional: Hong Kong and Shenzhen both co-operate and compete. A clear division of labour, with Hong Kong on internationalisation and rules and Shenzhen on volume production, is complementary; an unclear one duplicates investment and drives prices down on both sides.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

市場失靈確實存在：早期硬科技的風險與回報期，私人資本不會單獨承擔，政府先行入場是補位而非搶位。

站反方？這樣駁

市場失靈是入場的理由，不是無限入場的理由。若沒有退出機制和成效公開，「補位」很容易變成長期補貼；而且早期硬科技失敗率高，公帑承擔的是私人不願承擔的虧損。

FOR 1

There is a genuine market failure: private capital alone will not carry the risk and time horizon of early-stage hard technology, so the state entering first fills a gap rather than displacing anyone.

Counter

Market failure justifies entry, not unlimited entry. Without exit rules and published results, “filling a gap” becomes a permanent subsidy, and with high failure rates in early hard tech the public purse absorbs losses private investors avoid.

支持 2

產業集群有正外部性：一家鏈主企業帶來供應商、人才與培訓體系，其社會回報遠高於該企業自身回報，故值得公共補貼。

站反方？這樣駁

正外部性要真的在香港發生才算數。若鏈主企業只在港設「總部」而把供應商和生產放在灣區其他城市，香港付出補貼、外溢效益卻留在區外。

FOR 2

Clusters have positive externalities: an anchor firm brings suppliers, talent and training whose social return exceeds the firm’s own, which justifies public support.

Counter

Positive externalities count only if they happen here. If an anchor firm puts only its headquarters in Hong Kong while suppliers and production sit elsewhere in the Bay Area, Hong Kong pays the subsidy and the spillovers leave.

支持 3

國家定位已經給定：「十五五」規劃建議把國際創新科技中心列為香港定位之一，不投入等同放棄國家給予的位置。

站反方？這樣駁

國家定位給的是方向，不是任何一筆開支的理由。承擔國家定位最有效的方式，是做香港最有優勢的環節（研發、融資、國際標準），而不是在所有環節平均投入。

FOR 3

The national positioning is settled: the 15th Five-Year Plan Proposals name an international I&T centre among Hong Kong’s roles, and not investing means forfeiting a position the country has assigned.

Counter

A national role gives direction, not a blank cheque for any expenditure. The best way to fulfil it is to do what Hong Kong is best at — research, finance, international standards — not to spend across every link.

反對／制約 1

政府沒有能力挑選贏家：官員缺乏產業判斷力與退出紀律，容易被遊說俘獲，最後投向最懂寫申請書而非最有前景的企業。

站支持方？這樣駁

政府不需要自己揀贏家：母基金把投資決定交給專業基金經理，並要求私人資本配對；政府挑的是「規則」和「經理」，不是企業。

AGAINST 1

Government cannot pick winners: officials lack industrial judgement and exit discipline, are open to capture by lobbying, and end up funding the best applicants rather than the best prospects.

Counter

The Government need not pick winners itself: a fund of funds leaves decisions to professional managers and demands private matching; officials choose rules and managers, not companies.

反對／制約 2

成本結構不可能逆轉：地價、電價、工資三項在可見將來都不會下降，製造業回流的假設本身不成立，投入只會沉沒。

站支持方？這樣駁

新型工業化針對的是自動化、高增值、需要國際認證的環節，人手和土地成本佔比低；而且有大灣區配套，香港不需要在所有成本上勝出。

AGAINST 2

The cost structure cannot be reversed: land, power and wages will not fall in any foreseeable period, so the premise of returning manufacturing does not hold and the money is sunk.

Counter

New industrialisation targets automated, high-value, certification-heavy activities where land and labour are a small share of cost, backed by the Bay Area; Hong Kong need not win on every cost.

反對／制約 3

機會成本：同一筆錢投入醫療、安老或房屋，受益人數與確定性都高得多，而創科的回報不確定且集中於少數人。

站支持方？這樣駁

機會成本論忽略了稅基：沒有新產業，未來醫療、安老和房屋的開支從何而來？創科投資正是為長遠民生開支擴闊稅基。

AGAINST 3

Opportunity cost: the same money in healthcare, elderly care or housing reaches far more people with far more certainty, whereas I&T returns are uncertain and narrowly captured.

Counter

The opportunity-cost argument ignores the tax base: without new industries, where will future spending on health, elderly care and housing come from? I&T investment broadens the base that pays for livelihoods.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

規則工具：加快藥械與自動車審批

以香港藥物及醫療器械監督管理中心、自動車規管框架為例，用規管確定性換取企業落戶，幾乎不涉公帑。

Rules: faster approval pathways

Use the Centre for Medical Products Regulation and the autonomous vehicle framework to trade regulatory certainty for corporate presence, at almost no cost to the public purse.

需求工具：政府先行採購

把政府自身變成第一單買家（AI 工具箱、智慧工地、醫管局引進創新藥械辦公室），為本地方案提供可驗證的參考案例。

Demand: government as first customer

Make the Government the first buyer, through the AI toolkit, smart site management and the Hospital Authority’s new office, giving local solutions a verifiable reference case.

空間工具：只租不賣的產業用地

以低於市值的長期租約換取產業承諾與就業指標，避免用地被轉手套利，並保留政府在違約時收回的權利。

Space: industrial land on lease only

Offer long leases below market value in exchange for industrial and employment undertakings, preventing resale for gain and reserving the right to re-enter on default.

資本工具：母基金與配對投資

以引導基金配對市場資金，設定明確退出安排與上行讓利，降低「政府揀贏家」的政治風險。

Capital: fund of funds and matching

Match private capital through the fund of funds, with explicit exit terms and upside conceded, reducing the political risk of picking winners.

人才工具：產線人才而非只有研究員

把資助由博士後擴展至工藝、驗證與品質工程，並與職訓局、應用科學大學對接，建立可見的產業職業階梯。

Talent: production staff, not only researchers

Extend support beyond post-doctoral posts to process, validation and quality engineering, linking the VTC and applied science universities to build a visible industrial career ladder.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

引導基金一旦錄得虧損，將被放大為公帑損失的政治事件，令後續撥款難以通過。

化解

預先設定組合層面而非個別項目的成功指標，公開年度組合報告與退出紀錄，並以母基金架構把個別投資決定交予專業管理人，避免官員直接揀項目。

Challenge 1

A loss in the fund will be amplified into a political event about wasted public money, making later funding hard to pass.

Mitigation

Set success measures at portfolio rather than project level, publish annual portfolio and exit reporting, and use the fund-of-funds structure to place individual decisions with professional managers rather than officials.

挑戰 2

北都土地要到二○三○年代才陸續交付，企業的投資決定卻在未來兩三年內作出。

化解

以現有科學園、創新園與活化工廈提供過渡空間，並在批地文件中給予企業由過渡空間遷入北都的優先權與搬遷補貼，把時間差變成鎖定客戶的工具。

Challenge 2

Northern Metropolis land arrives through the 2030s, while investment decisions are made in the next two to three years.

Mitigation

Provide interim space in existing parks and revitalised industrial buildings, and give firms priority and relocation support to move into the Northern Metropolis, turning the timing gap into a device for locking in tenants.

挑戰 3

與深圳的分工若無明文安排，容易演變成兩地爭奪同一批企業並互相壓低條件。

化解

透過河套「一區兩園」的聯合機制，把「香港做臨床試驗、國際註冊與融資，深圳做量產與供應鏈」寫成可操作的項目分流標準，並定期檢視個案。

Challenge 3

Without an explicit division of labour with Shenzhen, the two sides compete for the same firms and undercut each other.

Mitigation

Use the joint mechanism of the Loop to set workable criteria routing clinical trials, international registration and financing to Hong Kong and volume production and supply chain to Shenzhen, with periodic case review.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

財經事務及庫務局

不談願景，談財政紀律：引導基金屬資本投資，設有組合上限、退出安排與年度報告，不構成經常開支的剛性增長。

FSTB

Do not speak of vision; speak of fiscal discipline. The fund is capital investment with a portfolio cap, exit arrangements and annual reporting, and creates no rigid growth in recurrent expenditure.

本地科技企業

強調確定性：批地條件、審批時間表與政府採購目錄要可預期，企業最怕的不是要求高，而是規則變。

Local technology firms

Stress certainty. Land terms, approval timetables and government procurement pipelines must be predictable; firms fear changing rules more than demanding ones.

大學與研究人員

強調成果轉化不影響學術自主：知識產權歸屬、衍生公司持股與升遷準則要一併調整，否則轉化只是口號。

Universities and researchers

Stress that commercialisation does not compromise academic autonomy: intellectual property ownership, spin-off equity and promotion criteria must be adjusted together, or transfer remains rhetoric.

立法會議員

強調本地就業：每一項撥款都應附本地職位數目、薪酬水平與培訓名額，讓議員能向選民交代錢去了哪裏。

Legislators

Stress local employment: every funding item should carry the number of local jobs, wage levels and training places, so members can account to constituents for where the money went.

Ready sentences

香港的優勢在上游，缺口在中下游；政策的着力點應放在把實驗室成果帶到可量產的中試環節。

Hong Kong’s strength lies upstream and its gap downstream; policy should focus on carrying laboratory results through to pilot-scale production.

政府的角色應是風險分擔者與規則制定者，而非直接的產業經營者。

The Government’s role should be that of a risk-sharer and rule-setter rather than an operator of industry.

與深圳的關係應建立在分工之上，而非在同一環節上互相競逐。

The relationship with Shenzhen should rest on a division of labour rather than competition at the same point in the chain.

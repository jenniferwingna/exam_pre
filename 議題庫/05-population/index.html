人口、生育與人才

Population, Fertility and Talent
← 議題庫
指標措施方向取態比較出題目的矛盾現況問題影響論點與反駁方案挑戰游說句式詞彙

一句話方向

兩個「轉向」：生育政策由「不干預」轉為主動鼓勵生育，以「組合拳」加碼現金、稅務、置業、託兒和輔助生育，並特別向第二胎傾斜；人才政策由「搶人」轉向「精準引才＋留學香港」——人才清單加入AI類別、放寬初創人才續簽、非本地專上學生目標10萬人，把高等教育變成人口政策的一部分。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，現金獎勵和稅務優惠無助提升生育率。你是否同意？試分析香港生育率偏低的原因，就政府的鼓勵生育措施提出優化建議，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說僱主團體支持家庭友善措施。

出處：施政報告§288–290「鼓勵生育十一措施」、§145–152「國際高端人才集聚高地」；五年規劃第九章第六節（FYP 2.55–2.57）、第十九章（FYP 5.11–5.19）、第二十二章專欄36、主要指標13

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Two turns: fertility policy has moved from non-intervention to actively promoting childbirth, with a package of cash, tax, home-purchase, childcare and assisted-reproduction measures weighted towards second children; talent policy has moved from “grabbing talent” to targeted recruitment plus “Study in Hong Kong” — AI roles added to the Talent List, easier extensions for start-up founders, and a target of 100 000 non-local post-secondary students, making higher education part of population policy.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say cash rewards and tax concessions do nothing to raise fertility. Do you agree? Analyse why Hong Kong’s fertility is low, propose improvements to the Government’s measures, analyse implementation challenges and responses, and persuade employers’ associations to support family-friendly practices.

§150–151

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

不干預生育→主動鼓勵生育

施政報告原文：「本屆政府改變過去不干預政策，轉為鼓勵生育」。這是價值取向的轉變——生育被界定為公共政策問題。

Non-intervention in fertility→Actively promoting fertility

The Address says outright that the Government “has shifted from a non-interventionist approach to actively promoting fertility”: childbirth is now framed as a matter of public policy.

一次性、普遍的獎勵→向第二胎傾斜

獎勵金和免稅額的加碼只適用於第二名及以後的子女，針對「生一個就停」的家庭。

One-off universal rewards→Weighted towards the second child

The increases apply only to second and later children, targeting families who stop at one.

以現金為主→「組合拳」：現金＋稅務＋置業＋託兒＋醫療

把生育和住屋掛鈎（印花稅、95%按揭）是新元素，承認住屋空間是延遲生育的主因之一。

Mainly cash→A package: cash, tax, housing, childcare and health

Linking fertility with home purchase (stamp duty, 95 per cent mortgages) is new, acknowledging housing space as a main reason for delay.

以數量吸引人才→精準引才並留住人才

人才清單按需要加入AI類別、放寬初創續簽、宿舍和國際學校配套，重點由「來」轉向「留」。

Attracting talent by volume→Targeted recruitment and retention

Adding AI roles to the Talent List, easier extensions for founders, hostels and international schools shift the emphasis from arrival to staying.

高等教育是教育政策→高等教育也是人口與人才政策

非本地學生10萬人寫入五年規劃主要指標；學生畢業後留港就業成為人口補充的一環。

Higher education as education policy→Higher education as population and talent policy

100 000 non-local students is a key Plan indicator, and graduates who stay become part of population replenishment.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府明言放棄「不干預」，承認低生育率屬公共問題；但措施仍以誘因和營造環境為主，不涉強制，亦未引入每月育兒津貼一類的大規模經常性福利。人才方面以「精準」取代「放寬」：高才通等計劃不收緊，但加入配對、續簽彈性和生活配套。兩者都指向同一目標——在勞動人口下降的情況下維持人口質素與規模。

Government stance

The Government has explicitly abandoned non-intervention and treats low fertility as a public problem, but its tools remain incentives and environment rather than compulsion, and there is no large recurrent benefit such as a monthly child allowance. On talent, targeting replaces loosening: schemes such as the Top Talent Pass are not being tightened, but matching, flexible extensions and living support are added. Both serve one aim: sustaining the size and quality of the population as the workforce shrinks.

現金和稅務獎勵能否提升生育率？

支持的理據

養育成本高昂，現金與稅務可減輕即時負擔；向第二胎傾斜針對「生一個就停」的家庭；亦向社會傳遞重視生育的訊號。

質疑的理據

東亞經驗（韓國、新加坡、日本）顯示現金效果有限，關鍵在住屋、工時、教育競爭和照顧支援；一次性金額相對十多年的養育成本很小；可能只令生育提早而非增加。

Can cash and tax incentives raise fertility?

Case for

Raising children is expensive and cash and tax relief ease the immediate burden; weighting towards second children targets families who stop at one; it also signals that society values childbearing.

Case against

East Asian experience (Korea, Singapore, Japan) shows cash has limited effect; housing, working hours, educational competition and care support matter more; a one-off sum is small against two decades of costs; it may bring births forward rather than add to them.

把生育與置業掛鈎：針對性強，還是扭曲市場？

支持的理據

住屋空間是年輕夫婦延遲生育的主要原因；把置業支援與生育掛鈎，針對性比普遍措施強。

質疑的理據

95%按揭提高槓桿和還款風險；可能推高需求；租住的基層家庭完全受惠不到，存在公平問題。

Linking fertility to home purchase: well targeted or distorting?

Case for

Lack of space is a main reason couples delay; tying purchase support to childbirth is better targeted than general measures.

Case against

95 per cent mortgages raise leverage and repayment risk, may push up demand, and do nothing for low-income renting families — a fairness problem.

非本地學生10萬人：國際化，還是擠佔本地資源？

支持的理據

帶來收入、人才和國際化，彌補學齡人口下降造成的學額空置；畢業生留港可補充勞動力。

質疑的理據

宿舍、教研資源和校園空間的分配公平；商廈改裝的學舍質素與安全；若學生來源過度集中，國際化名不副實。

100 000 non-local students: internationalisation or crowding out locals?

Case for

Brings revenue, talent and diversity, fills places left by a shrinking school-age population, and graduates who stay add to the workforce.

Case against

Fairness in hostels, teaching resources and campus space; quality and safety of converted hostels; if sources are too concentrated, internationalisation is nominal.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持「營造生育友善環境」的方向，但論證要承認現金作用有限，把重點放在結構性因素（住屋、託兒、工作安排、教育壓力）和措施的配合。人才題可取「支持引才、強調留才與配套」。紅線：不要把生育寫成「國民責任」或主張懲罰不生育；不要用「人口替代」一類語言談輸入人才；也不要否定引進人才政策——它與國家戰略和五年規劃一致。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support the aim of a fertility-friendly environment, but concede that cash has limited effect and focus on structural factors — housing, childcare, working arrangements, educational pressure — and on how measures fit together. On talent, support recruitment while stressing retention and living support. Red lines: do not frame childbearing as a civic duty or suggest penalties for not having children; do not use “population replacement” language about incoming talent; and do not reject talent admission, which is aligned with national strategy and the Plan.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

「嬰兒花紅」現金禮（2023年起第一、二胎各11,000坡元，第三胎起13,000坡元）加兒童發展戶口的政府配對儲蓄；共享育嬰假；組屋分配優先照顧有子女家庭；2025年再加推大家庭支援。

政策全面，但總和生育率仍跌至1以下（2023年約0.97）。

✓ 可借鑑

「政府配對儲蓄」比一次性現金更能長期支持養育，並可把公帑鎖定在託兒、醫療等指定用途——香港可研究把部分獎勵金改為指定用途的戶口。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡以組屋和公積金作政策槓桿，約八成居民住組屋；香港私人住宅比例高，置業誘因的覆蓋面和效果完全不同。

Singapore

Baby Bonus cash gifts (from 2023, S$11,000 for the first and second child and S$13,000 from the third) plus government co-matching in Child Development Accounts; shared parental leave; priority in public flat allocation for families with children; extra support for large families from 2025.

Comprehensive, yet total fertility has fallen below 1 (about 0.97 in 2023).

✓ Transferable

Co-matched savings support child-rearing longer than one-off cash and can tie public money to childcare and health; Hong Kong could turn part of the bonus into a designated-use account.

✗ Not transferable

Singapore uses public housing and the CPF as levers, with about four in five residents in public flats; Hong Kong’s large private market means home-purchase incentives reach far fewer families.

韓國

近二十年投入巨額預算鼓勵生育；2024年起為零歲嬰兒每月發放100萬韓圜「父母給付」；總和生育率2023年跌至0.72的全球最低，2024年微升至0.75。

大量現金未能扭轉趨勢；研究普遍指向住屋、補習競爭、長工時和職場性別不平等；近年微升亦與疫後結婚數字回升有關。

✓ 可借鑑

教訓是「現金不能代替結構改革」——答題時可用作論證：香港的現金措施必須配合彈性工作安排、託兒和住屋，否則效果有限。

✗ 不可照搬

韓國的補習文化、首都圈集中和兵役等結構因素與香港不同，其數字變化不能直接推論香港會有同樣效果。

South Korea

Two decades of heavy spending on fertility; from 2024 a monthly parental payment of KRW 1 million for babies under one; total fertility fell to a world-low 0.72 in 2023 and edged up to 0.75 in 2024.

Heavy cash spending did not reverse the trend; research points to housing, cram-school competition, long hours and workplace gender inequality; the recent uptick also reflects a post-pandemic rebound in marriages.

✓ Transferable

The lesson is that cash cannot replace structural reform: Hong Kong’s cash measures need flexible work, childcare and housing alongside them.

✗ Not transferable

Korea’s cram-school culture, concentration in the capital region and military service differ from Hong Kong, so its numbers cannot be read across directly.

日本

2023年「兒童未來戰略」每年加碼約3.6萬億日圓；兒童津貼取消收入上限、延長至高中階段、第三胎起每月3萬日圓（2024年10月起）；2023年成立「兒童家庭廳」統籌兒童政策。

2024年出生人數首次跌破70萬，生育率約1.15，創新低。

✓ 可借鑑

由單一部門統籌兒童及家庭政策並設指標——香港生育、託兒、家庭政策分屬醫衞局、勞福局、教育局、房屋局，可考慮設專責統籌機制。

✗ 不可照搬

日本以向醫療保險供款人徵收的「支援金」融資，屬社會保險模式；香港沒有這類融資渠道，擴大開支只能靠一般稅收。

Japan

The 2023 children’s future strategy adds about ¥3.6 trillion a year; child allowances lost their income cap, were extended to high-school age and pay ¥30,000 a month from the third child (from October 2024); a Children and Families Agency was created in 2023.

Births fell below 700 000 for the first time in 2024, with fertility at about 1.15, a record low.

✓ Transferable

One agency co-ordinating child and family policy with targets: Hong Kong’s fertility, childcare and family policies sit across four bureaux and could use a dedicated co-ordinating mechanism.

✗ Not transferable

Japan funds part of this through a levy on health insurance contributors, a social-insurance route Hong Kong lacks; extra spending here must come from general revenue.

內地

2025年起全國發放育兒補貼，三歲以下嬰幼兒每人每年3 600元人民幣；多地延長產假、發展普惠託育。

仍在推行初期；政策信號由「限制」全面轉為「鼓勵」。

✓ 可借鑑

以「按年發放至三歲」代替一次性獎勵，把支援延伸到最昂貴、最辛苦的嬰幼兒階段；香港可研究分期發放並與託兒服務使用掛鈎。

✗ 不可照搬

內地以中央財政統一發放，配合戶籍與社保制度；香港的居民身份、人口流動和跨境生育等歷史議題不同，設計資格時必須另行考慮。

Mainland China

From 2025 a national childcare subsidy of RMB 3,600 a year for each child under three; many provinces have extended maternity leave and expanded affordable childcare.

Still early; the policy signal has moved fully from restriction to encouragement.

✓ Transferable

Annual payments to age three instead of a one-off sum extend support across the most costly years; Hong Kong could pay in instalments linked to childcare use.

✗ Not transferable

The Mainland pays centrally through its household registration and social insurance systems; Hong Kong’s residency rules, mobility and history of cross-boundary births require different eligibility design.

政策目的

Policy objective

維持人口與勞動力的規模及質素，減慢人口老化的速度，並鞏固香港作為國際高端人才集聚高地的定位。

To maintain the size and quality of the population and workforce, to slow the pace of ageing, and to consolidate Hong Kong's position as a hub for high-calibre talent from around the world.

核心矛盾

Core tension

生育決定屬個人選擇，公共政策的影響力有限；人才可以引進，但引進與留住是兩回事；而任何擴大人口的措施，都會即時觸發「本地資源被攤薄」的公平性爭議。

Childbearing is a private decision on which public policy has limited leverage. Talent can be admitted, but admitting and retaining are different problems. And any measure that enlarges the population immediately raises the fairness question of local resources being spread thinner.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

出生率長期偏低，人口自然增長為負，人口增長主要靠遷入。本屆政府已推出新生嬰兒獎勵金2萬元、雙倍免稅額、公屋優先配屋、居屋優先選樓、增加幼兒中心及課後託管等措施；本年再加推11項。人才方面，香港世界人才排名第四、五所大學躋身全球百強；各類入境計劃申請踴躍，但留港年期與落戶是另一組問題。非本地專上學生由2024/25學年約7.98萬人起步。

Current position

Fertility has long been low and natural increase is negative, so growth relies on inflows. This term has already introduced a $20,000 newborn baby bonus, doubled child allowances for newborns, priority public housing and HOS selection for families with newborns, and more childcare and after-school care; eleven more measures come this year. On talent, Hong Kong ranks fourth in world talent and has five universities in the global top 100; admission schemes are oversubscribed, but length of stay and settlement are separate issues. Non-local post-secondary students start from about 79 800 in 2024/25.

長期結構

出生率長期偏低，人口自然增長轉為負數，人口增長主要依靠遷入；各類人才入境計劃近年大幅擴大，申請踴躍，但留港年期與落戶情況是另一組問題。學額、住屋與醫療的承載力成為公眾關注焦點。

Long-run structure

The birth rate has been low for years, natural increase has turned negative, and population growth now depends chiefly on inward movement. Talent admission schemes have expanded substantially and attract strong application volumes, but length of stay and actual settlement are a separate question. Capacity in school places, housing and health care has become a focus of public concern.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1現金誘因對生育決定的作用有限

國際經驗顯示，一次性現金獎勵對生育率的影響短暫且微弱；真正的障礙是住屋成本、託兒供應、工時與職業代價，而這些都不是一筆錢能解決的。

1Cash incentives have limited effect on childbearing

International experience shows that one-off cash bonuses have a weak and short-lived effect on fertility. The real barriers are housing costs, child-care supply, working hours and the career penalty — none of which a single payment addresses.

2人才「來了但留不住」

吸納指標達標，政策目的卻可能落空。人才流失的原因多屬配套斷層：住屋成本、子女學額、配偶就業、社會網絡，以及本地產業結構未能提供對應的長期職位與晉升路徑。

2Talent arrives but does not stay

The intake target can be met while the policy objective fails. Attrition is usually a matter of missing supports — housing cost, school places for children, spouse employment, social networks — and of an industrial structure that cannot offer matching long-term posts and progression.

3公平性與承載力的政治成本

當本地居民感到學額、醫療與住屋被攤薄，任何人口政策都會失去社會支持，即使其長遠經濟理據充分。

3The political cost of fairness and capacity

When residents feel that school places, health care and housing are being spread thinner, any population policy loses public support, however strong its long-run economic case.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

勞動人口收縮是既成事實，遷入是短期內唯一能改變分母的手段。

站反方？這樣駁

改變分母不等於改變分子。若遷入者集中在特定年齡與職業，而學額、住屋、醫療不同步，人口增加反而加劇本地的資源競爭，社會成本會抵銷經濟收益。

FOR 1

The contraction of the workforce is already determined, and inward movement is the only lever that changes the denominator in the short run.

Counter

Changing the denominator is not the same as changing the numerator. If arrivals cluster in particular ages and occupations while school places, housing and health care do not keep pace, a larger population intensifies competition for local resources and the social cost offsets the economic gain.

支持 2

持續性的育兒支援（託兒、學前教育、醫療）比一次性獎勵更有實證基礎。

站反方？這樣駁

實證同時顯示，即使最慷慨的持續支援，對生育率的提升也相當有限。若以提高出生率為目標去投放長期經常開支，很可能付出巨大成本而看不到人口效果——應該誠實地把它定位為「減輕育兒負擔」，而不是人口政策。

FOR 2

Continuing support for raising children — child care, pre-school education, health — has a stronger evidence base than one-off bonuses.

Counter

The same evidence shows that even generous continuing support lifts fertility only modestly. Committing long-term recurrent expenditure in the name of the birth rate risks heavy cost with no demographic result; it is more honest to present it as relief of the cost of raising children than as population policy.

支持 3

高端人才帶來的不只是人力，還有資本、網絡與產業能力。

站反方？這樣駁

這假設人才會留下並投入本地產業。若本地沒有對應的產業與職位，高端人才只會把香港當成中轉站——帶來的網絡與資本同樣會隨人離開。

FOR 3

High-calibre talent brings not only labour but capital, networks and industrial capability.

Counter

This assumes talent stays and invests in local industry. Without matching sectors and posts, Hong Kong becomes a staging point, and the networks and capital leave with the people who brought them.

反對／制約 1

生育支援屬長期經常開支，而成效可能十年後仍難以證明。

站支持方？這樣駁

成效的定義錯了。託兒與學前教育的回報不止於出生率，還包括婦女勞動參與、兒童發展與長期稅基——這些都可量度而且回報明確。以出生率作唯一指標，才會得出「無效」的結論。

AGAINST 1

Support for childbearing is long-term recurrent expenditure whose effect may still be unprovable a decade later.

Counter

The measure is wrong. The returns to child care and pre-school education are not confined to the birth rate: female participation, child development and the long-run tax base are all measurable and well evidenced. Only by using fertility as the sole indicator does the policy look ineffective.

反對／制約 2

以住屋或學額優先權作誘因，會直接衝擊現行輪候與派位的公平性。

站支持方？這樣駁

公平的對象是誰？現行制度也不是中立的——它隱含地把資源分配給早到的人。以公開準則、上限配額與同步增加總供應處理，比維持一個看似中立實則排他的現狀更公平。

AGAINST 2

Using priority in housing or school places as an incentive strikes directly at the fairness of existing queues and allocation systems.

Counter

Fair to whom? The existing system is not neutral either: it allocates by order of arrival. Published criteria, a capped quota and a simultaneous increase in total supply are fairer than preserving a status quo that is exclusionary in effect while neutral in appearance.

反對／制約 3

人才政策可能引來以身份為目的而非以就業為目的的申請者。

站支持方？這樣駁

這是審批設計問題，不是政策方向問題。以受僱紀錄、稅務紀錄與續期條件作為留任門檻，就能把以身份為目的的申請者篩走，不必因此放棄整個政策。

AGAINST 3

Talent schemes can attract applicants whose object is status rather than employment.

Counter

This is a matter of scheme design, not of policy direction. Employment records, tax records and renewal conditions screen out applicants whose object is status, without abandoning the policy itself.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊與品牌

以真實的落戶故事與清晰的資訊平台取代宣傳式推廣；讓潛在人才在來港前就看清成本與路徑。

Information and brand

Replace promotional messaging with real settlement stories and a clear information platform, so that prospective arrivals see the costs and the pathway before they come.

配套服務

大幅擴充資助託兒與課餘照顧（尤其零至三歲與非辦公時段）；擴充國際與本地學額；簡化配偶就業許可。

Enabling services

Expand subsidised child care and after-school care substantially, particularly for the under-threes and outside office hours; expand school places; and simplify spouse employment permission.

誘因設計

把稅務或住屋支援與留任年期掛鈎，並設追討條款，確保支援換來的是落戶而非過境。

Incentive design

Tie tax or housing support to length of stay with a clawback provision, so that the support buys settlement rather than transit.

僱傭制度

以立法訂定家庭友善僱傭的最低標準（產假、侍產假、彈性安排的申請權），令支援不限於有能力提供的大企業。

Employment standards

Legislate a floor for family-friendly employment — maternity and paternity leave, a right to request flexible arrangements — so that support is not confined to employers who can afford it.

指標改革

把成功指標由「入境人數」改為「第三年留任率」與「受僱於目標產業的比例」，並公開發布。

Reform the indicators

Change the measure of success from arrivals to the third-year retention rate and the share employed in target sectors, and publish both.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

住屋或學額優先權會被視為插隊，公平性爭議極大。

化解

改用彈性配額而非直接優先，公開準則與名額上限，並同步增加整體供應以免形成零和。

Challenge 1

Priority in housing or school places is read as queue-jumping and the fairness dispute is severe.

Mitigation

Use a flexible quota rather than outright priority, publish the criteria and the ceiling, and expand total supply at the same time so the arrangement is not zero-sum.

挑戰 2

託兒服務本身人手不足，擴容有物理上限。

化解

先用較輕的模式擴容：社區保姆、學校共用空間、與長者中心跨代共址、延長現有中心時段。

Challenge 2

Child-care services are themselves short of staff, so expansion has a physical ceiling.

Mitigation

Expand first through lighter models — community child-minders, shared school premises, intergenerational co-location with elderly centres, and longer hours at existing centres.

挑戰 3

留任率屬滯後指標，短期內無法證明政策有效。

化解

同步設立領先指標：配偶就業比例、子女入學比例、續期申請比例、離港者的離職調查。

Challenge 3

Retention is a lagging indicator and cannot demonstrate success in the short term.

Mitigation

Establish leading indicators alongside it: spouse employment, children enrolled in school, renewal applications, and exit surveys of those who leave.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

本地家庭與青年

先講整體供應增加，再講人才配額；承諾公開承載力數據。

Local families and young people

Lead with the increase in total supply before any mention of talent quotas, and commit to publishing capacity data.

僱主

以人才留任帶來的招聘成本節省作論據，換取他們在培訓與家庭友善措施上的投入。

Employers

Argue from the recruitment costs avoided by retention, in exchange for their investment in training and family-friendly measures.

立法會

以留任率與產業就業比例等可審視的指標回應問責關注。

The legislature

Answer accountability concerns with reviewable indicators such as retention and sectoral employment.

目標人才群體

他們關心的是子女教育、配偶工作與長期職涯，不是宣傳口號。

The target talent group

What they weigh is schooling for their children, work for their spouse and a long-term career — not promotional messaging.

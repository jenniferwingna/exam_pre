一句話方向

交通政策同時做「新規管」和「新基建」：網約車規管8月生效、第四季發牌，並以數據管理系統為日後供求評估作準備；自動駕駛由測試走向商業化；智慧綠色集體運輸系統突破重鐵局限；北都以北環線（2034年）、港深西部鐵路（2035年）、北都公路和青龍大橋打通南北與跨境；以AI「交通管理平台」應對極端天氣。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，網約車規管制度落實後，政府應按市場需求放寬網約車數目，以提升點對點交通服務。你是否同意？試分析現況，提出建議，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說的士業界。

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Transport policy combines new regulation with new infrastructure: ride-hailing rules took effect in August with licences from Q4 and a data system for future supply-demand review; autonomous vehicles move from trials to commercial service; smart green mass transit breaks the limits of heavy rail; the Northern Link (2034), the Hong Kong–Shenzhen Western Rail Link (2035), the Northern Metropolis Highway and Tsing Lung Bridge connect north and south and across the boundary; an AI traffic management platform prepares for extreme weather.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say that once ride-hailing regulation is in place, the Government should relax the number of vehicles according to demand to improve point-to-point services. Do you agree? Analyse the situation, make proposals, analyse the challenges and responses, and persuade the taxi trade.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

「公共交通為本、鐵路為骨幹」不變；新模式（網約車、自動駕駛）以發牌和數據監管納入體制，而非禁止；本年未見電子道路收費等新的需求管理措施——政府以基建擴容和科技管理為主軸。

Government stance

Public transport first, with rail as the backbone, remains the principle; new modes such as ride-hailing and autonomous vehicles are brought into the system through licensing and data, not banned; there is no new demand management such as road pricing this year — the thrust is capacity and technology.

網約車發牌後應否按需求放寬數目？

支持的理據

市民需要多元點對點服務；合法化後司機和車輛有保險和背景審查；數據系統可監察平台並按需求調整牌照數目。

質疑的理據

的士業界投資了昂貴牌照，面對不對等競爭；網約車數目若大增，會加重路面擠塞；數據收集涉及私隱。

Should ride-hailing numbers be relaxed according to demand after licensing?

Case for

People want more point-to-point options; legalisation brings insurance and background checks; the data system lets government adjust licence numbers to demand.

Case against

Taxi owners paid for expensive licences and face uneven competition; a surge in numbers adds congestion; data collection raises privacy issues.

自動駕駛商業化：安全與責任由誰承擔？

支持的理據

自動駕駛可紓緩職業司機老化和短缺，並提升安全；先在封閉或低速路段營運，風險可控。

質疑的理據

事故責任（製造商、營運商、遙距操作員）的法律安排未清晰；公眾信任不足；對職業司機就業有影響。

Autonomous vehicles: who bears safety and liability?

Case for

They can ease the ageing and shortage of professional drivers and improve safety; starting on closed or low-speed routes keeps risk manageable.

Case against

Liability among manufacturers, operators and remote operators is unclear; public trust is low; professional drivers’ jobs are affected.

「粵車南下」擴展：促進融合，還是加重本地交通壓力？

支持的理據

雙向便利促進兩地人流和消費；配額和指定泊車可控制流量。

質疑的理據

市區道路和泊車位緊張；兩地交通規則和駕駛習慣不同帶來安全問題；跨境保險索償複雜。

Wider Southbound Travel: integration or more pressure on local roads?

Case for

Two-way access boosts travel and spending; quotas and designated parking control volumes.

Case against

Urban roads and parking are tight; different rules and driving habits raise safety concerns; cross-boundary insurance claims are complex.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持「以規管和數據把新模式納入體制」；答題強調數據監察、分階段放寬、乘客安全和的士業轉型支援。紅線：不要主張取消網約車發牌或全面禁止；也不要把「粵車南下」寫成負面——可以談配額、泊車和安全配套，而不是反對跨境融合。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support bringing new modes into the system through regulation and data; stress monitoring, phased relaxation, passenger safety and transition support for taxis. Red lines: do not propose abolishing ride-hailing licensing or banning it; and do not frame Southbound Travel negatively — discuss quotas, parking and safety rather than opposing cross-boundary integration.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

《點對點載客運輸業法》（2019年）要求車隊達800輛或以上的網約車平台申領營運牌照，司機須持職業執照；以電子道路收費（1998年起，正過渡至衞星系統）和擁車證配額（1990年起）管理道路需求。

網約車與的士並存，受同一套服務標準規管；道路需求以價格管理。

✓ 可借鑑

平台發牌＋司機職業執照＋數據提交，與香港網約車規管一致；新加坡以同一套標準規管的士和網約車，可作為香港「公平競爭」的方向。

✗ 不可照搬

擁車證和電子道路收費在新加坡有數十年的政治接受度；香港電子道路收費研究多年未落實，屬高度政治敏感議題，不能輕言照搬。

Singapore

The Point-to-Point Passenger Transport Industry Act (2019) requires ride-hail platforms with 800 or more vehicles to be licensed and drivers to hold vocational licences; road demand is managed through Electronic Road Pricing (since 1998, moving to a satellite system) and a vehicle quota (since 1990).

Ride-hailing and taxis coexist under common service standards; road demand is managed by price.

✓ Transferable

Platform licensing, driver vocational licences and data submission match Hong Kong’s regime; common standards for taxis and ride-hailing point to fair competition.

✗ Not transferable

Vehicle quotas and road pricing have decades of acceptance in Singapore; Hong Kong has studied road pricing for years without implementing it, and it remains politically sensitive.

日本

網約車長期受嚴格限制；2024年起推出「日本版共乘」，只容許的士公司在司機不足的地區和時段以私家車提供服務。

保護了的士業，但偏遠地區和繁忙時段「叫車難」仍然突出。

✓ 可借鑑

以「地區、時段」定向開放，說明規管可以精細化——香港網約車日後的供求評估可按地區和時段調整牌照或服務要求。

✗ 不可照搬

日本的士業規模大、政治影響力強，政策以保護現有業界為先；香港已選擇發牌規管平台，不宜倒退到只由的士公司營運的模式。

Japan

Ride-hailing has long been tightly restricted; since 2024 a limited scheme lets taxi companies use private cars in areas and hours short of drivers.

Taxis are protected, but getting a ride in remote areas and peak hours remains hard.

✓ Transferable

Opening by area and time shows regulation can be fine-grained; Hong Kong’s future supply-demand reviews could vary licences or service obligations by district and hour.

✗ Not transferable

Japan’s taxi lobby is large and influential and policy protects incumbents; Hong Kong has chosen to license platforms and should not revert to a taxi-only model.

深圳

2022年施行《深圳經濟特區智能網聯汽車管理條例》，在內地地方法規中率先明確自動駕駛事故的責任分配；公交和的士基本全面電動化；武漢等城市已有大規模無人駕駛出租車營運。

法規先行令企業可在指定區域商業營運；但無人駕駛出租車亦引起司機生計爭議。

✓ 可借鑑

「先訂責任規則、再擴大營運」：香港自動駕駛商業化可參考深圳條例，明確製造商、營運商和安全員的責任與保險要求。

✗ 不可照搬

內地城市道路較寬、有大面積新區可作測試；香港道路狹窄、人車密度高，擴展須更審慎並以場景分級推進。

Shenzhen

Shenzhen’s 2022 regulation on intelligent connected vehicles was the first local law in the Mainland to allocate liability for autonomous-vehicle accidents; buses and taxis are almost fully electric; cities such as Wuhan run large robotaxi fleets.

Clear rules allowed commercial operation in designated zones, though robotaxis stirred disputes over drivers’ livelihoods.

✓ Transferable

Set liability rules first, then scale up: Hong Kong can borrow Shenzhen’s allocation of responsibility and insurance among makers, operators and safety officers.

✗ Not transferable

Mainland cities have wide roads and large new districts for testing; Hong Kong’s narrow, crowded streets require more caution and staged expansion by scenario.

韓國（首爾）

2004年巴士改革：路線重組為幹線與支線、半公營制（政府保證營運收入、按服務表現付款）、中央公交專用道和轉乘優惠；2020年修法限制以「租車＋司機」形式營運的網約車服務。

巴士服務可靠度提升；但半公營制的財政補貼持續增加。

✓ 可借鑑

「按服務表現付款」的營運資助——香港綠色專線小巴或偏遠路線的資助，可研究與準時率和覆蓋掛鈎。

✗ 不可照搬

首爾半公營制令政府承擔營運虧損；香港專營巴士以商業原則營運、票價機制不同，全面引入會帶來龐大財政承擔。

South Korea (Seoul)

The 2004 bus reform reorganised routes into trunk and feeder lines, introduced semi-public operation (guaranteed revenue with payment for performance), median bus lanes and transfer discounts; a 2020 law curbed ride-hailing run as car rental with a driver.

Bus reliability improved, but subsidies under semi-public operation keep rising.

✓ Transferable

Payment for performance could shape support for Hong Kong’s green minibuses or remote routes, tied to punctuality and coverage.

✗ Not transferable

Seoul’s model puts operating losses on the government; Hong Kong’s franchised buses run commercially with a different fare mechanism, so full adoption would be a large fiscal commitment.

政策目的

Policy objective

在高密度城市中維持全球罕見的公共交通載客比例，同時以鐵路帶動北部都會區的新增人口與就業，並為自動駕駛、低空經濟等新科技建立規管軌道。

To sustain a public transport share rarely seen elsewhere in a high-density city, to drive the new population and employment of the Northern Metropolis through rail, and to build regulatory pathways for autonomous vehicles and the low-altitude economy.

核心矛盾

Core tension

公共交通高度依賴專營與交叉補貼：賺錢的路線養蝕錢的路線，鐵路以物業發展補貼營運。一旦引入新的競爭者（網約車、跨境巴士、自動車），原有的補貼結構就會鬆動；但不引入，服務質素與選擇又長期停滯。

Public transport depends on franchises and cross-subsidy: profitable routes carry loss-making ones and rail operations are subsidised by property development. Introducing new competitors such as ride-hailing, cross-boundary coaches and autonomous vehicles loosens that structure; not introducing them leaves service quality and choice stagnant.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

本屆政府已立法規管網約車、實施三隧分流；六號幹線今年全線開通後，繁忙時段將軍澳往油麻地由約65分鐘減至約12分鐘。運輸署已發出九個自動車先導牌照，逾60輛在多區測試。電動私家車佔新登記逾七成。「港車北上」運作穩定，「粵車南下」逐步擴展。北都九個新發展區全數開動，南北向交通需求急增。

Current position

This term has legislated for ride-hailing and introduced differential tunnel tolls; with Route 6 fully open this year, peak journeys from Tseung Kwan O to Yau Ma Tei fall from about 65 to about 12 minutes. Nine pilot licences cover over 60 autonomous vehicles in several districts. Electric cars are over 70 per cent of new private registrations. Northbound Travel for Hong Kong Vehicles runs steadily and Southbound Travel is expanding. All nine Northern Metropolis development areas are under way, sharply increasing north-south travel demand.

長期結構

《2025 年道路交通（修訂）（網約車服務）條例草案》於二○二五年十月十五日在立法會通過，終結超過十年的規管空白。自動駕駛車輛規管框架已於二○二四年三月實施，並開始跨區試行及「遙距操作自動駕駛車輛」測試。北環線主線與支線合併推展，爭取二○三四年或之前同步開通；港深西部鐵路（洪水橋至前海）香港段目標二○二七年招標。東涌線延線、小蠔灣站、屯門南延線、古洞站、洪水橋站將於二○二七年起陸續完成。運輸基建以「八縱八橫」新布局推展，並首次引入智慧綠色集體運輸系統（啟德先行）。

Long-run structure

The Road Traffic (Amendment) (Ride-hailing Service) Bill 2025 was passed on 15 October 2025, ending a regulatory gap of more than a decade. The regulatory framework for autonomous vehicles took effect in March 2024, with cross-district trials and remote-operation testing under way. The Northern Link main line and spur have been merged for simultaneous opening by 2034 or earlier, and the Hong Kong section of the Hong Kong–Shenzhen Western Rail Link is targeted for tender in 2027. The Tung Chung Line extension, Siu Ho Wan station, Tuen Mun South extension, Kwu Tung and Hung Shui Kiu stations complete progressively from 2027. Transport infrastructure follows a new "eight vertical, eight horizontal" layout, and a smart green mass transit system is being introduced for the first time, starting at Kai Tak.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1交叉補貼結構脆弱

專營巴士以繁忙路線補貼偏遠路線，鐵路以物業補貼營運。新競爭者只搶最賺錢的一段，留下不賺錢的部分給原有營辦商，最終由公帑或乘客補上。

1A fragile cross-subsidy

Franchised buses fund remote routes from busy ones and rail funds operations from property. New entrants take only the profitable segment, leaving the rest to incumbents and ultimately to the public purse or passengers.

2基建周期與人口周期不匹配

鐵路由規劃到通車超過十年，而新發展區的人口在通車前已遷入，形成「有樓無路」的空窗期，居民的不滿在這段時間累積。

2Infrastructure and population cycles are out of step

Rail takes more than a decade from planning to opening, while residents move into new areas before then, creating a window of housing without transport in which discontent accumulates.

3新科技的責任歸屬未明

自動駕駛一旦發生事故，責任在製造商、營運者、遙距操作員還是道路管理者，現行法例未有完整安排；責任不清會令保險市場卻步，進而卡住商業營運。

3Liability for new technology is unsettled

If an autonomous vehicle causes an accident, whether the manufacturer, operator, remote supervisor or road authority is liable is not fully settled; unclear liability deters insurers and in turn blocks commercial operation.

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

3區域影響：港深西部鐵路與北環線把兩地通勤圈接合，將改變居住與就業的空間分布，長遠影響房屋需求與稅基分布。

3Regional: the Western Rail Link and Northern Link join the two commuting areas, reshaping where people live and work and, over time, housing demand and the distribution of the tax base.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

引入新供給能直接提升服務質素：長期缺乏競爭令的士服務水平停滯，規管後的網約車提供了可問責的替代選擇。

站反方？這樣駁

新供給若無數量和擠塞管理，只會把的士的問題換成路面擠塞；服務質素的提升亦可以靠規管的士本身達到。

FOR 1

New supply raises service quality directly: a long absence of competition left taxi standards static, and regulated ride-hailing provides an accountable alternative.

Counter

New supply without limits simply trades taxi problems for congestion; taxi service quality can also be raised by regulating taxis themselves.

支持 2

自動駕駛可紓緩司機短缺：公共交通與物流業的司機老化與招聘困難是結構性問題，技術是少數可能的解方。

站反方？這樣駁

自動駕駛距離在香港複雜路面大規模營運仍遠，短期內無法填補司機缺口；以此為理由延後改善司機待遇，只會令短缺惡化。

FOR 2

Autonomous vehicles can relieve driver shortages: an ageing and hard-to-recruit driver workforce in public transport and logistics is structural, and technology is one of the few available answers.

Counter

Large-scale autonomy on Hong Kong’s complex roads is far off and cannot fill the driver gap soon; using it to postpone better pay for drivers worsens the shortage.

支持 3

鐵路先行可鎖定發展形態：先定線再發展，能把密度集中在車站周邊，避免新市鎮低密度蔓延與長遠的營運虧損。

站反方？這樣駁

鐵路先行的前提是人口和就業會到位；若產業延遲，昂貴的鐵路長期低載客，營運虧損最終仍由公帑承擔。

FOR 3

Leading with rail fixes the urban form: setting alignments first concentrates density around stations and avoids the low-density sprawl and long-run operating deficits of earlier new towns.

Counter

Rail first assumes people and jobs will arrive; if industry is late, an expensive line runs empty and the losses still fall on the public.

反對／制約 1

開放市場會瓦解交叉補貼：偏遠地區與深夜服務沒有商業理由存在，最終要由公帑以特別補貼維持，總成本未必下降。

站支持方？這樣駁

交叉補貼本已不透明；以「目標為本」的服務補貼直接資助偏遠和深夜服務，比依賴壟斷利潤交叉補貼更有效率、更可問責。

AGAINST 1

Opening the market dissolves cross-subsidy: remote and late-night services have no commercial rationale and must then be sustained by targeted public subsidy, with no guarantee of lower total cost.

Counter

Cross-subsidy is opaque anyway; targeted subsidies for remote and late-night services are more efficient and accountable than relying on monopoly profits.

反對／制約 2

自動駕駛的安全風險不對稱：一宗致命事故所帶來的公眾抗拒，足以令整個技術路線停擺數年，而收益是漸進的。

站支持方？這樣駁

正因風險不對稱，所以要按場景分級、先低風險路段、公開測試數據和明確責任；這是管理風險，而非放棄技術。

AGAINST 2

The risk from autonomous vehicles is asymmetric: a single fatal incident can stall the entire technology for years, while the benefits accrue only gradually.

Counter

Because the risks are asymmetric, deployment should be tiered by scenario, low-risk routes first, with published data and clear liability — managing risk, not abandoning the technology.

反對／制約 3

基建先行的財政賭注過大：若產業導入不如預期，昂貴的鐵路將長期低載客量運作，成為後續政府的負擔。

站支持方？這樣駁

不先建交通，產業和居民更不會來；北都公路、北環線是降低所有其他風險的前提，分期建設亦可控制財政承擔。

AGAINST 3

Infrastructure-led development is a large fiscal wager: if industry does not follow, costly rail runs at low patronage and becomes a burden on later administrations.

Counter

Without transport first, neither firms nor residents will come; the highway and Northern Link lower every other risk, and phasing controls the fiscal exposure.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊工具：實時路況與班次開放數據

以開放數據讓市場開發出行規劃工具，幾乎零成本而能顯著改善轉乘與候車體驗。

Information: open real-time data

Release real-time traffic and service data so the market builds journey-planning tools, at almost no cost and with clear gains in interchange and waiting experience.

規管工具：發牌與標準

網約車發牌、自動車規管框架、技術標準與保險要求，以確定性換取投資，是最具成本效益的一級。

Regulatory: licensing and standards

Ride-hailing licences, the autonomous vehicle framework, technical standards and insurance requirements trade certainty for investment, the most cost-effective rung.

財政工具：目標為本的服務補貼

以路線為單位公開招標並補貼指定服務水平，取代以營辦商為單位的交叉補貼，使補貼去向透明。

Fiscal: targeted service subsidy

Tender route by route and subsidise specified service levels, replacing operator-level cross-subsidy so that the destination of subsidy is transparent.

基建工具：鐵路與智慧綠色集體運輸

大型鐵路配合中運量的智慧綠色集體運輸系統，處理新發展區在鐵路通車前的接駁需求。

Infrastructure: rail with smart green mass transit

Pair heavy rail with medium-capacity smart green mass transit to serve new areas before rail opens.

需求管理工具：電子道路收費

最重的一級，需要同步處理收入用途、公眾信任與替代出行選擇，否則難以取得社會授權。

Demand management: road pricing

The heaviest rung, requiring the use of revenue, public trust and alternative travel options to be settled together before social consent can be obtained.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

網約車發牌後，的士業界會以罷駛或司法覆核等方式表達反對，衝擊條例的落實時間表。

化解

設定過渡期與司機轉換渠道，讓現有的士司機可優先取得網約車司機資格；同時以車隊制度與服務質素指標同步提升的士服務，使業界看到自身也有出路。

Challenge 1

After licensing, the taxi trade may respond with strikes or judicial review, disrupting the implementation timetable.

Mitigation

Set a transition period and a conversion route so existing taxi drivers can obtain ride-hailing licences first, while raising taxi standards through fleet licensing and service indicators so the trade sees a path of its own.

挑戰 2

自動駕駛的責任與保險安排未定，令商業營運無法展開。

化解

先在法例中訂明遙距操作員與營運者的法定責任，並與保險業界共同設計試驗期的分層保險方案，以有限規模的商業試營運累積理賠數據。

Challenge 2

Unsettled liability and insurance prevent commercial operation from starting.

Mitigation

Fix the statutory responsibilities of remote operators and operating companies in legislation and work with insurers on a layered scheme for the trial period, accumulating claims data through limited-scale commercial trials.

挑戰 3

北都鐵路投資龐大，若人口與產業進駐延遲，將出現長期低載客。

化解

把鐵路分期通車與土地釋放掛鈎，設定每期的人口與就業門檻作為下一期動工的條件；並在初期以接駁巴士與智慧綠色集體運輸補位，避免一次過承擔全部風險。

Challenge 3

Heavy rail investment in the Northern Metropolis risks prolonged low patronage if population and industry arrive late.

Mitigation

Tie phased opening to land release, making population and employment thresholds a condition of starting the next phase, and cover early demand with feeder buses and smart green mass transit rather than carrying the whole risk at once.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

的士與小巴業界

談生存空間而非談公平：具體說明車隊制度、司機轉換渠道與服務質素提升的支援，讓他們看到自己在新格局中的位置。

Taxi and minibus trades

Speak of where they will stand, not of fairness in the abstract: set out fleet licensing, conversion routes and support for raising standards so they can see their place in the new arrangement.

專營巴士與鐵路營運商

談可預期性：路線重組、票價調整機制與補貼安排要有明確規則，營運商最怕的是政策逐年變動。

Franchised bus and rail operators

Speak of predictability: route rationalisation, fare adjustment mechanisms and subsidy arrangements need settled rules, since year-to-year change is what operators fear most.

新發展區居民

談時間表而非願景：哪一年通車、之前以什麼接駁、班次多密，比任何規劃圖都更能取得支持。

Residents of new development areas

Speak of timetables, not visions: which year the line opens, what serves them until then and at what frequency does more to win support than any plan drawing.

保險業界

談可保性：責任分層、數據存取與理賠標準若不先確立，自動駕駛的商業營運無法起步，這是規管而非技術問題。

The insurance industry

Speak of insurability: without settled layers of liability, data access and claims standards, commercial autonomous operation cannot begin, and that is a regulatory rather than a technical problem.

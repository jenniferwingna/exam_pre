一句話方向

文化政策由「資助藝術」轉向「文藝創意產業」：以《文藝創意產業發展藍圖》為綱，目標2030年產業增加值1,700億元、就業22.8萬人；主要藝團資助引入「與表現掛鈎的撥款和進出機制」；電影發展基金全面檢視以吸引私人投資。體育推動「普及化、精英化、盛事化、專業化、產業化」，把體育總會資助與管治合規掛鈎，並研究把維園網球場重建為全天候多用途場館。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府改革藝團資助制度、引入與表現掛鈎的撥款和進出機制，有助推動表演藝術持續發展。你是否同意？試分析並提出確保機制公平有效的措施，分析執行挑戰，並游說主要藝團支持。

出處：五年規劃第二篇第十章第三節「建設中外文化藝術交流中心」（FYP 2.63–2.66、專欄21–22）；施政報告§176–182（文化藝術）、§29–30（國家發展博物館、弘揚中華文化）、§339–348（電影與體育）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Cultural policy is moving from subsidising the arts to building a creative industry: under the Blueprint for Arts and Culture and Creative Industries, value added is to reach $170 billion and employment 228 000 by 2030; funding for major performing arts groups gets a performance-linked in-and-out mechanism; the Film Development Fund is under review to attract private money. Sport pursues popularisation, elite development, mega events, professionalism and industrialisation, links NSA funding to governance compliance, and studies turning Victoria Park’s tennis centre into an all-weather multi-purpose venue.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say reforming arts funding with performance-linked allocations and an in-and-out mechanism will support the sustainable development of the performing arts. Do you agree? Analyse and propose measures to make it fair and effective, analyse the challenges, and persuade the major groups to support it.

Source: FYP Part 2, Chapter 10 Section 3 (paras 2.63–2.66, Tables 21–22); Policy Address §176–182 (arts and culture), §29–30 (museum of national development, promoting Chinese culture) and §339–348 (film and sport)

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

按歷史延續資助藝團→產業化＋表現掛鈎的進出機制

資助不再「一入主要藝團就長期不變」，表現決定上落。

Funding by historical allocation→Industry thinking and a performance-linked in-and-out mechanism

Being a major group no longer guarantees fixed funding: performance decides movement up or down.

政府出資支持電影製作→吸引私人投資、以設施和服務支援

電影發展基金檢視的重點是「可持續」和「私人投資」，政府角色轉向基建（搭景場地）和服務（拍攝支援）。

Public money for film production→Private investment, with government providing facilities and services

The fund review stresses sustainability and private capital; the Government shifts to infrastructure (studio sets) and services (production support).

體育總會自律→資助與管治合規掛鈎

以資助作槓桿，要求總會在選拔、財務、利益申報上達標。

NSA self-regulation→Funding tied to governance compliance

Funding becomes leverage for standards on selection, finance and declarations of interest.

場地只是康樂設施→場地作為盛事經濟平台

啟德、維園重建都以「體育＋盛事」為目的。

Venues as recreation facilities→Venues as platforms for the events economy

Kai Tak and the Victoria Park proposal both serve sport plus mega events.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府以「產業化」和「市場力量」為文化政策主軸，強調公帑問責（表現掛鈎、管治合規）；同時強化中華文化和國家發展敘事，並以香港作為「中外文化藝術交流中心」說好中國故事。體育政策「五化」並重，場地政策服務盛事經濟。

Government stance

Industry and market forces are the backbone of cultural policy, with accountability for public money (performance-linked funding, governance compliance); Chinese culture and the national development narrative are strengthened, with Hong Kong as an East-meets-West cultural exchange centre telling China’s story. Sport pursues five goals together and venue policy serves the events economy.

藝團資助「進出機制」：激勵創新，還是削弱藝術自主？

支持的理據

主要藝團長期獲穩定資助、缺乏競爭；與表現和藝術水平掛鈎可獎勵優秀，讓中型藝團有上升通道；亦符合公帑問責。

質疑的理據

藝術水平難以量化，指標可能偏向票房；主要藝團需要長期規劃，進出的不確定性影響人才留任；評審人選和準則是否透明。

In-and-out funding for arts groups: spurring innovation or undermining artistic autonomy?

Case for

Major groups have enjoyed stable funding with little competition; tying money to performance and merit rewards excellence, opens a path for medium-sized groups and answers for public money.

Case against

Artistic merit is hard to quantify and indicators may drift towards box office; major groups need long-term planning and uncertainty affects retention; who assesses, and how transparent are the criteria?

維園網球場重建：盛事需要，還是犧牲公共空間？

支持的理據

香港缺乏中型全天候場館，重建可承接更高級別賽事和表演；維園交通便利，可與銅鑼灣商圈結合。

質疑的理據

維園是市區少有的大型公園，市民日常康樂受影響；噪音和人流；與啟德的分工是否重疊。

Rebuilding Victoria Park’s tennis centre: events or public space?

Case for

Hong Kong lacks a mid-sized all-weather venue; rebuilding can host higher-level events, and the site is accessible and close to Causeway Bay’s shops.

Case against

Victoria Park is one of few large urban parks and daily recreation would suffer; noise and crowds; possible overlap with Kai Tak.

體育總會資助與管治掛鈎：提升透明，還是打擊小型總會？

支持的理據

部分總會在選拔、財務、利益申報上惹爭議；以合規證書作資助條件，提升公平和透明。

質疑的理據

小型總會缺乏行政人手，合規成本高；可能出現「形式合規」。

Linking NSA funding to governance: transparency or a burden on small federations?

Case for

Some federations have faced controversy over selection, finances and declarations; certification as a funding condition improves fairness and transparency.

Case against

Small federations lack administrative staff and compliance is costly; box-ticking may follow.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持「產業化＋問責」方向，但強調評審透明、藝術自主和過渡安排；維園重建可取「支持，但保留公共康樂功能並做社區諮詢」。紅線：不要否定弘揚中華文化或國家發展博物館——可以討論內容設計和展示方式；對「說好中國故事」不作負面評論。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support industrialisation plus accountability while stressing transparent assessment, artistic autonomy and transition; on Victoria Park, support with conditions — keep public recreation and consult the community. Red lines: do not reject the promotion of Chinese culture or the national development museum — discuss content and presentation; do not comment negatively on telling China’s story.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

國家藝術理事會的「主要藝團計劃」為重點藝團提供多年期資助並定期檢討；新加坡體育城以公私營合作（PPP）興建營運，2022年政府提前收回自營。

多年期資助配定期評估兼顧穩定與問責；體育城PPP因活動量和使用率未達預期而收回。

✓ 可借鑑

「多年期資助＋定期評估」可與§181進出機制結合；體育城的教訓是場館營運合約須有清晰的活動量和公眾使用指標。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡政府可較直接重整場館營運；啟德體育園以設計、建造及營運合約交由私營承辦，須按合約條款處理，彈性不同。

Singapore

The National Arts Council’s Major Company Scheme gives key companies multi-year funding with periodic review; the Singapore Sports Hub was built and run as a PPP until the Government took it back early in 2022.

Multi-year funding with periodic review balances stability and accountability; the Sports Hub PPP was ended because events and usage fell short.

✓ Transferable

Multi-year funding with periodic review can pair with the §181 mechanism; the Sports Hub lesson is that venue contracts need clear event and public-use targets.

✗ Not transferable

Singapore could restructure directly; Kai Tak is run under a design-build-operate contract, so changes depend on contract terms.

韓國

以電影振興委員會等機構資助電影，並以「銀幕配額」保障本地電影放映日數（2006年由146日減至73日）；以文化產業振興院推動內容出口。

韓流內容全球化，影視出口龐大。

✓ 可借鑑

以公營機構作「產業投資和海外推廣」而非直接製作——與電影發展基金檢視、吸引私人投資的方向一致。

✗ 不可照搬

銀幕配額屬保護主義措施，在香港自由市場和戲院營運模式下難以推行；韓國內需市場亦遠大於香港。

South Korea

Film support through bodies such as the Korean Film Council and a screen quota guaranteeing days for domestic films (cut from 146 to 73 days in 2006); a content agency drives exports.

Korean content went global, with large film and drama exports.

✓ Transferable

Public bodies that invest and promote abroad rather than produce directly — consistent with the film fund review’s push for private capital.

✗ Not transferable

A screen quota is protectionist and hard to apply in Hong Kong’s free market and cinema model; Korea’s home market is also far larger.

日本

2013年成立「酷日本機構」投資文化產業出海，多個項目虧損，屢受審計批評。

政府主導的文化投資基金回報欠佳，被批評缺乏專業投資判斷。

✓ 可借鑑

反面教材：香港檢視電影發展基金時，應由專業人士作投資決定、設清晰退出機制和成效指標，避免「政府選片」。

✗ 不可照搬

日本基金以股權投資海外商業項目、規模較大；香港電影基金以資助和融資為主，性質不同，應借其教訓而非其模式。

Japan

The Cool Japan Fund, set up in 2013 to invest in cultural exports, made losses on many projects and drew repeated audit criticism.

A state-led cultural investment fund underperformed and was criticised for weak investment judgment.

✓ Transferable

A cautionary tale: Hong Kong’s film fund review should leave investment decisions to professionals with exit rules and outcome measures, avoiding government picking films.

✗ Not transferable

Japan’s fund took equity stakes in overseas ventures at scale; Hong Kong’s fund mainly grants and finances, so borrow the lesson, not the model.

台灣地區

2019年成立「文化內容策進院」，以國家發展基金與民間共同投資影視內容（「投資而非補助」），並推動國際合製和市場推廣。

帶動民間資金進入影視製作，部分作品在國際串流平台取得成功。

✓ 可借鑑

「共同投資」模式：政府資金作種子、要求私人資金配對，由專業團隊評估——與§341「吸引更多私人投資」一致。

✗ 不可照搬

文策院依託國家發展基金，屬台灣的產業政策工具；香港電影發展基金的資金規模和法律框架不同。

Taiwan region

The Taiwan Creative Content Agency (2019) co-invests with private partners through the National Development Fund — investment rather than subsidy — and promotes co-productions and markets.

It drew private money into film and television, with some titles succeeding on global streaming platforms.

✓ Transferable

Co-investment — public seed money matched by private capital and assessed by professionals — fits §341’s aim of attracting private investment.

✗ Not transferable

The agency relies on Taiwan’s National Development Fund; Hong Kong’s film fund differs in scale and legal basis.

政策目的

Policy objective

把文化與體育由「公共服務開支」轉為「產業與軟實力」：發展中外文化藝術交流中心、推動體育普及化、精英化、盛事化、專業化和產業化；同時保存城市的歷史記憶與非物質文化遺產，並弘揚中華優秀傳統文化。

To turn culture and sport from public service expenditure into industry and soft power: developing an East-meets-West centre for international cultural exchange; promoting sport through community participation, elite support, major events, professionalism and industry; and preserving the city’s historical memory and intangible cultural heritage while promoting fine traditional Chinese culture.

核心矛盾

Core tension

產業化要求市場邏輯：票房、贊助、營運效率；文化與保育的價值卻往往正是市場不會付錢的部分。政府越強調「產業化」與「市場營運」，越需要回答誰去保護那些不賺錢但重要的東西。另一層張力是定位：「中外文化藝術交流中心」是「十四五」的八大中心之一，卻未列入「十五五」規劃建議的「四中心、一高地」。

Industrialisation demands market logic, with box office, sponsorship and efficiency, yet the value of culture and conservation often lies precisely in what the market will not pay for. The more the Government stresses industry and market operation, the more it must answer who protects what matters but does not pay. A second tension concerns positioning: the cultural exchange centre was one of the eight centres under the 14th Five-Year Plan but does not appear among the "four centres and one hub" in the 15th Plan Proposals.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

西九文化區2025年逾1 700萬人次到訪，西九演藝中心明年啟用；香港是全球三大藝術品交易中心之一，並是巴塞爾藝術展在區內唯一主辦城市。啟德體育園至今逾170場盛事、260萬觀眾人次。粵港澳三地首次合辦全國運動會。弘揚中華文化辦公室2024年成立，「中華文化節」和「中國通史系列展覽」已成旗艦項目。

Current position

West Kowloon Cultural District drew over 17 million visits in 2025, and its performing arts centre opens next year; Hong Kong is one of the world’s three largest art markets and the only regional host of Art Basel. Kai Tak Sports Park has held over 170 events with 2.6 million attendees. Guangdong, Hong Kong and Macao co-hosted the National Games for the first time. The Chinese Culture Promotion Office, set up in 2024, runs the flagship Chinese Culture Festival and a Chinese history exhibition series.

長期結構

啟德體育園於二○二五年三月正式啟用，開幕未滿一年主場館門票銷量達亞洲第一、全球第三，總收入為亞洲之冠。二○二五至二六年度政府對香港體育學院撥款預算超過十億元，支援超過 1 600 名精英運動員；精英運動員直接資助自二○二五年四月起上調百分之八點三。香港運動員在巴黎奧運取得兩金兩銅。香港是全球三大藝術品交易中心之一，並是巴塞爾藝術展在區內唯一的主辦城市。政府已推展 24 個歷史建築保育項目，其中四個獲國際獎項，並資助八十多幢私人歷史建築維修；二○二六年《財政預算案》向「保育歷史建築基金」增撥十億元。《文藝創意產業發展藍圖》為文化政策的綱領文件。

Long-run structure

The Kai Tak Sports Park opened in March 2025; within a year the main stadium ranked first in Asia and third worldwide in ticket sales, with the highest total revenue in Asia. Funding for the Hong Kong Sports Institute in 2025-26 exceeds $1 billion, supporting more than 1,600 elite athletes, and direct financial support for athletes rose by 8.3 per cent from April 2025. Hong Kong athletes won two golds and two bronzes at the Paris Olympics. Hong Kong is one of the world’s three largest art trading centres and the only host city in the region for Art Basel. Twenty-four heritage revitalisation projects have been completed, four winning international awards, and repairs to more than 80 privately owned historic buildings funded; the 2026-27 Budget adds $1 billion to the Built Heritage Conservation Fund. The Blueprint for Arts and Culture and Creative Industries Development frames cultural policy.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1本地市場規模不足

七百多萬人口的市場難以單獨支撐專業藝團與職業體育聯賽，藝團依賴政府資助，職業運動員收入不穩定，人才外流或轉行的比例高。

1A local market too small

A market of just over seven million cannot on its own sustain professional arts groups or professional leagues; groups rely on subsidy, athletes’ incomes are unstable, and talent leaves or changes career at a high rate.

2大型場地的營運可持續性

啟德體育園等大型場地前期表現亮麗，但長期要依靠穩定的國際盛事與演唱會排期；一旦盛事減少，場地將面臨高昂的維修與營運成本。

2Sustaining large venues

Kai Tak and similar venues have started strongly, but in the long run depend on a steady calendar of international events and concerts; if events fall away, maintenance and operating costs will be heavy.

3保育與業權的衝突

大部分未獲評級或屬私人業權的舊建築缺乏法定保護，業主有權拆卸重建；政府若要保育，只能以經濟誘因換取，但誘因的成本與先例效應往往令政府卻步。

3Conservation against property rights

Most ungraded or privately owned old buildings lack statutory protection and owners may demolish and redevelop; conservation can only be bought with economic incentives, whose cost and precedent effect often deter the Government.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1經濟影響：文化體育的產業化能帶動旅遊、餐飲與零售，但效益集中於少數大型場地與盛事，中小型藝團與地區體育設施較難受惠。

1Economic: industrialising culture and sport lifts tourism, catering and retail, but gains concentrate on a few large venues and events, with small arts groups and district sports facilities benefiting less.

2社會影響：市場化營運可提升服務效率，但可能推高收費，削弱基層市民使用公共文化康樂設施的機會，與「普及化」目標存在張力。

2Social: market operation can improve efficiency but may raise charges and reduce access for lower-income residents, in tension with the goal of wide participation.

3身分影響：文化政策同時承載國家認同與本土記憶兩種功能，處理得宜可互相補充，處理不當則容易被解讀為取捨，引發社會爭議。

3Identity: cultural policy carries both national identity and local memory; handled well, the two complement each other, handled poorly, they are read as a trade-off and provoke controversy.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

盛事與大型場地帶來可量化的經濟效益與國際曝光，能在短期內提升城市形象，並為文化體育產業化提供現金流。

站反方？這樣駁

盛事的曝光和現金流屬短期；若場地租金和檔期被大型盛事佔據，本地藝團和社區活動更難找到場地，長遠損害產業根基。

FOR 1

Events and large venues bring measurable economic returns and international exposure, lifting the city’s image quickly and providing cash flow for industrialisation.

Counter

Exposure and cash flow from mega events are short-term; if they absorb venue slots and push up rents, local groups and community events are squeezed out, eroding the industry’s roots.

支持 2

市場營運能引入商業創意與效率，改善公共場地長期使用率偏低、服務僵化的問題。

站反方？這樣駁

市場營運的效率以可收費項目為主；若合約沒有公眾使用和本地節目比例要求，公共場地會變成商業場館。

FOR 2

Market operation brings commercial creativity and efficiency, addressing chronically low utilisation and rigid service at public venues.

Counter

Market operators are efficient at chargeable activities; without contractual quotas for public use and local programmes, public venues become commercial ones.

支持 3

活化再用令保育項目有持續收入，比純粹以公帑維持的原址保存更可持續，也讓市民真正使用歷史建築。

站反方？這樣駁

活化的「持續收入」往往來自餐飲零售，保育價值被邊緣化；應以保育成效而非收入評估項目。

FOR 3

Adaptive reuse gives conservation projects an income, making them more sustainable than preservation funded wholly by the public purse, and lets people actually use historic buildings.

Counter

The steady income in revitalisation often comes from dining and retail, sidelining heritage value; projects should be judged on conservation outcomes, not revenue.

反對／制約 1

過度追求盛事會令文化政策「重煙花、輕土壤」，本地藝術人才與社區文化的長期培育被邊緣化。

站支持方？這樣駁

盛事和土壤並不對立：盛事收入可回饋本地培育（例如場地優先時段、藝團資助改革的新增資源），關鍵在機制設計。

AGAINST 1

Chasing events risks cultural policy that favours fireworks over soil, marginalising the long-term nurture of local artists and community culture.

Counter

Events and grassroots are not opposites: event revenue can fund local development — priority slots, new money under the funding reform — if the mechanism is designed for it.

反對／制約 2

市場營運以盈利為先，冷門但重要的展覽與非牟利節目會被擠出，公共文化服務的普及性下降。

站支持方？這樣駁

以合約條款保障非牟利和冷門節目（公眾使用配額、社區檔期），市場營運與公共服務可以並存。

AGAINST 2

Market operators put profit first, squeezing out unpopular but important exhibitions and non-profit programmes and narrowing access to public culture.

Counter

Contract terms — public-use quotas, community slots — protect non-profit and niche programming, so market operation and public service can coexist.

反對／制約 3

活化再用容易變成商業化改造，歷史建築淪為餐廳與商店的外殼，失去原有的社區意義。

站支持方？這樣駁

商業化程度可以由保育指引和評審控制；沒有收入，很多歷史建築根本無法維修，最終只會荒廢。

AGAINST 3

Adaptive reuse easily becomes commercial conversion, reducing historic buildings to shells for restaurants and shops and stripping them of community meaning.

Counter

Heritage guidelines and assessment can limit commercialisation; without income many historic buildings cannot be maintained and will simply decay.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊工具：公開場地使用率與盛事效益評估

公開康文署場地使用率、盛事的過夜旅客與非本地觀眾比例，讓資源分配以數據為基礎。

Information: publish utilisation and event assessments

Publish LCSD venue utilisation and the share of overnight and non-local audiences at events so allocation rests on evidence.

合約工具：市場營運附帶公共服務條款

在營運合約中訂明收費上限、免費時段、本地藝團使用配額與社區節目比例，平衡效率與普及。

Contract: public service terms in operating agreements

Write fee caps, free periods, quotas for local groups and community programming into operating contracts to balance efficiency and access.

財政工具：多年期資助與配對基金

以三至四年期撥款支持中型藝團，並以配對基金鼓勵私人贊助，減少對單一年度資助的依賴。

Fiscal: multi-year grants and matching funds

Support mid-sized groups with three- to four-year grants and encourage private sponsorship through matching funds, reducing reliance on annual subsidy.

規劃工具：保育區與地積比轉移

在法定圖則中劃設保育區並以地積比轉移或換地補償業主，以經濟誘因取代強制手段。

Planning: conservation areas and plot ratio transfer

Designate conservation areas in statutory plans and compensate owners through plot ratio transfer or land exchange, using incentives rather than compulsion.

法定工具：擴大法定古蹟與評級的法律效力

最重的一級，把評級建築納入法定保護，但涉及業權補償與司法挑戰，需審慎評估財政承擔。

Statutory: strengthening legal protection for graded buildings

The heaviest rung, bringing graded buildings under statutory protection, with compensation and judicial challenge requiring careful assessment of fiscal exposure.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

市場化營運遭文化界批評為「私有化公共文化」。

化解

以試點形式先在少數場地推行，合約中明確保障本地藝團檔期與基層收費優惠，並公開營運表現報告；表現未達標可收回營運權，讓批評者看到公共利益有制度保障。

Challenge 1

Market operation is attacked by the cultural sector as privatising public culture.

Mitigation

Pilot at a few venues first, guarantee local group bookings and concessionary fees in contracts, and publish performance reports, with the right to recover operation if targets are missed, so critics can see the public interest safeguarded in the system.

挑戰 2

大型盛事的排期不穩定，場地出現閒置期。

化解

以多年期盛事基金提前鎖定國際賽事與演出，並在淡季安排社區體育、學界比賽與本地文化節目填補檔期，把閒置成本轉化為普及化的機會。

Challenge 2

The events calendar is uneven, leaving venues idle.

Mitigation

Lock in international events and performances ahead through a multi-year fund, and fill off-peak dates with community sport, school competitions and local cultural programmes, turning idle cost into an opportunity for participation.

挑戰 3

私人歷史建築保育的補償成本高，並可能引發其他業主要求同等待遇。

化解

訂立公開、按評級分級的補償準則，並以地積比轉移為主要工具而非現金補償，把先例效應轉化為可預期的制度，而非個案式談判。

Challenge 3

Compensation for conserving private historic buildings is costly and may prompt other owners to demand the same.

Mitigation

Set public criteria graded by heritage status and use plot ratio transfer rather than cash as the main tool, turning precedent risk into a predictable scheme rather than case-by-case negotiation.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

文化藝術界

談專業性與穩定性：多年期資助、透明的評審機制與本地藝團在大型場地的檔期保障，比單次盛事撥款更有實際意義。

The arts and culture sector

Speak of professionalism and stability: multi-year funding, transparent assessment and guaranteed bookings at major venues matter more than one-off event money.

體育總會與運動員

談職業出路：退役運動員轉型、教練專業化與體育產業職位，讓精英化的投入延伸為可持續的職業階梯。

Sports associations and athletes

Speak of careers: transition for retiring athletes, professional coaching and sports industry jobs, extending elite investment into a sustainable career ladder.

歷史建築業主

談經濟補償的可預期性：地積比轉移與換地的準則要清晰，業主最擔心的是保育等同資產凍結且沒有補償。

Owners of historic buildings

Speak of predictable compensation: clear rules for plot ratio transfer and land exchange, since owners fear conservation means a frozen asset without redress.

地區居民與保育團體

談社區使用權：活化項目應保留社區用途與公眾開放時段，而非只作商業營運，讓保育真正回應地區記憶。

Residents and conservation groups

Speak of community access: revitalised buildings should keep community uses and public opening hours rather than purely commercial operation, so conservation answers to local memory.

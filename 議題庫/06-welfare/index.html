一句話方向

扶貧的語言由「接濟」轉為「主動識別、精準扶助」：以社福數據分享平台和AI找出高風險家庭；把照顧者（以老護老、以老護殘、照顧特殊需要兒童）納入精準扶貧目標群組；由政府獨力承擔轉為「政、商、民」三方協作，並以配對平台把商界資源制度化。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府建立社福數據分享平台並以人工智能識別高風險家庭，可以令支援更精準。試分析其政策目的及潛在問題，提出確保平台有效及得到公眾信任的措施，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說社福界支持。

出處：施政報告第七章第八節「加強社會民生保障」（§372–380）；五年規劃第五篇第二十二章（FYP 5.41–5.50、專欄36）；《香港精準扶貧成果報告》（2026年6月）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

The language of poverty relief has moved from “relief” to “proactive identification and targeted support”: a welfare data platform with AI to find high-risk households; carers — elders caring for elders, for disabled relatives, or for children with special needs — made a target group; and a shift from the Government acting alone to government–business–community collaboration, institutionalised through a matching platform.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say a welfare data sharing platform using AI to identify high-risk households will make support more precise. Analyse its objective and potential problems, propose measures to make it effective and trusted, analyse implementation challenges and responses, and persuade the welfare sector to support it.

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

「接濟」思維→主動識別、精準扶助

施政報告直接用了這組對照：未來工作方向由傳統的接濟，邁向主動識別、精準扶助。

A “relief” mindset→Proactive identification and targeted support

The Address uses this contrast directly: from traditional relief to proactive identification and targeted help.

政府獨力承擔→「政、商、民」三方協作並制度化

政府識別對象和定規則，商界出資源，民間設計和執行——五年規劃5.46寫明分工。

Government acting alone→Government–business–community collaboration, institutionalised

Government identifies targets and sets rules, business provides resources, NGOs design and deliver — the division is spelt out in FYP 5.46.

被動等候求助→以數據把服務前移至風險累積之前

五年規劃5.45要求「讓服務盡可能前移至風險累積前的預防階段」。

Waiting for requests→Using data to move services upstream of risk

FYP 5.45 asks for services to be moved as far as possible to the preventive stage before risks accumulate.

家庭支援以危機應對為主→預防與促進家庭韌力

五年規劃5.48提出由「危機應對」逐步轉向「預防與促進家庭韌力」的長期策略。

Family services as crisis response→Prevention and family resilience

FYP 5.48 sets a long-term shift from crisis response to prevention and resilience.

照顧是家庭私事→照顧者成為政策目標群組

以老護老、以老護殘和照顧特殊需要兒童的人士被正式納入精準扶貧對象。

Care as a private family matter→Carers as a policy target group

Elders caring for elders or disabled relatives, and parents of children with special needs, are now formal targets.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

「精準而非普及」：本年沒有普遍提高綜援或推出全民現金援助，而是以服務、精準識別和社會資源為主；「有能者自力更生、無法自助者獲基本保障」，並為有能力者「造血賦能」。數據平台在「保障私隱、依法合規、建立公眾信任」的前提下推進。

Government stance

“Targeted, not universal”: no general CSSA increase or cash handout this year; the emphasis is services, precise identification and social resources. Self-reliance for those who can, a basic safety net for those who cannot, and capacity-building for those able to help themselves. The data platform proceeds on condition of privacy protection, legal compliance and public trust.

社福數據平台＋AI：及早介入，還是監控和標籤？

支持的理據

不少悲劇（雙老家庭、照顧者崩潰）事後才被發現；整合房屋、醫療、社福數據，可在風險累積前介入，令資源更精準。

質疑的理據

私隱和數據使用授權；AI誤判令家庭被標籤；「被識別」後若服務不足，只會增加焦慮；弱勢社群對政府收集數據的信任本來就低。

Welfare data and AI: early intervention or surveillance and labelling?

Case for

Many tragedies — elderly couples, carers at breaking point — are found only afterwards; linking housing, health and welfare data allows intervention before risks build up and targets resources better.

Case against

Privacy and legal authority for data use; false positives labelling families; identification without enough services only adds anxiety; trust in government data collection is already low among vulnerable groups.

「政、商、民」三方扶貧：擴大資源，還是政府卸責？

支持的理據

商界和家族辦公室有資金與專業，社福機構有前線經驗，政府負責配對和定規則，可以做到政府單獨做不到的創新項目。

質疑的理據

商界捐助不穩定，偏好短期和「看得見」的項目；基本保障應由政府承擔；配對平台的透明度和問責機制未明。

Tripartite poverty relief: more resources or government stepping back?

Case for

Business and family offices bring money and expertise, NGOs bring frontline experience, and government matches and sets rules — enabling innovations it could not deliver alone.

Case against

Corporate giving is volatile and favours short, visible projects; the basic safety net is the Government’s duty; the platform’s transparency and accountability are unclear.

照顧者：加津貼，還是加服務？

支持的理據

津貼直接紓緩經濟壓力，並承認照顧者的貢獻；加一成至3,300元簡單易行、即時見效。

質疑的理據

照顧者最缺的是喘息服務和時間；津貼遠低於照顧的機會成本；應同時擴大暫託、日間服務和緊急支援。

Carers: more allowance or more services?

Case for

Allowances ease financial strain directly and recognise carers’ contribution; a 10 per cent rise to $3,300 is simple and immediate.

Case against

What carers lack most is respite and time; the allowance is far below the opportunity cost of caring; respite, day care and emergency support must grow too.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持精準扶貧方向，但要寫出「精準的代價」：審查成本、標籤效應、門檻邊緣的人、數據私隱。建議取「數據找人、人做服務」的雙軌：數據用來發現需要，服務必須由人（社工、關愛隊）上門跟進。紅線：不要以「全民派錢」或大幅提高綜援作為核心方案——與政府方向相悖且財政不可持續；對數據平台不要全盤反對，可以支持但必須提出私隱保障和人工覆核。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support targeted poverty alleviation but spell out the costs of targeting: means-testing costs, labelling, people just above thresholds, data privacy. A good line is “data finds people, people deliver services”: data flags need, social workers and Care Teams follow up in person. Red lines: do not make universal cash handouts or large CSSA increases your core proposal — contrary to government direction and fiscally unsustainable; and do not reject the data platform outright — support it with privacy safeguards and human review.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

韓國

2014年松坡區三母女因貧困輕生後，建立「福利死角發掘系統」，整合數十種數據（例如水電費和健保費拖欠、斷水斷電）識別高風險家庭，由地方政府上門探訪。

每年識別大量高危家庭；但2022年水原三母女事件顯示，若登記地址與實際居所不符，系統仍會漏網。

✓ 可借鑑

以「拖欠公共費用」等非福利數據作預警訊號：香港可把公屋租金拖欠、水電費、醫管局缺席覆診等納入平台；同時必須配上門探訪的人手——關愛隊可以擔當。

✗ 不可照搬

韓國有統一的居民登記和社福資訊系統；香港各部門數據系統分散、法律授權各異，必須先處理《個人資料（私隱）條例》下的使用目的和授權問題。

South Korea

After three women in Songpa took their lives in poverty in 2014, Korea built a welfare blind-spot detection system that combines dozens of data items — utility and health insurance arrears, supply cut-offs — to flag high-risk households for home visits by local government.

Large numbers of households are flagged each year, but the 2022 Suwon case showed people slip through when registered and actual addresses differ.

✓ Transferable

Use non-welfare signals such as arrears: Hong Kong could include public housing rent arrears, utility bills and missed HA appointments, with home visits by Care Teams.

✗ Not transferable

Korea has unified resident registration and a welfare information system; Hong Kong’s data sits in separate systems under different legal powers, so purpose and authority under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance must be settled first.

日本

「兒童食堂」由民間和非牟利團體營運，全國已超過一萬間，為兒童及社區人士提供廉價或免費膳食，同時成為發現有需要家庭的入口。

由下而上迅速擴張；但資金多靠捐款和義工，質素與持續性參差。

✓ 可借鑑

飯堂不只是提供膳食，而是「發現需要」的入口——正是§375(iii)社區照顧者飯堂的設計邏輯，關鍵是配套轉介機制。

✗ 不可照搬

日本兒童食堂靠民間自發和捐助；香港計劃由政府推動、借用青少年中心場地，需要穩定資助和清晰的營運責任，不能只靠義工。

Japan

Children’s cafeterias (kodomo shokudo), run by volunteers and non-profits, now number over 10 000 nationwide, offering cheap or free meals and serving as a way to find families in need.

Rapid bottom-up growth, but reliance on donations and volunteers makes quality and continuity uneven.

✓ Transferable

The canteen is an entry point for finding need, not just a meal — exactly the logic of §375(iii); what matters is the referral mechanism behind it.

✗ Not transferable

Japan’s cafeterias rely on grassroots initiative and donations; Hong Kong’s scheme is government-led in youth centres and needs stable funding and clear operating responsibility, not just volunteers.

新加坡

ComCare按入息審查提供援助；2024年推出「ComLink+」，為有子女的租住組屋家庭提供「進步配套」——達到就業、儲蓄、子女學前教育等里程碑可獲獎勵及儲蓄配對。

以誘因推動自立；但亦被批評附帶條件會增加家庭壓力。

✓ 可借鑑

里程碑式獎勵把「造血賦能」具體化——可用於劏房戶上樓過渡期或「共創明『Teen』」一類計劃的設計。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡以組屋租戶身份作為識別和介入的單位，並有公積金戶口作配對工具；香港劏房戶分散在私人市場，缺乏同等的行政觸點和戶口工具。

Singapore

ComCare provides means-tested aid; ComLink+ (2024) gives families with children in public rental flats “progress packages”, with rewards and savings matching for milestones in work, savings and pre-school attendance.

Incentives promote self-reliance, though critics say conditions add pressure on families.

✓ Transferable

Milestone rewards make capacity-building concrete — useful for subdivided-unit households moving to public housing or for mentoring schemes.

✗ Not transferable

Singapore identifies families through public rental tenancy and uses CPF accounts for matching; Hong Kong’s subdivided-unit tenants are scattered in the private market without equivalent administrative touchpoints.

台灣地區

2018年起推行「強化社會安全網計畫」，整合社會福利、保護服務、自殺防治及精神衞生；以「脆弱家庭」風險分級，由社會福利服務中心作單一窗口。

通報和介入量上升；但社工人手不足和流失成為瓶頸。

✓ 可借鑑

「脆弱家庭」分級和單一窗口，令不同部門的通報匯流到同一個案管理——可對照香港照顧者、兒童保護、精神健康各自分散的現況。

✗ 不可照搬

台灣由縣市政府主責社福中心；香港地區行政沒有獨立財政和社福權力，須由社署統一部署，地區只能協調。

Taiwan region

Since 2018 a social safety net programme has integrated welfare, protection, suicide prevention and mental health, grading “vulnerable families” by risk with welfare service centres as single entry points.

Reports and interventions rose, but social worker shortages and turnover became the bottleneck.

✓ Transferable

Risk grading and a single entry point channel reports from different agencies into one case management system — a contrast with Hong Kong’s fragmented carer, child protection and mental health services.

✗ Not transferable

Taiwan’s counties run the centres; Hong Kong’s districts have no separate budgets or welfare powers, so deployment must come from the SWD with districts co-ordinating.

政策目的

Policy objective

為最有需要的人提供可靠的安全網，阻斷跨代貧窮，並在不損害就業誘因的前提下維持制度的財政可持續性。

To provide a reliable safety net for those in greatest need, to break the intergenerational transmission of poverty, and to keep the system fiscally sustainable without blunting the incentive to work.

核心矛盾

Core tension

精準扶貧的資源效益最高，但審查成本與標籤效應也最高，而且必然有一群「剛好超過門檻」的人被排除在外；普及措施沒有這些問題，卻把資源給了不需要的人。

Targeted relief gives the highest return per dollar but carries the highest administrative cost and the heaviest stigma, and always excludes a group who fall just above the line. Universal measures avoid both problems but spend money on people who do not need it.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

扶貧委員會已改稱「精準扶貧委員會」，由政務司司長領導；2026年6月的成果報告指「共創明『Teen』計劃」、社區客廳、在校課後託管等項目均達關鍵績效指標。劏房戶、雙老家庭、照顧者和單親家庭是最持續的困難群組。《強制舉報虐待兒童條例》2026年1月生效。政府原則是「有能者自力更生，無法自助者獲基本保障」。

Current position

The Commission on Poverty is now the Commission on Targeted Poverty Alleviation, chaired by the Chief Secretary; its June 2026 report says the Strive and Rise mentoring programme, community living rooms and school-based after-school care all met their KPIs. Subdivided-unit households, elderly couples, carers and single parents remain the most persistent groups in difficulty. The Mandatory Reporting of Child Abuse Ordinance took effect in January 2026. The principle is self-reliance for those who can and a basic safety net for those who cannot.

長期結構

在職貧窮、劏房住戶、單親家庭與獨居長者是最持續的貧窮群體；住屋開支是基層家庭最大的支出項，因此住屋政策實際上是最重要的扶貧政策；而跨代貧窮的核心機制是教育與社會網絡的差距，並非單純收入不足。

Long-run structure

The most persistent groups in poverty are the working poor, households in subdivided units, single-parent families and older people living alone. Housing is the largest item of expenditure for low-income households, which makes housing policy in practice the most important anti-poverty policy. The mechanism behind intergenerational poverty is the gap in education and social networks, not simply a shortfall in income.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1門檻效應與懸崖式斷層

以固定門檻界定受助資格，會令收入略高於門檻的家庭一無所得，甚至出現「多賺一元、失去全部津貼」的反誘因。

1Threshold effects and cliff edges

Fixed eligibility thresholds leave households slightly above the line with nothing, and can produce the perverse result that earning one more dollar removes the entire benefit.

2申請門檻高，覆蓋率低於資格率

程序繁複、文件要求多、標籤效應強，令部分合資格家庭從不申請；覆蓋率不足會使計劃在成效評估中顯得失敗，而原因其實在設計。

2Take-up falls short of eligibility

Complex procedures, heavy documentation and stigma mean that some eligible households never apply. Low coverage then makes the scheme look ineffective, when the cause lies in its design.

3現金轉移解決不了跨代機制

現金能紓緩當下困境，但打破跨代貧窮靠的是幼兒教育、託兒、課餘支援與社會網絡——這些屬社會投資，回報期長。

3Cash transfers do not address the intergenerational mechanism

Cash relieves present hardship, but breaking the cycle depends on early childhood education, child care, after-school support and social networks — social investment whose returns come slowly.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1以受惠人數作為成效指標，會鼓勵擴大覆蓋而非改善結果，令資源流向最容易計算的地方。

1Measuring success by the number of beneficiaries encourages wider coverage rather than better outcomes, and steers resources towards whatever is easiest to count.

2低覆蓋率造成「看似不需要」的錯覺，下一輪撥款隨之削減，最需要的人反而先受影響。

2Low take-up creates the illusion that the need is not there, funding is trimmed at the next round, and those in greatest need are the first affected.

3若扶貧只靠現金，十年後貧窮人口的結構不會改變，只是每年支出上升。

3If relief rests on cash alone, the composition of the poor will look the same in ten years' time — only the annual expenditure will be higher.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

精準投放令有限資源產生最大效益，並可量度。

站反方？這樣駁

可量度的只是投入，不是效益。精準的代價是把邊緣個案排除在外，而這些人往往離貧窮線最近、最容易跌回去；省下的錢日後會以更貴的形式支出。

FOR 1

Targeting makes limited resources go furthest and can be measured.

Counter

What is measurable is the input, not the benefit. The price of targeting is the exclusion of marginal cases — households closest to the line and most likely to fall back — and the money saved returns later in a more expensive form.

支持 2

以行政數據自動識別並主動邀請，可同時提高覆蓋率與降低標籤效應。

站反方？這樣駁

自動識別把判斷權交給數據品質。數據反映的是已經與系統接觸過的人；最邊緣、最不在紀錄中的家庭反而最容易被漏掉——技術會放大既有的盲點。

FOR 2

Using administrative data to identify and invite households raises take-up and lowers stigma at the same time.

Counter

Automatic identification hands judgement to the quality of the data. Records capture households that have already touched the system; those furthest outside it are the most likely to be missed, and the technology amplifies the existing blind spot.

支持 3

社會投資取向（幼教、託兒、課餘支援）的長期回報有充分實證。

站反方？這樣駁

實證多來自制度與人口結構不同的社會，遷移到香港時效果未必相同；而且社會投資的回報期長達十數年，期間貧窮家庭的即時困境仍然存在，不能以未來的回報回應當下的匱乏。

FOR 3

The social-investment approach — early education, child care, after-school support — has a solid evidence base for long-run returns.

Counter

Much of the evidence comes from societies with different institutions and demographics, and may not transfer. Social investment also pays back over a decade or more, during which present hardship remains — a future return is no answer to a present want.

反對／制約 1

資產審查的行政成本可能吞掉節省下來的資源。

站支持方？這樣駁

行政成本可以壓縮。自動識別、簡化申報、跨部門數據核對都能大幅降低單位成本；而放棄審查的代價是把資源大量分配給不需要的人——那不是節省，是用浪費換來的方便。

AGAINST 1

The administrative cost of means-testing can consume the savings it produces.

Counter

Administrative cost can be compressed: automatic identification, simplified declarations and cross-departmental verification cut it substantially. Abandoning the test means directing large sums to people who do not need them — not a saving, but convenience bought with waste.

反對／制約 2

目標群組的定義本身是政治選擇，容易引起「為何不是我們」的爭議。

站支持方？這樣駁

所有分配都是政治選擇，普及措施也一樣——它選擇了把資源平均分給所有人。差別在於精準扶貧把選擇公開並可辯論，而普及措施把選擇藏在「一視同仁」的表述之下。

AGAINST 2

Defining the target group is itself a political choice and invites the question "why not us".

Counter

Every allocation is a political choice, universal measures included — they choose to spread resources evenly. The difference is that targeting makes the choice explicit and arguable, while universality conceals it behind the language of treating everyone alike.

反對／制約 3

普及措施的社會凝聚功能一旦失去，制度的政治支持基礎會削弱。

站支持方？這樣駁

凝聚不必靠現金。醫療、教育、房屋等公共服務本身就是普及的，已經承擔了凝聚功能；把有限的現金資源集中於最有需要者，不會削弱這個基礎。

AGAINST 3

Universal measures carry a social-cohesion function; losing it weakens the political base of the system.

Counter

Cohesion need not rest on cash. Health, education and housing are already universal and already carry that function; concentrating limited cash on those in greatest need does not erode it.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

行政簡化

以現有行政數據自動識別合資格家庭並主動發出邀請，把申請由「證明你有需要」改為「確認資料正確」。

Administrative simplification

Use existing administrative data to identify eligible households and invite them, turning the application from "prove you are in need" into "confirm these details".

設計過渡帶

把懸崖式門檻改為遞減式安排，令收入上升不會即時失去全部支援。

Build a taper

Replace cliff-edge thresholds with a tapered withdrawal so that rising income does not instantly remove all support.

服務而非只有現金

託兒、課餘照顧、就業支援與師友計劃直接處理跨代機制，且不產生依賴誘因。

Services, not only cash

Child care, after-school support, employment assistance and mentoring address the intergenerational mechanism directly and create no dependency incentive.

住屋

住屋是基層家庭最大的支出項，任何扶貧組合若不包含住屋措施，效果都會被租金抵銷。

Housing

Housing is the largest item in a low-income household's budget; any anti-poverty package that omits it will see its effect absorbed by rent.

指標改革

以脫貧率、就業轉銜率與跨代指標（子女的教育與就業結果）取代受惠人數。

Reform the indicators

Replace beneficiary counts with exit rates, transitions into employment and intergenerational outcomes for children.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

自動識別涉及跨部門數據共享，法律基礎與私隱疑慮必須先處理。

化解

以明確的法定授權、最小必要原則、分層存取與完整審計紀錄處理，並在推行前完成法例檢討。

Challenge 1

Automatic identification requires cross-departmental data sharing, and the legal basis and privacy concerns must be settled first.

Mitigation

Deal with them through explicit statutory authority, data minimisation, tiered access rights and a full audit trail, completing the legislative review before rollout.

挑戰 2

遞減式安排會增加計算複雜度與行政成本。

化解

以簡單分級（而非連續函數）實施，並在申請平台自動計算，令複雜度留在系統而非市民身上。

Challenge 2

A taper adds computational complexity and administrative cost.

Mitigation

Implement it in simple bands rather than as a continuous function and compute it automatically on the platform, so the complexity sits in the system rather than with the applicant.

挑戰 3

社會投資的回報要十年後才顯現，難以在年度撥款中競爭。

化解

設立中期的領先指標（出席率、學習成果、家長參與）並定期公開，讓進度可被看見。

Challenge 3

The returns to social investment appear only after a decade and compete poorly in an annual budget.

Mitigation

Set interim leading indicators — attendance, learning outcomes, parental engagement — and publish them regularly so that progress is visible.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

財政當局

以脫貧率與長期依賴人數下降作論據，並提出定期檢討與退場條件。

The fiscal authorities

Argue from exit rates and a falling caseload of long-term dependency, with periodic review and exit conditions.

社福界與服務機構

他們最關心的是行政負擔與撥款穩定性；承諾簡化申報並提供多年期撥款。

The welfare sector

Their concerns are administrative burden and funding stability; offer simplified reporting and multi-year funding.

受助家庭

去標籤化是實質議題：主動邀請、電子申請、單一聯絡點，比宣傳更有效。

Recipient families

Destigmatisation is a substantive issue: proactive invitation, electronic application and a single point of contact do more than publicity.

公眾

以「資源給最有需要的人」與「幫助他們離開而非長期依賴」兩句話回應公平性質疑。

The public

Answer the fairness question with two propositions: resources go to those in greatest need, and the aim is exit rather than long-term dependency.

一句話方向

AI政策由「推應用」進入「應用＋治理」並重：政府以「AI效能提升組」（已落實30個項目）、「AI城市大腦」和「智方便」一網通辦把自己變成最大用家；同時首次提出AI風險治理七方面行動——打擊AI犯罪（研究深偽立法、立法賦權下架詐騙內容）、保護未成年人、倫理框架、採購須作安全與道德申報、產品責任、AI Agent指引、就業影響——並在數字辦設AI專員。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府應立法賦權當局要求網上平台刪除詐騙內容（包括人工智能生成的詐騙內容）。你是否同意？試分析現況，提出立法框架建議，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說科技業界支持。

出處：施政報告§121–123（AI風險治理七方面行動）、§418–420（AI城市大腦）、§437–442（AI公務與網絡安全）、§102（AI+與產業發展策略委員會）；五年規劃第二十七章第二節（FYP 6.20–6.21、專欄42–43）、專欄16、FYP 6.7–6.8

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

AI policy moves from promoting use to use plus governance: through the AI Efficiency Enhancement Team (30 projects delivered), the AI City Brain and one-stop services on iAM Smart, the Government becomes the biggest user; and for the first time it sets out seven actions on AI risk — tackling AI crime (studying deepfake laws, legal powers to take down scam content), protecting minors, an ethics framework, safety and ethics declarations in procurement, product liability, AI agent guidelines and employment effects — with an AI Commissioner in the Digital Policy Office.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say the Government should legislate to empower authorities to require online platforms to remove scam content, including AI-generated scams. Do you agree? Analyse the situation, propose a legislative framework, analyse the challenges and responses, and persuade the technology sector to support it.

§122–123

NewSeven actions on AI risk governance

Tackle AI-enabled crime, protect minors, build an ethics framework, promote safe use, clarify product liability, issue AI agent guidelines and study wider impacts; co-ordinated by the Chief Secretary with an AI Commissioner in the Digital Policy Office.

§122–123

研深偽罪行立法研究

法律改革委員會電腦網絡罪行小組委員會檢視現有法律，適時就如何應對以深偽技術製作色情影像、網絡詐騙及欺詐徵詢公眾；政府按建議研究立法。

§123(i)(a)

StudyStudying deepfake offences

The Law Reform Commission’s cybercrime sub-committee will consult on deepfake pornography and online fraud; the Government will consider legislation on its recommendations.

§123(i)(a)

法立法賦權下架詐騙內容

財庫局今年內就優化法律框架進行公眾諮詢，推動科技與電訊等行業加強偵測和刪除涉及詐騙（包括濫用AI生成）的內容。

§123(i)(b)

LegislateLegal powers to take down scam content

The FSTB will consult this year on a framework requiring technology and telecoms sectors to detect and remove fraudulent content, including AI-generated scams.

§123(i)(b)

研兒童使用社交媒體專責小組

律政司領導的工作組下設專責小組，檢視兒童使用社交媒體（包括AI互動）的風險，以及是否需要立法及行政措施；教育局在價值觀教育框架下教導學生善用、慎用AI。

§123(ii)

StudyTask force on children and social media

A task force under the DoJ-led working group will review risks of children’s social media use, including AI interaction, and whether legislative or administrative measures are needed; schools teach responsible AI use.

§123(ii)

新政府AI採購須作安全與道德申報

創科局挑選試點部門，採購面向公眾的AI服務時要求投標者遵守數字辦AI指引，並就所用AI技術作安全和道德合規申報；逐步延伸至所有部門。

§123(iii)

NewSafety and ethics declarations in AI procurement

Pilot departments will require bidders for public-facing AI services to follow DPO guidelines and declare safety and ethical compliance, later extended to all departments.

§123(iii)

研AI產品意外的法律責任

律政司領導的工作組檢視現行法律是否足以處理AI產品導致的意外或損害，研究以立法或軟法（實務守則、指引）處理。

§123(v)

StudyLiability for AI product accidents

A DoJ-led group will review whether current law covers accidents or harm caused by AI products and consider legislation or soft law such as codes and guidelines.

§123(v)

新AI Agent規範

數字辦在政府內部試行AI Agent，並訂明：只宜用於標準化、重複性的內部政務，不得直接面向公眾作決定、不得取代人員專業判斷；高風險動作須人為監督；禁止把機密或個人資料輸送至外部平台；須風險評估、權限控制並保存操作紀錄。

§123(vi)；註12

NewRules for AI agents

The DPO is piloting AI agents in government under rules: only for standardised, repetitive internal work; no decisions made directly for the public and no replacement of professional judgment; human oversight for high-risk actions; no confidential or personal data sent to external platforms; risk assessment, access controls and logs.

§123(vi)；註12

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

「發展與治理並重、制度與產業並行」：以軟法（指引、實務守則、認證）先行，避免窒礙創新；但對明確危害（深偽色情、詐騙、兒童）研究立法。政府自身使用AI的原則是：人為監督、不直接對公眾作決定、數據不外流、可追溯。

Government stance

“Development and governance in parallel, institutions and industry together”: soft law — guidelines, codes, certification — first, so as not to stifle innovation, but legislation for clear harms such as deepfake pornography, fraud and risks to children. The Government’s own AI use follows human oversight, no direct decisions for the public, no data leaving government, and traceability.

AI城市大腦與數據共享：提升效率，還是私隱與監控憂慮？

支持的理據

極端天氣和重大事故需要實時跨部門數據；杭州等城市經驗顯示可改善交通和應急反應。

質疑的理據

數據集中帶來私隱和網絡安全風險；用途可能逐步擴張；公眾信任不足會影響數據質素和配合。

AI City Brain and data sharing: efficiency or privacy concerns?

Case for

Extreme weather and major incidents need real-time cross-departmental data; Hangzhou and others show gains in traffic and emergency response.

Case against

Concentrated data brings privacy and cyber risks; uses may creep; low public trust would undermine data quality and co-operation.

立法要求平台下架詐騙內容：保障市民，還是影響網絡營運？

支持的理據

網騙損失巨大、AI詐騙急增；平台有技術能力偵測和刪除；新加坡等地已有先例。

質疑的理據

「詐騙內容」定義與誤刪；境外平台的執法；程序保障和上訴機制。

Requiring platforms to remove scam content: protecting the public or burdening the internet?

Case for

Fraud losses are huge and AI scams are rising; platforms can detect and remove content; Singapore and others have precedents.

Case against

Definitions and wrongful removal; enforcement against offshore platforms; due process and appeal.

AI監管：立法還是指引？

支持的理據

軟法靈活、不窒礙創新；香港市場規模小，過早立法可能趕走企業。

質疑的理據

明確危害需要法律阻嚇；產品責任不清令受害人難以索償；歐盟、韓國已有綜合立法。

Regulating AI: law or guidelines?

Case for

Soft law is flexible and innovation-friendly; a small market like Hong Kong risks driving firms away with early legislation.

Case against

Clear harms need legal deterrence; unclear liability leaves victims without remedy; the EU and Korea have comprehensive laws.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持「發展與治理並重」：對個別高風險範疇（深偽、詐騙、兒童）支持立法，對一般應用支持指引和認證。政府內部AI應用可直接引用官方原則（人為監督、不直接對公眾決策、可追溯）作為答題框架。紅線：不要用「監控社會」一類語言全盤否定城市大腦；應提出私隱影響評估、用途限制、數據保留期限和獨立監察。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support development with governance: legislation for specific high-risk areas (deepfakes, fraud, children), guidelines and certification for general use. For AI in government, quote the official principles — human oversight, no direct decisions for the public, traceability — as your framework. Red line: do not dismiss the City Brain in “surveillance state” terms; propose privacy impact assessment, purpose limits, retention periods and independent oversight.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

「智慧國家2.0」（2024年）；國民數碼身份Singpass覆蓋絕大部分居民；2019年起發布《人工智能治理模範框架》，2022年推出AI測試工具「AI Verify」；2023年《網上刑事傷害法》授權當局要求平台移除詐騙等刑事內容。

以軟法加測試工具建立信任；網上詐騙以法律賦權快速下架。

✓ 可借鑑

《網上刑事傷害法》正是§123(i)(b)立法賦權下架詐騙內容的參考；AI Verify式測試工具可配合政府採購的「安全和道德合規申報」。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡網上內容法例賦予部長較大指令權；香港推行時須配合更清晰的司法覆核和上訴程序，以維持法治和國際營商信心。

Singapore

Smart Nation 2.0 (2024); the Singpass digital identity covers almost all residents; a Model AI Governance Framework since 2019 and the AI Verify testing toolkit in 2022; the Online Criminal Harms Act (2023) lets authorities direct platforms to remove scam and other criminal content.

Trust built through soft law plus testing tools; scam content removed quickly under legal powers.

✓ Transferable

The Online Criminal Harms Act is the reference for §123(i)(b); an AI Verify-style toolkit could support procurement declarations.

✗ Not transferable

Singapore gives ministers broad directive powers; Hong Kong would need clearer judicial review and appeal to sustain the rule of law and business confidence.

內地

《互聯網信息服務深度合成管理規定》（2023年）要求深度合成內容顯著標識；《生成式人工智能服務管理暫行辦法》（2023年）；《人工智能生成合成內容標識辦法》2025年9月起施行；《未成年人網絡保護條例》（2024年）；杭州「城市大腦」自2016年起以數據優化交通和應急。

以專項規章快速回應深偽和生成式AI風險；城市大腦改善交通通行效率。

✓ 可借鑑

「AI生成內容強制標識」可作為香港深偽立法的一環；杭州由交通切入、逐步擴展，與§419由應急中心首階段接入的漸進做法一致。

✗ 不可照搬

內地以行政規章快速立規並由網信部門統一執法；香港立法須經法改會研究、公眾諮詢和立法會審議，並符合普通法和《個人資料（私隱）條例》框架。

Mainland China

Deep synthesis rules (2023) require clear labelling; interim measures on generative AI (2023); rules on labelling AI-generated content from September 2025; regulations protecting minors online (2024); Hangzhou’s City Brain has used data to optimise traffic and emergencies since 2016.

Targeted rules responded quickly to deepfakes and generative AI; the City Brain improved traffic flow.

✓ Transferable

Mandatory labelling of AI content could be part of Hong Kong’s deepfake law; Hangzhou’s start with traffic and gradual expansion mirrors §419’s phased start with the emergency centre.

✗ Not transferable

The Mainland regulates quickly by administrative rules with a single cyberspace regulator; Hong Kong must go through the Law Reform Commission, consultation and LegCo within the common law and the Privacy Ordinance.

韓國

2024年12月通過《人工智能基本法》（2026年1月生效），對「高影響AI」訂透明度和風險管理義務；另一方面，2011年起對16歲以下實施深夜網絡遊戲「關機令」，2021年因成效不彰、易被繞過而廢除。

框架立法、細節由施行令補充；「關機令」證明一刀切限制未成年人上網難以執行。

✓ 可借鑑

「風險分級」立法思路——只對高影響AI設義務；「關機令」的教訓可直接用於兒童社交媒體題：限制措施須可執行，並配合家長工具與平台責任。

✗ 不可照搬

韓國有統一居民登記號碼作實名驗證基礎；香港若以身份證號碼作網上年齡驗證，涉及私隱爭議，驗證機制須另行設計。

South Korea

The AI Basic Act, passed in December 2024 and in force from January 2026, sets transparency and risk duties for “high-impact” AI; separately, a 2011 midnight gaming shutdown for under-16s was scrapped in 2021 as ineffective and easy to evade.

Framework legislation with details in decrees; the shutdown law showed blanket limits on minors online are hard to enforce.

✓ Transferable

Risk-tiered law — duties only for high-impact AI; the shutdown lesson applies to the children’s social media question: limits must be enforceable and backed by parental tools and platform duties.

✗ Not transferable

Korea has a universal resident number for real-name checks; using HKID for online age verification would raise privacy concerns, so a different mechanism is needed.

日本

2021年設立數碼廳推動行政數碼化；「個人編號卡」推行時發生資料錯配（例如醫療保險和公款帳戶連結錯誤），引發信任危機；2025年通過《人工智能推進法》，以促進為主、未設罰則。

數碼化加速，但數據整合錯誤打擊信任；AI法以軟性框架為主。

✓ 可借鑑

數據整合的「質素控制」教訓：香港城市大腦和社福數據平台必須先做數據清洗與核對，並設市民查閱及更正機制。

✗ 不可照搬

日本行政數碼化起步較晚、以紙本為基礎；香港已有「智方便」等基礎，重點應是數據共享的授權和治理，而非由零開始。

Japan

A Digital Agency since 2021; the My Number card suffered data mismatches — wrongly linked health insurance and benefit accounts — that hit public trust; the 2025 AI Promotion Act is promotional, without penalties.

Digitalisation sped up but data errors damaged trust; the AI law is a soft framework.

✓ Transferable

A lesson in data quality: Hong Kong’s City Brain and welfare platform need data cleansing, reconciliation and a way for citizens to check and correct records.

✗ Not transferable

Japan started late from paper; Hong Kong already has iAM Smart, so its focus is authority and governance for data sharing, not starting from scratch.

政策目的

Policy objective

以人工智能與數據重組政府的工作流程，把公共服務由「市民跑部門」轉為「一網通辦」；同時完善人工智能與數據治理框架，保護關鍵基礎設施的網絡安全，並在便利、效率與私隱、安全之間取得公眾信任。

To re-engineer government work through AI and data, moving public services from citizens visiting departments to a single online gateway; and to build AI and data governance frameworks, protect the cybersecurity of critical infrastructure, and earn public trust in the balance between convenience and efficiency on one side and privacy and security on the other.

核心矛盾

Core tension

數字政府的效益來自數據打通：部門之間共享得越多，市民要重複提交的就越少。但數據越集中，私隱風險、監控觀感與單點失效的後果就越大。政府需要市民信任才能收集數據，而信任又取決於政府如何使用數據；這是一個必須先建立規則、再擴大應用的次序問題。

The gains of digital government come from joining data up: the more departments share, the less citizens must resubmit. But the more data is concentrated, the greater the privacy risk, the perception of surveillance and the consequence of a single point of failure. Government needs trust to collect data, and trust depends on how data is used; it is a question of sequence, with rules needed before applications are scaled.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

《保護關鍵基礎設施（電腦系統）條例》2026年1月1日生效。政府設「AI+與產業發展策略委員會」（財政司司長主持）和「AI效能提升組」（政務司副司長領導）；香港人工智能研發院今年運作。網上詐騙和深偽技術濫用成為執法焦點；律政司領導的工作組已開始檢視兒童使用社交媒體（包括AI互動）的風險。

Current position

The Protection of Critical Infrastructures (Computer Systems) Ordinance took effect on 1 January 2026. There is an AI+ strategy committee chaired by the Financial Secretary and an AI Efficiency Enhancement Team led by the Deputy Chief Secretary; the Hong Kong AI R&D Institute starts work this year. Online fraud and deepfake misuse are enforcement priorities, and a DoJ-led working group is reviewing the risks of children’s social media use, including AI interaction.

長期結構

二○二五年《施政報告》成立由政務司副司長領導的「AI 效能提升組」，統籌部門應用人工智能並研究重組工作流程；數字政策辦公室提供「AI 工具箱」，目標二○二六年內推出涵蓋 100 個政務環節的 AI 工具，並於二○二六／二七及二○二七／二八年度陸續推出「AI 旗艦項目」。現時 34 個局／部門營運 75 個流動應用程式，政府目標把服務遷移至「智方便」小程序平台以達致「一網通辦」，並在兩年內把應用程式數量減半。《保護關鍵基礎設施（電腦系統）條例》於二○二六年一月一日生效。數碼港人工智能超算中心提供 3 000 PFLOPS 算力。「香港網絡安全攻防演練」二○二五年有 25 個政府部門及 9 間公共機構作為防守藍隊，15 支紅隊參與攻擊。

Long-run structure

The 2025 Policy Address set up the Task Force on Enhancing Efficiency with AI under the Deputy Chief Secretary to coordinate departmental adoption and re-engineer workflows; the Digital Policy Office provides an AI toolkit, with AI tools covering 100 government processes targeted within 2026 and flagship AI projects rolled out in 2026-27 and 2027-28. Thirty-four bureaux and departments currently run 75 mobile apps; services are to move onto the iAM Smart mini-programme platform for a single gateway, halving the number of apps within two years. The Protection of Critical Infrastructures (Computer Systems) Ordinance took effect on 1 January 2026. The Cyberport AI Supercomputing Centre provides 3,000 PFLOPS. The 2025 Hong Kong Cyber Security Attack and Defence Drill involved 25 departments and 9 public bodies as blue teams against 15 red teams.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1部門數據孤島

各部門系統由不同年代、不同承辦商建立，數據格式與標準不一，而且部門有「數據屬於本部門」的慣性；技術上可以打通，行政與法律上卻缺乏共享授權。

1Departmental data silos

Systems were built in different eras by different contractors with inconsistent formats, and departments treat data as their own; joining up is technically possible but lacks administrative and legal authority to share.

2AI 決策的問責歸屬

當 AI 協助甚至作出審批、分配資源或風險評估的決定，一旦出錯，責任在使用部門、開發商還是批准採用的官員，現行制度未有清晰安排，市民亦缺乏知情與申訴渠道。

2Accountability for AI decisions

When AI assists or makes decisions on approvals, allocation or risk assessment, it is unclear whether the using department, the vendor or the approving official answers for errors, and residents lack channels to know or to complain.

3數字鴻溝

長者、低收入人士與少數族裔對數字服務的使用能力與設備較弱，若服務全面轉向網上，這些群體反而更難取得公共服務，與「以民為本」的原則衝突。

3The digital divide

Older people, low-income households and ethnic minorities have less capacity and equipment to use digital services; moving services wholly online makes public services harder for them to reach, contrary to a people-centred approach.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1財政影響：AI 與系統整合的前期投入大，但長遠可減少重複建設與人手密集的處理工作；在公務員編制零增長的背景下，數字化是維持服務水平的主要手段。

1Fiscal: AI and system integration require heavy upfront spending but reduce duplication and labour-intensive processing over time; with zero growth in the establishment, digitalisation is the main way to sustain service levels.

2社會影響：服務便利程度提升會令多數市民受惠，但若忽視數字鴻溝，受惠分布將偏向年輕與高學歷群體，擴大服務取得的不平等。

2Social: greater convenience benefits most residents, but if the digital divide is ignored, benefits skew towards the young and well educated, widening inequality of access.

3管治影響：數據使用的每一次失誤都會被放大為私隱或監控事件，信任一旦受損，將拖慢所有後續的數字政府項目，因此規則的建立必須先於應用的擴張。

3Governance: every misstep in data use is magnified into a privacy or surveillance incident, and damaged trust slows every subsequent project, so rules must come before applications scale.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

AI 與數據整合能在公務員編制零增長的前提下維持甚至提升服務水平，是應對人口老化與勞動力下降的必要手段。

站反方？這樣駁

在數據質素和人員能力未到位時，以AI填補縮編會令錯誤規模化；效率提升要以準確和可覆核為前提。

FOR 1

AI and data integration can sustain or raise service levels with zero growth in the establishment, a necessary response to ageing and a shrinking workforce.

Counter

If data quality and staff skills lag, using AI to fill posts scales up errors; efficiency must rest on accuracy and reviewability.

支持 2

「一網通辦」減少市民重複提交資料與奔走部門的時間成本，受惠最大的正是需要與多個部門打交道的弱勢群體。

站反方？這樣駁

「一網通辦」對會用手機的人最方便；弱勢群體若缺乏數碼能力，反而更難取得服務，必須保留實體渠道。

FOR 2

A single gateway saves residents the time of resubmitting information and visiting departments, and the greatest beneficiaries are vulnerable groups who deal with several departments.

Counter

One-stop online services help most those who use smartphones; vulnerable people without digital skills may find access harder, so physical channels must remain.

支持 3

政府率先大規模應用 AI，能為本地 AI 產業提供真實場景與參考案例，同時帶動產業發展。

站反方？這樣駁

政府作為首個用家有利產業，但採購若偏向大型供應商，本地中小型AI企業反而被排擠。

FOR 3

Large-scale government adoption provides local AI firms with real use cases and references, driving industry development at the same time.

Counter

Government as first user helps industry, but if procurement favours large vendors, local small AI firms are squeezed out.

反對／制約 1

數據集中與跨部門共享擴大私隱風險，一旦發生外洩或被濫用，損害是不可逆的，而且會嚴重削弱公眾對政府的信任。

站支持方？這樣駁

私隱風險可以用制度控制：用途限制、授權數據交換、私隱影響評估、存取紀錄和獨立監察；不共享數據同樣有代價——錯失及早介入。

AGAINST 1

Concentrating and sharing data across departments enlarges privacy risk; a leak or misuse causes irreversible harm and badly damages public trust.

Counter

Privacy risk can be controlled by design — purpose limits, consented exchange, privacy impact assessment, access logs, independent oversight; not sharing data also has costs, such as missed early intervention.

反對／制約 2

AI 決策缺乏透明度，可能複製訓練數據中的偏差，令部分群體在審批或資源分配中受到不公平對待，而市民難以質疑。

站支持方？這樣駁

官方AI Agent原則已訂明不得直接對公眾作決定、須人為監督和可追溯；偏差問題可以用定期審核和申訴渠道處理。

AGAINST 2

AI decisions lack transparency and may reproduce bias in training data, treating some groups unfairly in approvals or allocation in ways residents find hard to challenge.

Counter

Official rules already bar AI agents from deciding directly for the public and require human oversight and traceability; bias can be handled by audits and complaint channels.

反對／制約 3

過度強調數字化會令不熟悉科技的市民被邊緣化，實體服務縮減等同變相削減對長者與基層的公共服務。

站支持方？這樣駁

數字化節省的人手可以轉移到實體和外展服務，專門照顧不熟悉科技的市民；數字化與實體保底可以並行。

AGAINST 3

Over-emphasis on digital services marginalises those unfamiliar with technology, and reducing in-person services amounts to cutting services for older and lower-income residents.

Counter

Staff freed by digitalisation can move to counter and outreach services for those less comfortable with technology; digital-first and a physical safety net can coexist.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊工具：公開 AI 應用登記冊

公開各部門使用 AI 的項目、用途、數據來源與覆核機制，以透明度建立信任，成本低而效果直接。

Information: a public AI register

Publish departmental AI projects, purposes, data sources and review mechanisms, building trust through transparency at low cost.

標準工具：服務標準與數據標準

訂立統一的數碼服務標準與數據格式，新服務須通過用戶需要評審才可上線。

Standards: service and data standards

Set common digital service and data standards, with new services required to pass a user-needs assessment before launch.

架構工具：分散式數據交換

以授權、驗證與自攜為原則建立數據交換層，避免建立中央資料庫，降低單點失效與濫用風險。

Architecture: distributed data exchange

Build an exchange layer on consent, validation and user-held records rather than a central database, reducing single-point failure and misuse risk.

服務工具：數字共融與實體保底

保留實體與電話服務渠道作為保底，並以社區數字大使協助長者使用網上服務。

Service: digital inclusion with a physical floor

Keep in-person and telephone channels as a floor and support older residents through community digital ambassadors.

法律工具：AI 與數據使用的法定規範

最重的一級，就政府使用 AI 作出影響權益的決定訂立法定的知情、解釋與覆核權利，需與《個人資料（私隱）條例》的檢討一併處理。

Legal: statutory rules for AI and data use

The heaviest rung, creating statutory rights to be informed, to an explanation and to review where government AI decisions affect rights, to be taken forward with the review of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

部門以私隱或法律限制為由拒絕共享數據，令跨部門流程重組停滯。

化解

由 AI 效能提升組以行政主導方式指定優先的跨部門「人生事件」流程，並由律政司與私隱專員公署協助釐清法律依據；以市民授權模式處理有疑問的資料，而非以部門意願決定。

Challenge 1

Departments cite privacy or legal limits to refuse sharing, stalling cross-departmental redesign.

Mitigation

Have the AI task force designate priority cross-departmental life-event processes under executive leadership, with the Department of Justice and the Privacy Commissioner clarifying legal bases, and use citizen consent for doubtful cases rather than leaving it to departmental preference.

挑戰 2

AI 應用出現錯誤判斷，引發公眾對政府使用 AI 的信任危機。

化解

對影響市民權益的決定設立人手覆核與申訴渠道，先在低風險環節（例如查詢分流、文書處理）累積成效，再逐步擴展至審批環節；同時公開錯誤率與改善措施。

Challenge 2

An AI error provokes a crisis of trust in government use of AI.

Mitigation

Provide human review and a complaints channel for decisions affecting rights, build a record first in low-risk steps such as enquiry triage and document handling before extending to approvals, and publish error rates and remedial action.

挑戰 3

長者與基層市民未能適應數字服務，被批評為服務倒退。

化解

承諾在指定期間內不削減實體服務，並以使用率數據決定渠道調整；在社區中心與長者中心設置協助點，把數字化的節省部分用於加強實體服務的質素。

Challenge 3

Older and lower-income residents cannot adapt, prompting criticism that service has gone backwards.

Mitigation

Commit not to cut in-person services for a set period and adjust channels on usage data; set up help points in community and elderly centres and reinvest part of the savings from digitalisation in the quality of in-person service.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

私隱專員與關注團體

談用途鎖定與存取透明：數據只用於收集時的目的，市民可查閱誰曾存取自己的資料，並有獨立監察。

The Privacy Commissioner and advocacy groups

Speak of purpose limitation and transparent access: data used only for its collection purpose, residents able to see who accessed their data, with independent oversight.

公務員與工會

談工作重組而非裁員：AI 取代的是重複性工序，人手會轉往需要判斷與人際互動的工作，並提供再培訓。

Civil servants and unions

Speak of redesigning work, not cutting staff: AI replaces repetitive steps, people move to work requiring judgement and human contact, with retraining provided.

長者與社福界

談保底與協助：實體服務不會在指定期間內削減，並有社區支援協助使用網上服務。

Older residents and the welfare sector

Speak of the floor and of help: in-person services protected for a set period, with community support to use online services.

科技業界

談採購機會與標準清晰：政府的 AI 旗艦項目與統一標準為本地企業提供可預期的市場與參考案例。

The technology sector

Speak of procurement and clear standards: flagship projects and common standards give local firms a predictable market and reference cases.

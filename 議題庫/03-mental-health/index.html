精神健康

Mental Health
← 議題庫
指標措施方向取態比較出題目的矛盾現況問題影響論點與反駁方案挑戰游說句式詞彙

一句話方向

政府採用「綜合模式」織三道網：學校（三層應急機制）、網上（網上青年支援隊）、社區（地區康健中心「健康心靈先導計劃」）；並已接納「精神健康分層護理模式」框架——下一步是訂人員能力標準、培訓和部門分工。重點不是增加專科，而是把輕中度個案留在社區處理。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府應全面推行「精神健康分層護理模式」，讓輕度至中度個案由社區及基層醫療處理。試分析現況，提出落實分層護理的措施，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說精神科醫生及社工界支持。

出處：施政報告第七章第三節§313「促進精神健康」11項措施；五年規劃第二十章 FYP 5.21（加強社區心理和精神健康支援）、5.30；施政報告§123(ii)（兒童使用社交媒體）、§381（注意力不足／過度活躍症學生）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

An integrated approach weaves three nets: schools (the three-tier emergency mechanism), online (Cyber Youth Support Teams) and the community (the Healthy Mind Pilot Project at District Health Centres). The Government has endorsed the Stepped Care Model framework; the next step is competency standards, training and division of duties. The point is not more specialists but keeping mild and moderate cases in the community.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some argue the Government should fully implement the Stepped Care Model so that mild to moderate cases are handled in the community and primary care. Analyse the situation, propose measures to implement it, analyse the challenges and responses, and persuade psychiatrists and social workers to support it.

Source: Policy Address Chapter 7 Section 3, §313 (eleven mental health measures); FYP paras 5.21 and 5.30; Policy Address §123(ii) (children’s use of social media) and §381 (students with AD/HD)

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

出事後危機處理→三道網及早識別：學校、網上、社區

重點由事後輔導轉向事前識別；但識別之後要有地方轉介，否則只是「識別而無處安置」。

Crisis response after the event→Early identification through three nets: school, online, community

The emphasis moves from after-the-fact counselling to spotting risk earlier; but identification needs somewhere to refer to, or it becomes identification without placement.

以專科為中心→分層護理：按需要程度配對服務

家庭醫生、社區中心、輔導服務承擔輕中度個案，專科集中處理重症；分層成敗在於中層人手的能力標準。

Specialist-centred care→Stepped care: services matched to level of need

Family doctors, community centres and counselling take mild and moderate cases while specialists focus on severe illness; success depends on competency standards for the middle tier.

各部門各自提供→統一框架：能力標準與跨部門分工

分層護理框架第一次為醫療、社福、教育三個系統提供共同語言；但由誰牽頭、如何撥款仍待落實。

Each department on its own→A common framework: competency standards and division of duties

The framework gives health, welfare and education a shared language for the first time, but who leads and how it is funded are still to be settled.

去污名靠宣傳→把精神健康素養嵌入日常場景

體驗展覽、職場約章、家長資源套，把精神健康由「病」變成「日常能力」。

Destigmatising by publicity→Mental health literacy built into everyday settings

Exhibitions, the workplace charter and parent resources recast mental health from illness to an everyday capacity.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府把精神健康視為醫療、教育、社福的跨界議題，以基層和學校為前線，盡量不把輕中度個案推向專科；不設新法定機構，而是以分層護理框架統一標準。資源以試行、資助計劃和馬會等社會資源為主。對兒童使用社交媒體，政府取態審慎開放——先研究風險和是否需要立法，未有定論。

Government stance

The Government treats mental health as a cross-sector issue across health, education and welfare, with primary care and schools on the front line, keeping mild and moderate cases away from specialists where possible. No new statutory body; the stepped care framework sets common standards. Funding comes mainly through pilots, grant schemes and the Jockey Club. On children’s social media use, it is cautious and open — studying risks and whether legislation is needed, without a conclusion yet.

學校三層應急機制：及早識別，還是加重學校負擔？

支持的理據

學生大部分時間在學校，教師往往最早察覺異樣；三層機制讓高風險個案快速轉介，可減少悲劇。

質疑的理據

教師不是精神健康專業人員，額外責任增加壓力；若轉介後輪候仍長，機制只是「識別而無處轉介」；標籤效應亦令學生不願表露。

The school mechanism: early identification or a heavier load on schools?

Case for

Students spend most of their day at school and teachers often notice first; a three-tier mechanism speeds referral of high-risk cases and can prevent tragedies.

Case against

Teachers are not mental health professionals and the extra duty adds stress; if referral leads to long waits, the mechanism identifies without placing; labelling makes students less willing to speak.

分層護理：合理分流，還是變相降級？

支持的理據

大部分個案屬輕中度，適合由社區、家庭醫生和輔導服務處理；分層讓專科集中重症，縮短輪候。

質疑的理據

分層需要一支受訓、有能力標準的中層人手；否則輕中度個案在社區得不到足夠支援，惡化後又回流專科。框架已接納，但細節和時間表未定。

Stepped care: sensible triage or a disguised downgrade?

Case for

Most cases are mild to moderate and suit community, family doctor and counselling care; stepping lets specialists focus on severe illness and shortens waits.

Case against

Stepping needs a trained middle tier with competency standards; without it, mild cases get too little support, deteriorate and return to specialists. The framework is endorsed but details and timetable are open.

立法限制兒童使用社交媒體：保護，還是越界？

支持的理據

社交媒體與青少年焦慮、網絡欺凌、自我傷害內容有關，AI聊天互動帶來新風險；政府有責任設底線，不能只靠家長。

質疑的理據

年齡驗證涉及私隱和執行困難；一刀切禁令可能把兒童推向更隱蔽的平台；家長教育與平台責任（預設私隱、演算法透明）可能比禁令更有效。

Legislating on children’s social media: protection or overreach?

Case for

Social media is linked to youth anxiety, cyberbullying and self-harm content, and AI chat adds new risks; the Government must set a floor rather than leave it to parents.

Case against

Age verification raises privacy and enforcement problems; blanket bans may push children to less visible platforms; parent education and platform duties (default privacy, algorithm transparency) may work better than bans.

考生取態建議與紅線

建議取態是「支持及早識別和分層護理，但以人手和銜接為先決條件」：答題重點放在學校—社區—醫療之間的轉介斷層、中層人手的能力標準、由誰擔任個案統籌。社交媒體題可取「有條件支持規管」：先要求平台責任和預設保護，再視乎成效考慮年齡限制。紅線：不要把精神病康復者寫成「危險」，或以社區安全為由反對設施——官方樣本題正是精神健康設施，要處理鄰避但必須去污名；也不要建議強制篩查全體學生而不提私隱與標籤。

Recommended stance and red lines

Recommended stance: support early identification and stepped care, conditional on manpower and joined-up referral. Focus on the referral gaps between school, community and hospital, competency standards for the middle tier, and who co-ordinates each case. On social media, take a conditional regulatory line: platform duties and default protections first, then age limits depending on results. Red lines: do not describe people in recovery as dangerous or oppose facilities on safety grounds — the official sample question is itself about a mental health facility, so handle NIMBY while removing stigma; and do not propose compulsory screening of all students without addressing privacy and labelling.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

《國家精神健康及福祉策略》（2023年）採用分層護理模式；2024年推出全國精神健康熱線「1771」作為單一入口；長期以「精神健康—家庭醫生夥伴計劃」讓一般科醫生跟進穩定個案。

服務入口統一，輕中度個案較多在社區處理；專科資源集中重症。

✓ 可借鑑

先解決「不知道去哪裡求助」：單一熱線或入口；分層模式必須配人員能力標準——這正是§313(vii)的下一步，可直接引用作為參考。

✗ 不可照搬

新加坡由衞生部統籌醫療與相關社區服務，行政高度集中；香港分屬醫衞局、勞福局、教育局，須另建跨局統籌機制，不能以為設一條熱線就解決。

Singapore

The National Mental Health and Well-being Strategy (2023) adopts a tiered care model; the national mindline 1771 (2024) provides a single entry point; a long-running Mental Health–GP Partnership lets family doctors follow up stable cases.

A single entry point, more mild and moderate cases handled in the community, specialist resources focused on severe illness.

✓ Transferable

Solve “not knowing where to go” first with one hotline or entry point; tiered care needs competency standards for staff, exactly the next step under §313(vii).

✗ Not transferable

Singapore’s health ministry co-ordinates centrally; Hong Kong splits the work across three bureaux, so a cross-bureau co-ordination mechanism must be built — a hotline alone will not do it.

日本

2015年起，50人或以上的職場須每年為員工進行「壓力檢查」；2006年訂立《自殺對策基本法》；2021年設立孤獨・孤立對策擔當大臣，2024年《孤獨・孤立對策推進法》生效。

整體自殺人數由2003年高峰大幅回落；但學生及青少年自殺數字仍處高位，顯示成人職場措施未能覆蓋青少年。

✓ 可借鑑

職場壓力檢查制度化：香港《精神健康職場約章》屬自願，可先在政府和公營機構推行年度壓力評估，再以誘因推廣至大型僱主。

✗ 不可照搬

日本以法律強制企業；香港勞工法例以最低保障為原則，強制檢查會增加中小企成本，宜先以約章加誘因推行，而非立即立法。

Japan

Since 2015 workplaces with 50 or more staff must run an annual stress check; the Basic Act on Suicide Countermeasures dates from 2006; a minister for loneliness was appointed in 2021 and a loneliness act took effect in 2024.

Total suicides have fallen sharply from the 2003 peak, but student and youth suicides remain high, showing adult workplace measures do not reach the young.

✓ Transferable

Institutionalise workplace stress checks: Hong Kong’s charter is voluntary; start with annual assessments in the civil service and public bodies, then extend to large employers with incentives.

✗ Not transferable

Japan compels employers by law; Hong Kong labour law sets minimum standards, and a mandate would burden SMEs — charter plus incentives first, not immediate legislation.

韓國

2024年推出「全民心理投資支援計劃」，向有需要人士發放心理輔導券（約八節專業輔導）；國家健康檢查逐步擴大抑鬱篩查。

服務容量迅速擴大；但輔導人員資歷參差、服務質素不一成為問題。

✓ 可借鑑

以「券」購買私營輔導，能快速擴大輕中度個案的服務容量，與§313(vi)以公私營協作引入家庭醫生的方向一致。

✗ 不可照搬

香港臨床心理學家和輔導員沒有法定註冊（只有認可註冊計劃），若推行輔導券，須先解決服務提供者的資歷認證，否則重蹈質素參差的問題。

South Korea

A national psychological support programme (2024) issues counselling vouchers (about eight professional sessions) to those in need; national health screening is expanding depression checks.

Capacity grew quickly, but uneven counsellor qualifications and variable quality became problems.

✓ Transferable

Vouchers for private counselling expand capacity for mild and moderate cases quickly, in line with §313(vi) on public-private partnership.

✗ Not transferable

Hong Kong clinical psychologists and counsellors have no statutory registration, only an accredited registers scheme; vouchers would first need provider accreditation, or quality problems will follow.

台灣地區

2023年推出「年輕族群心理健康支持方案」，15至30歲可獲三次免費心理諮商，毋須先確診；2024年擴大至15至45歲。

名額很快用完，反映需求龐大；亦帶動社區心理諮商所參與。

✓ 可借鑑

免費、低門檻的「首三次」輔導作為早期介入，不需確診——可作為香港地區康健中心「健康心靈先導計劃」的入口設計。

✗ 不可照搬

台灣心理師有《心理師法》法定證照，私營諮商所網絡成熟；香港私營輔導市場的規模和規管不同，須先建立服務提供者名冊。

Taiwan region

A youth mental health support scheme (2023) gave people aged 15–30 three free counselling sessions without a diagnosis; in 2024 it was widened to 15–45.

Places ran out quickly, showing the scale of demand, and drew community counselling practices into the system.

✓ Transferable

Free, low-threshold first sessions without diagnosis are good early intervention and could shape the entry point of Hong Kong’s Healthy Mind Pilot Project.

✗ Not transferable

Taiwan’s psychologists are licensed under statute and private practices are well established; Hong Kong’s market and regulation differ, so a provider register must come first.

政策目的

Policy objective

及早識別、及早介入、去污名化，讓輕度至中度個案在社區得到支援，減少危機事件，並支持康復者重返學習與職場。

To identify and intervene early, to remove stigma, to support mild-to-moderate cases in the community, to reduce crisis events, and to help people in recovery return to study and to work.

核心矛盾

Core tension

需求急升且低齡化，但專科人手極度短缺；服務分散於醫療、社福與教育三個系統之間，沒有人統籌整個個案；而把服務放進社區必然觸發鄰避反應。

Demand is rising sharply and reaching younger age groups, yet specialist manpower is acutely short. Services are split across the health, welfare and education systems with nobody owning the whole case, and putting services into the community reliably triggers local opposition.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

精神健康服務分散在醫衞局（醫管局、基層醫療署）、勞福局（社署、精神健康綜合社區中心）和教育局（學校）三個系統。學生自殺風險、在職人士壓力、專科門診輪候長是三大焦點。本年《施政報告》沒有新設法定機構，而是在現有架構上以「分層護理」框架統一標準，並以試行計劃、馬會「行政長官社會資助計劃」等社會資源擴大服務。

Current position

Mental health services are split across three systems: the Health Bureau (HA, Primary Healthcare Commission), the Labour and Welfare Bureau (SWD, Integrated Community Centres for Mental Wellness) and the Education Bureau (schools). Student suicide risk, workplace stress and long specialist waits are the three focal points. This year’s Address creates no new statutory body; it uses the stepped care framework to set common standards on existing structures, and expands services through pilots and Jockey Club funding.

長期結構

學童與在職人士的求助個案持續增加；醫院專科門診輪候時間長，輕中度個案在等候期間無處可去；社會對精神疾病的污名仍然存在，令求助被延後至危機階段。

Long-run structure

Help-seeking among students and working adults continues to rise. Waiting times at hospital specialist clinics are long and mild-to-moderate cases have nowhere to go while they wait. Stigma persists, so help-seeking is deferred until the point of crisis.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1選址與社區接受度

綜合中心必須設在人口密集的地區，而那正是土地最緊張、居民最有組織的地方。反對很少以反對精神健康服務的形式出現，而是以治安與樓價的憂慮表達；由於這是風險感知而非事實問題，單靠數據無法化解。程序設計加劇了問題：先選址、後諮詢，令居民除了反對之外無事可做。

1Siting and community acceptance

Integrated centres must sit where people live, which is where land is most contested and residents best organised. Opposition rarely presents itself as opposition to mental health care; it is expressed as concern about safety and property values. Because the concern is one of perceived risk rather than of fact, evidence alone will not dissolve it. The sequence makes it worse: sites are chosen first and consulted on afterwards, leaving residents nothing to do but object.

2跨專業團隊組不成

綜合模式假設精神科護理、臨床心理、社工、職業治療與朋輩支援按共同個案計劃協作，但這幾個專業各自短缺，且分屬不同聘用與督導體系。向新中心招聘往往不是創造容量，而是把容量從本已緊絀的醫院服務搬過來。

2Multi-disciplinary teams cannot be assembled

The integrated model assumes psychiatric nursing, clinical psychology, social work, occupational therapy and peer support working to a common case plan. Each is in short supply and each is employed and supervised under different arrangements. Recruiting to a new centre therefore tends not to create capacity but to move it, most often from hospital services already under strain.

3入口太窄，識別太遲

學校與職場是最有效的接觸點，但教師與僱主既無能力也無誘因承擔識別角色；家庭往往是最後知道的一方。

3The entry points are too narrow and identification comes too late

Schools and workplaces are the most effective points of contact, yet teachers and employers have neither the capability nor the incentive to take on an identification role, and families are often the last to know.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

及早介入的成本遠低於危機處理與住院，是明確的財政理性。

站反方？這樣駁

這個比較假設及早介入真的能阻止危機，但輕中度個案中只有一部分會惡化。大規模及早介入可能把資源投放在本來就不會惡化的人身上，而真正高危的一群仍然接觸不到。

FOR 1

Early intervention costs far less than crisis response and admission; the fiscal case is unambiguous.

Counter

The comparison assumes early intervention prevents crisis, yet only a share of mild-to-moderate cases would ever deteriorate. Intervening at scale can direct resources to people who were never going to escalate, while those genuinely at risk remain out of reach.

支持 2

服務必須在社區裡看得見，去污名化才會真正發生。

站反方？這樣駁

可見度是雙刃劍。設施一旦出現任何事故，社區的恐懼會被放大並固化，令下一次選址更難。可見度應該是去污名化的結果，不是手段。

FOR 2

Stigma only falls when services are visible in the community itself.

Counter

Visibility cuts both ways. A single incident at a visible facility amplifies and hardens local fear, making the next site harder to secure. Visibility should be the result of destigmatisation, not the instrument of it.

支持 3

學校與職場的分層支援可以在專科人手不變的情況下擴大覆蓋。

站反方？這樣駁

把識別責任交給沒有專業訓練的人，可能造成大量誤判與過度轉介，反而擠塞本已不足的專業服務；而被錯誤標籤的學生所承受的傷害是不可逆的。

FOR 3

Tiered support in schools and workplaces widens coverage without adding specialist manpower.

Counter

Placing identification with untrained people invites misjudgement and over-referral, congesting services that are already short; and the harm to a student wrongly labelled is not reversible.

反對／制約 1

社區服務質素難以統一，專業水平參差會反過來損害信任。

站支持方？這樣駁

質素靠標準與監察，不靠集中。訂明最低團隊組成、服務規格與公開指標，加上定期審計，可以在分散提供下維持水平。集中於醫院的現狀同樣沒有解決質素問題，只是把它藏在輪候之後。

AGAINST 1

Quality is hard to standardise across community providers, and uneven standards damage trust.

Counter

Quality comes from standards and oversight, not from concentration. A minimum team composition, published service specifications and regular audit hold the line across dispersed providers. Concentrating in hospitals has not solved quality either; it has hidden it behind a waiting list.

反對／制約 2

及早介入的成效滯後且難以量度，撥款競爭中處於劣勢。

站支持方？這樣駁

可以用領先指標補位——輪候時間、轉介回應時間、危機個案的重複率都在一年內見效。要求十年成效才撥款，等於保證這件事永遠不會開始。

AGAINST 2

The benefits of early intervention are delayed and hard to measure, which weakens the case at每 allocation round.

Counter

Leading indicators fill the gap: waiting times, referral response times and repeat crisis contacts all show movement within a year. Requiring ten-year outcomes before funding guarantees the work never starts.

反對／制約 3

把識別責任推給學校與僱主，可能造成標籤化與過度轉介。

站支持方？這樣駁

把職責限定在「識別與轉介」而非「判斷與治療」，配合標準化工具與專業覆核，誤判風險可以控制。而現狀的代價是延誤，延誤的後果比誤判嚴重得多。

AGAINST 3

Pushing identification onto schools and employers risks labelling and over-referral.

Counter

Confine the duty to identification and referral rather than judgement and treatment, add standardised tools and professional review, and the risk of misjudgement is containable. The cost of the status quo is delay, and delay does more damage than a false positive.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

程序

把選址次序倒轉：先公開選址準則，邀請地區提出並排序候選地點，讓居民由反對者變成參與者。

Process

Reverse the sequence: publish the site-selection criteria first and invite districts to propose and rank candidate locations, so residents become participants rather than objectors.

共址

與已被社區接受的設施共用處所——健康中心、長者服務單位、社區會堂——同時降低資本成本與象徵性可見度。

Co-location

Share premises with facilities the community already accepts — health centres, elderly units, community halls — cutting both capital cost and symbolic visibility.

試行與指標

每所中心以三年試行期開展，訂明輪候、轉介回應與投訴指標，向地區聯絡委員會定期匯報。

Trial and indicators

Open each centre on a three-year trial with agreed indicators on waiting times, referral response and complaints, reported to a local liaison committee.

分層服務

以分層照顧模式（stepped care）分流：數碼初篩與熱線 → 朋輩與輔導 → 臨床心理 → 精神科，只有必要時才上移。

Stepped care

Triage through a stepped-care model — digital screening and helplines, then peer support and counselling, then clinical psychology, then psychiatry — escalating only when necessary.

供應側

擴大培訓學額，設立朋輩支援員的正式職級與階梯，並研究哪些服務可由受訓非專科人員在督導下提供。

Supply side

Expand training places, create a formal grade and career ladder for peer support workers, and examine which services trained non-specialists can deliver under supervision.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

居民的疑慮屬情緒與風險感知，數據說服力有限。

化解

以實證資料回應治安疑慮，同時正面承認情緒面；設立居民聯絡委員會與定期匯報，讓承諾可被檢驗。

Challenge 1

Residents' concerns are emotional and about perceived risk, so data persuades only so far.

Mitigation

Answer the safety question with evidence while acknowledging the feeling directly; set up a residents' liaison committee with regular reporting so the undertaking can be tested.

挑戰 2

團隊不齊就開中心，會提供比宣傳中更窄的服務，令轉介者回流醫院。

化解

訂明最低團隊組成，未達標不開；人手不足時採用樞紐—衞星安排，一所滿員中心支援數個定時服務點。

Challenge 2

Opening with incomplete teams delivers a narrower service than announced and pushes referrers back to hospital.

Mitigation

Set a minimum team composition below which a centre does not open, and use a hub-and-spoke arrangement where manpower is short.

挑戰 3

學校與僱主缺乏誘因承擔識別角色。

化解

提供免費培訓與現成工具包，把要求限於識別與轉介而非治療，並在職場層面以約章與表揚制度推動。

Challenge 3

Schools and employers lack any incentive to take on identification.

Mitigation

Provide free training and ready-made toolkits, confine the duty to identification and referral rather than treatment, and drive workplace uptake through a charter and recognition scheme.

游說要點

Stakeholder lines向誰說？他們在意什麼？

地區居民與區議會

先講選址準則與參與機制，再講設施本身；承諾試行期、指標與退場條件。

Local residents and district bodies

Lead with the criteria and the way they can take part, not with the facility; commit to a trial period, published indicators and exit conditions.

專業團體

強調分層照顧不是以非專業取代專業，而是讓專業處理真正需要專業的個案。

Professional bodies

Stress that stepped care does not substitute non-specialists for specialists; it frees specialists for the cases that need them.

學校與僱主

以減輕他們的實際負擔為賣點：現成工具、清晰轉介路徑、有人接手。

Schools and employers

Sell it as a reduction in their burden: ready tools, a clear referral route, and somebody who takes the case on.

傳媒

提供報道守則與可引用的正面康復故事，減少以個別事件強化污名。

The media

Offer reporting guidelines and quotable recovery stories to reduce the reinforcement of stigma by isolated incidents.

土地、房屋與居住質素

Land, Housing and Housing Conditions
← 議題庫
指標措施方向取態比較出題目的矛盾現況問題影響論點與反駁方案挑戰游說句式詞彙

一句話方向

公營房屋「提量、提速、提效、提質」進入收成期（2027-28年起五年平均每年逾35 000個）。重心由「追供應」轉向三件事：2030年內基本解決劣質劏房（簡樸房制度明年3月開始執法）；以「公營出租四成、資助出售三成、私人三成」構建多層階梯並鼓勵上流；以新發展區土地推動舊區重建。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府在2030年內基本解決劣質劏房問題的目標過於進取。試分析現況，提出確保目標達成的措施，分析執行上的挑戰（特別是住戶安置）及處理方法，並游說劏房業主遵從簡樸房制度。

出處：施政報告第七章第四節「完善多元安居體系」（§314–334）；五年規劃第五篇第二十一章（FYP 5.32–5.40、專欄33–35）；主要指標11–12（北都熟地與住宅）；§290(vi)（居屋95%按揭）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Public housing under “more, faster, more efficient, better” is entering its harvest years (over 35 000 units a year on average for five years from 2027-28). The focus shifts from chasing supply to three things: essentially ending substandard subdivided units by 2030 (enforcement of the Basic Housing Units regime starts next March); a multi-tier ladder split 40 per cent public rental, 30 per cent subsidised sale, 30 per cent private, with upward mobility encouraged; and using new development areas to drive redevelopment of old districts.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say the goal of essentially ending substandard subdivided units by 2030 is too ambitious. Analyse the situation, propose measures to achieve it, analyse the implementation challenges — especially rehousing — and responses, and persuade owners to comply with the Basic Housing Units regime.

Source: Policy Address Chapter 7 Section 4 (§314–334); FYP Part 5, Chapter 21 (paras 5.32–5.40, Tables 33–35); key indicators 11–12 (Northern Metropolis land and housing); §290(vi) (95 per cent HOS mortgages)

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

追求落成數字→供應進入收成期後轉向質素

數量有了「底氣」，政策語言轉向面積、設計、取締劣質劏房和「住大些」。

Chasing completion numbers→Turning to quality as supply arrives

With supply secured, the language turns to space, design, ending substandard units and larger flats.

容忍劏房作為「最後安全網」→以法定最低標準取締劣質劏房

簡樸房制度為分間單位訂下安全、消防、衞生標準，同時以簡約公屋和公屋供應承接住戶。

Tolerating subdivided units as the last resort→Statutory minimum standards to end substandard units

The Basic Housing Units regime sets safety, fire and hygiene standards, with Light Public Housing and public housing to absorb tenants.

重建靠逐個項目「拆建」→以新區土地支援舊區重建

跨區地積比轉移、「樓換樓」、額外地積比，突破舊區基建飽和的瓶頸；五年規劃明言要避免「單一項目式拆建思維」。

Project-by-project demolition and rebuilding→New-area land to support old-area renewal

Cross-district plot ratio transfer, flat-for-flat and extra plot ratio break the infrastructure bottleneck; the Plan explicitly rejects a single-project mindset.

資助出售單位長期鎖定→轉讓限制縮短，加快流轉

由「防炒賣」優先，轉為「流轉與上流」優先。

Subsidised flats locked in for long periods→Shorter restrictions, faster turnover

The priority moves from preventing speculation to turnover and upward mobility.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府以「供應主導」為核心，強調「掌握土地供應主導權」；公營房屋是基層的安全網和「底氣」。對劏房採取「先登記、後執法」、有序取締並提供人性化支援；對私人市場以賣地表調節供應，不作租務管制一類的直接干預。重建方面傾向「拆牆鬆綁」、引入市場力量。

Government stance

The Government’s core is supply-led policy — “taking the lead in land supply” — with public housing as the safety net. On subdivided units: register first, enforce later, orderly phase-out with humane support. For the private market it adjusts supply through the land sale programme and does not intervene directly, for instance through rent control. On redevelopment it favours removing barriers and bringing in market forces.

取締劣質劏房：保障安全，還是令基層無處可住？

支持的理據

劣質劏房的消防、衞生和結構風險危及性命；法定最低標準是基本保障；簡約公屋和公屋供應增加為取締提供「底氣」。

質疑的理據

合規單位減少可能推高租金；部分住戶（例如單身、新來港）不符合或未輪到公屋；執法後的過渡安置容量未必足夠。

Ending substandard subdivided units: safety or homelessness?

Case for

Fire, hygiene and structural risks in substandard units endanger lives; statutory minimum standards are a basic protection; more Light Public Housing and public housing give the policy its backing.

Case against

Fewer compliant units may push rents up; some tenants — singles, new arrivals — are ineligible or not yet reached on the waiting list; transitional capacity after enforcement may fall short.

縮短資助出售單位轉讓限制：促進流轉，還是變相套現？

支持的理據

業主可更早向上流動，騰出單位給下一批家庭；流轉加快令公共房屋資源用得更有效。

質疑的理據

資助房屋所含的公帑補貼可能在十年後被套現；削弱「自住」原意，並可能吸引投資性需求。

Shorter resale restrictions: mobility or cashing out?

Case for

Owners can move up sooner and free flats for the next families; faster turnover uses public housing assets better.

Case against

The public subsidy embedded in the flat can be cashed out after ten years, weakening the owner-occupation purpose and possibly attracting investment demand.

以新區土地換舊區重建、放寬地積比：加快更新，還是過度密集？

支持的理據

舊區基建飽和令重建困難；跨區轉移地積比和「樓換樓」突破瓶頸，令市建局財務可持續。

質疑的理據

額外地積比加重交通和社區設施負荷；「樓換樓」遷往北都令舊區居民失去社區網絡；檢視「七年樓齡」賠償準則會引起業主爭議。

Using new land and higher plot ratios for renewal: faster or too dense?

Case for

Saturated infrastructure blocks redevelopment; plot ratio transfer and flat-for-flat break the deadlock and keep the URA solvent.

Case against

Extra density strains transport and facilities; moving residents to the north breaks community networks; reviewing the seven-year-old-flat compensation basis will provoke owners.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持取締劣質劏房和供應主導的方向，把分數花在「過渡與安置」：不符公屋資格的住戶、租金上升、執法後去處、業主合規成本。紅線：不要主張全面租務管制或暫停執法；也不要否定簡約公屋——政府視之為成功的創新，可以提出優化（如社區配套、交通）而非否定。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support ending substandard units and the supply-led approach, and spend your marks on transition and rehousing: ineligible tenants, rent increases, where people go after enforcement, and owners’ compliance costs. Red lines: do not propose full rent control or suspending enforcement, and do not dismiss Light Public Housing — the Government regards it as a successful innovation; suggest improvements (community facilities, transport) instead.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

約八成居民住組屋（99年租約），公積金可用於供樓；2024年10月起新組屋分為「標準、優選、黃金」三類，較佳地段的單位設十年最低居住期，出售時須回撥部分補貼。

自置率高；按地段區分補貼防止好地段單位成為套利工具。

✓ 可借鑑

「按地段或補貼幅度分類＋出售時回撥補貼」——香港縮短轉讓限制時，可研究按補貼幅度設不同補價安排，平衡流轉與防套利。

✗ 不可照搬

組屋是新加坡的主流市場，公積金提供穩定的住屋儲蓄；香港資助出售房屋只佔少數，私人市場主導，也沒有公積金式的強制住屋儲蓄。

Singapore

About four in five residents live in HDB flats on 99-year leases, paid for with CPF savings; since October 2024 new flats are classed Standard, Plus or Prime, with better-located flats carrying a ten-year minimum occupation period and partial subsidy clawback on resale.

High ownership; location-based classes stop prime flats becoming arbitrage vehicles.

✓ Transferable

Classification by location or subsidy, with clawback on resale: as Hong Kong shortens restrictions, it could vary premium arrangements by subsidy level to balance turnover against arbitrage.

✗ Not transferable

HDB is the mainstream market and CPF provides housing savings; in Hong Kong subsidised sale flats are a minority in a private-led market with no compulsory housing savings.

上海／北京

規管「群租房」：上海規定每個房間的居住人數和人均居住面積下限，禁止把廚房、衞生間等改作居住；同時大規模供應保障性租賃住房。

群租大幅減少；但部分低收入租客被迫遷往更遠地區。

✓ 可借鑑

取締與供應同步：一邊定標準、一邊增加保障性租賃住房，與香港「簡樸房＋簡約公屋」思路一致；答題可強調「先有去處、再嚴執法」。

✗ 不可照搬

內地城市以街道和居委會網格化管理執法，行政觸達能力強；香港私人樓宇業權分散，執法依賴巡查和舉報，必須有足夠寬限期與登記。

Shanghai and Beijing

Group-rental rules: Shanghai caps occupants per room and sets a minimum area per person, banning kitchens and bathrooms as bedrooms, while supplying affordable rental housing at scale.

Group rentals fell sharply, though some low-income renters moved further out.

✓ Transferable

Enforcement and supply together — standards alongside affordable rentals — matches Hong Kong’s basic housing units plus Light Public Housing; stress “somewhere to go first, strict enforcement second”.

✗ Not transferable

Mainland cities enforce through street-level grid management with strong reach; Hong Kong’s fragmented private ownership relies on inspection and complaints, needing grace periods and registration.

台灣地區

以「社會住宅」和「包租代管」（政府補貼業者向私人業主租屋，再轉租予弱勢家庭）擴大可負擔租屋；2024年推出「囤房稅2.0」，提高多屋業主的房屋稅。

包租代管能快速增加供應而不需新建；但社會住宅興建進度落後目標。

✓ 可借鑑

包租代管善用私人空置單位：香港可由社福機構以政府資助向私人業主長租單位作過渡性房屋，承接執法後遷出的劏房住戶。

✗ 不可照搬

台灣房屋稅按持有數量累進，屬地方稅制；香港差餉和物業稅結構不同，以稅務手段打擊囤積涉及更大範圍的稅制改變。

Taiwan region

Social housing plus “sublease and management” schemes — subsidised operators lease private flats and sublet them to vulnerable households — expand affordable renting; a 2024 tax reform raised house tax on multiple-home owners.

Subleasing adds supply quickly without building, but new social housing lags its targets.

✓ Transferable

Use private vacant flats: Hong Kong NGOs could lease private units long-term with government support as transitional housing for displaced tenants.

✗ Not transferable

Taiwan’s progressive house tax is a local tax; Hong Kong’s rates and property tax differ, so using tax against vacant hoarding would mean wider tax reform.

日本（東京）

全國性分區法規讓符合規劃的住宅項目「按權利」獲批，審批毋須經公眾反對程序；東京新建住宅量長期位居發達城市前列。

供應彈性高，房價及租金相對穩定。

✓ 可借鑑

「按規劃即批」提高審批確定性——可用來支持北都專屬法例和精簡審批（§208–209）對加快供應的作用。

✗ 不可照搬

日本人口下降、土地私有且可自由開發；香港土地由政府批租、賣地收入是重要財政來源，放寬程度受財政和規劃制度制約。

Japan (Tokyo)

National zoning lets compliant housing proceed as of right without public objection procedures; Tokyo has long been among the most prolific builders of homes in the developed world.

Supply is elastic and prices and rents relatively stable.

✓ Transferable

As-of-right approval raises certainty — support for the Northern Metropolis bill and streamlined approvals (§208–209) as supply tools.

✗ Not transferable

Japan has a falling population and freehold land; Hong Kong’s leasehold system and reliance on land revenue limit how far deregulation can go.

為何可能：明年3月開始執法，時間表、安置、業主合規都是明年焦點；五年規劃把它寫成專欄目標。

  1. 現況寬限期登記反應理想；公屋輪候降至4.8年；簡約公屋提前完成。

政策目的

Policy objective

增加整體住屋供應、縮短公營房屋輪候時間、改善不適切居所的居住條件，並重建一條可負擔的置業階梯。

To increase total housing supply, shorten the wait for public rental housing, improve conditions in inadequate housing, and rebuild an affordable ladder into home ownership.

核心矛盾

Core tension

土地由規劃到入伙需時十年以上，而住屋需求是即時的。政府既要處理「現在住得差的人」，又要處理「十年後的供應」，兩者爭奪同一筆資源與同一批行政能力。

Land takes more than a decade to move from planning to occupation, while housing need is immediate. Government must deal both with people living badly now and with supply a decade hence, and the two claims compete for the same money and the same administrative capacity.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

公屋綜合輪候時間由本屆政府上任時的6.1年降至2026年6月的4.8年；一般申請約100 500宗、非長者一人申請約81 000宗，較高位分別下降逾30%及逾40%。3萬個簡約公屋單位提前完成。已收回約10 500個被濫用的公屋單位。簡樸房制度2026年3月起按「先登記、後執法」分階段推展，寬限期至2030年2月底。建造成本在國際指數排名首次跌出全球十大。

Current position

The composite waiting time for public rental housing has fallen from 6.1 years when this Government took office to 4.8 years in June 2026; general applications are about 100 500 and non-elderly one-person applications about 81 000, down over 30 and over 40 per cent from their peaks. All 30 000 Light Public Housing units were completed early. About 10 500 abused public flats have been recovered. The Basic Housing Units regime began in March 2026 on a “register first, enforce later” basis, with a grace period to the end of February 2030. Hong Kong has dropped out of the world’s top ten in an international construction cost index for the first time.

長期結構

公營房屋輪候時間長；相當數量的家庭居於分間單位等不適切居所；大型新發展區（北部都會區等）是中長期供應主力，但成本高、周期長；過渡性與簡約房屋屬短期紓緩措施，其長遠定位仍有爭議。

Long-run structure

Waiting times for public rental housing are long. A substantial number of households live in subdivided units and other inadequate housing. Large new development areas, including the Northern Metropolis, carry the medium-to-long-term supply, but they are costly and slow. Transitional and light public housing provide short-term relief, though their long-term place in the system remains contested.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1時序錯配：解決方案的周期長於問題的周期

新發展區十年後才能交樓，但輪候家庭的孩子在這十年間會長大並離開學齡。任何只講長遠供應的方案，都迴避了這十年。

1A timing mismatch: the solution is slower than the problem

New development areas deliver in a decade, but the children of households now waiting will have grown up and left school age by then. Any proposal that speaks only of long-term supply is silent about that decade.

2取締不適切居所的前提是替代供應存在

若在替代單位到位之前取締劏房，等於製造無家可歸；若永不取締，等於默許最惡劣的居住條件。這是一個先後次序問題，不是立場問題。

2Removing inadequate housing presupposes that a replacement exists

Clearing subdivided units before replacement supply is available creates homelessness; never clearing them condones the worst conditions. This is a question of sequencing, not of position.

3成本與財政周期

大型發展的資本開支前置、回報後置，在財政緊絀時最容易被延後，而延後又會推高最終成本。

3Cost and the fiscal cycle

Large developments front-load capital expenditure and back-load returns, so they are the first to be deferred when finances tighten — and deferral raises the eventual cost.

影響

Implications英文卷最拉分的一段

1在供應到位之前推行取締，只會把住戶推向更差、更隱蔽的居所，令問題從統計中消失而非從現實中消失。

1Enforcement ahead of supply pushes households into worse and less visible accommodation, removing the problem from the statistics rather than from reality.

2公營房屋輪候時間過長會削弱整個房屋階梯的可信度，連帶影響市民對置業與退休規劃的預期。

2Excessive waiting times undermine the credibility of the entire housing ladder and, with it, people's expectations about home ownership and retirement planning.

3若新市鎮的交通、學額與醫療配套滯後於入伙，早期居民會承受不成比例的代價，並形成長期的地區怨氣。

3If transport, school places and health services in a new town lag behind occupation, the earliest residents bear a disproportionate cost and lasting local resentment follows.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

住屋是最迫切的民生問題，增加供應是唯一的根本解。

站反方？這樣駁

供應是必要條件，不是充分條件。若新增供應落在遠離就業與交通的地區，或者類型與需求不匹配，輪候時間未必縮短——數字上增加，實際上無感。

FOR 1

Housing is the most pressing livelihood issue and increasing supply is the only fundamental answer.

Counter

Supply is necessary but not sufficient. If it lands far from jobs and transport, or in types that do not match need, waiting times need not fall: the figures rise while nothing changes for the household.

支持 2

訂立法定最低居住標準可為執法提供依據，並遏止最惡劣的單位。

站反方？這樣駁

標準的意義取決於執法能力與替代供應。兩者都缺位時立標準，只會把不合標準的單位推向更隱蔽的形式，並讓政府背上一個無法履行的法律責任。

FOR 2

A statutory minimum standard gives enforcement a basis and drives out the worst units.

Counter

A standard means what enforcement and replacement supply allow it to mean. Legislated without either, it drives substandard units into less visible forms and saddles government with a duty it cannot discharge.

支持 3

精簡法定程序可在不損害程序公義的前提下壓縮時間表。

站反方？這樣駁

程序公義的價值恰恰在於它會拖慢事情。每一次「精簡」都會被下一次援引為先例，累積起來會實質改變公眾參與的空間——這種改變不會在單一項目上顯現，只會在十年後顯現。

FOR 3

Streamlining statutory procedures can compress the timetable without sacrificing procedural fairness.

Counter

The value of procedural fairness lies precisely in the delay it imposes. Each streamlining becomes the precedent for the next, and the cumulative effect on public participation shows itself not in any single project but a decade later.

反對／制約 1

大型填海與新發展區成本高、回本期長，對財政構成長期壓力。

站支持方？這樣駁

延後同樣有成本，而且是複利的——建築成本上升、輪候家庭的社會成本、產業錯失的窗口。把資本開支與收入（賣地、稅基擴大）同期比較才是公允評估；只看支出一欄，必然得出不該做的結論。

AGAINST 1

Large-scale reclamation and new development areas are expensive with long payback, placing sustained pressure on public finances.

Counter

Deferral has its own cost, and it compounds: construction inflation, the social cost borne by waiting households, industrial windows missed. A fair assessment sets capital expenditure against the revenue and the widened tax base it generates; reading the expenditure column alone can only conclude against building.

反對／制約 2

取締不適切居所若無替代供應，受害的正是政策原意要保護的人。

站支持方？這樣駁

正因如此，方案的核心是次序而非原則。把取締時間表明文與替代供應掛鈎、先行登記並訂立安全底線，就能在不製造無家可歸的前提下推進——這是設計問題，不是放棄的理由。

AGAINST 2

Clearing inadequate housing without replacement harms precisely the people the policy sets out to protect.

Counter

That is exactly why the issue is sequencing rather than principle. Tie the enforcement timetable explicitly to replacement supply, register first and set a safety floor, and the work proceeds without creating homelessness. It is a design problem, not a reason to stop.

反對／制約 3

精簡程序可能削弱公眾參與與司法監察，並帶來新的法律風險。

站支持方？這樣駁

合併諮詢階段不等於取消諮詢。保留實質內容、公開每一階段的理由與回應、保留司法覆核途徑，程序保障並未消失；而現行程序的重複與拖延本身也在損害公眾利益——十年的等待也是一種不公義。

AGAINST 3

Streamlining may weaken public participation and judicial oversight, and creates new legal risk.

Counter

Merging consultation stages is not abolishing consultation. Keep the substance, publish the reasoning and the responses at each stage, preserve access to judicial review, and the safeguards remain. Duplication and delay damage the public interest too: ten years of waiting is itself an injustice.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊與登記

先建立強制登記制度，把不適切居所納入視線，為分階段標準與執法提供事實基礎。

Registration first

Introduce compulsory registration so that inadequate housing becomes visible, providing the factual basis for phased standards and enforcement.

分階段標準

由消防、電力、通風等安全底線開始，面積等標準分階段引入，並明文把取締時間表與替代供應掛鈎。

Phased standards

Begin with safety floors — fire, electrical, ventilation — and phase in standards such as floor area, with the enforcement timetable tied explicitly to replacement supply.

短期供應

過渡性與簡約房屋、市區重建的臨時安置、空置校舍與政府物業的改裝。

Short-term supply

Transitional and light public housing, temporary rehousing in urban renewal, and conversion of vacant school and government premises.

程序

精簡法定程序、合併諮詢階段、以標準設計加快審批，並設立跨部門統籌機構壓縮協調時間。

Process

Streamline statutory procedures, merge consultation stages, use standard designs to speed approval, and set up a cross-departmental body to compress coordination time.

配套門檻

為新發展區訂立「配套門檻」：每階段入伙人口對應必須到位的交通班次、學額與診所，並以臨時設施填補過渡期。

Infrastructure thresholds

Set thresholds for new development areas: each tranche of occupation must be matched by defined transport frequencies, school places and clinics, with temporary facilities bridging the gap.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

業主可能把合規成本轉嫁為加租，令住戶先受其害。

化解

同步引入租務規管與租金調整限制，並為受影響住戶提供優先安置承諾與搬遷支援。

Challenge 1

Landlords may pass compliance costs on as higher rents, so tenants bear the impact first.

Mitigation

Introduce tenancy regulation and limits on rent adjustment at the same time, with a rehousing commitment and relocation support for affected households.

挑戰 2

執法涉及進入私人物業的權限與人手。

化解

以登記制度取得基線資料，按風險分級巡查，並先處理安全風險最高的單位。

Challenge 2

Enforcement requires powers of entry into private premises and the manpower to use them.

Mitigation

Use registration to establish a baseline, inspect on a risk-graded basis, and deal first with the units posing the greatest safety risk.

挑戰 3

精簡程序容易被指削弱公眾參與。

化解

以「合併而非取消」為原則：諮詢階段合併但內容保留，並公開每一階段的理由與回應。

Challenge 3

Streamlining is easily attacked as a reduction in public participation.

Mitigation

Adopt the principle of merging rather than removing: consolidate consultation stages while preserving their substance, and publish the reasoning and responses at each stage.

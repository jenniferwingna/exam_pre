醫療衞生與基層醫療

Health Services and Primary Care
← 議題庫
指標措施方向取態比較出題目的矛盾現況問題影響論點與反駁方案挑戰游說句式詞彙

一句話方向

由「以醫院為中心」轉向「預防為重、基層為先」：收費改革把資助集中到「貧、急、重、危」；基層醫療以立法賦權、社區藥房和家庭醫生接住慢病；專科輪候以「截上游、放下游、中間分流」五年減半；同時以藥械審批改革和臨床試驗把醫療變成創科產業。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，公營醫療收費改革能令資源集中照顧最有需要的病人。試分析首階段改革的成效與不足，提出下一階段的優化措施，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說病人組織支持。

出處：五年規劃第五篇第二十章「深化醫療衞生體系改革」（FYP 5.20–5.31、專欄32）及第九章第四節「建設醫療創新體系」（專欄17–18）；施政報告第七章第三節（§297–312）、§134–136

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

From hospital-centred care to prevention and primary care first: the fees reform concentrates subsidy on poor, acute, serious and critical patients; primary care — with statutory powers, community pharmacies and family doctors — takes over chronic disease; specialist waits are to be halved in five years by “narrowing upstream, collaborating downstream, diverting midstream”; and drug-and-device approval reform plus clinical trials turn healthcare into an innovation industry.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say the public healthcare fees reform concentrates resources on the patients most in need. Analyse the results and shortcomings of the first phase, propose improvements for the next phase, analyse the implementation challenges and responses, and persuade patient groups to support it.

§312

政策方向：由……轉向……2026

Direction of travel寫「現況分析」時最有分量的一段

以醫院為中心、治療為主→預防為重、防治結合、基層為先

重整醫管局、衞生署與基層醫療署的分工；基層醫療署將獲立法賦權，並設立法定基層醫療服務提供者名冊。

Hospital-centred and treatment-led→Prevention first, integrated with treatment, primary care first

The division of work among the HA, DH and Primary Healthcare Commission is being reset; the Commission will get statutory powers and a statutory register of providers.

低收費、平均資助所有人→資助集中在「貧、急、重、危」

首階段改革提高非緊急服務收費，同時大幅擴大減免及設收費上限；政府強調「整體承擔不減」，改的是資助分配方式。

Low fees, subsidy spread evenly→Subsidy concentrated on poor, acute, serious and critical patients

The first phase raised non-urgent charges while widening waivers and capping annual spending; the Government stresses overall commitment is undiminished — what changes is how subsidy is distributed.

健康是政府的責任→市民是自身健康的「第一責任人」

策略文件把個人責任寫進政策語言，配合家庭醫生和個人化健康計劃；這是一個價值取向的轉變。

Health as the Government’s job→Individuals accept primary responsibility for their own health

Personal responsibility is written into policy language, supported by family doctors and personal health plans — a shift in values, not just in services.

醫療只是開支→醫療也是產業

第一層審批、臨床試驗、真實世界數據，把醫療系統變成吸引藥械企業和資本的平台。

Healthcare as cost only→Healthcare as an industry too

Primary evaluation, clinical trials and real-world data turn the health system into a platform for drug and device firms and capital.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

政府的定位是「改革而不削減」：公營醫療仍是全民安全網，但資助要精準；需求管理（收費、分流）與供應擴張（醫院、人手、基層）並行。對專業界別的取態比以往主動——醫委會改革、非本地醫生、收費透明度規例都由政府主導推動。

Government stance

The Government frames it as reform, not retrenchment: public healthcare remains the safety net for all, but subsidy must be targeted; demand management (fees, diversion) and supply expansion (hospitals, manpower, primary care) proceed together. It is more assertive with the professions than before: Medical Council reform, non-local doctors and price-transparency rules are all Government-led.

收費改革：減少濫用，還是令基層卻步？

支持的理據

急症室非緊急個案明顯減少；減免人數由不足14 000增至逾25萬，資源流向最需要的人；收費上限避免「因病致貧」。

質疑的理據

「緊急與否」由病人事前判斷，低收入但不符合減免資格的夾心層可能延誤求醫；私營基層收費不透明，未必能承接被分流的病人。

Fees reform: curbing misuse, or deterring the poor?

Case for

Non-urgent A&E cases have fallen; waivers rose from under 14 000 to over 250 000, directing resources to those most in need; the cap prevents impoverishment through illness.

Case against

Patients judge urgency in advance; the low-income “sandwich” group just above waiver thresholds may delay care; private primary care is opaque on price and may not absorb diverted patients.

專科輪候減半：靠分流，還是靠人手？

支持的理據

大量穩定舊症佔用專科名額，分流至基層可釋放容量；AI分流和跨專業診所能提高效率。

質疑的理據

基層醫療人手本身不足，分流只是把輪候轉移；若共同治理網絡的資助不足，會形成「下游塞車」。

Halving specialist waits: diversion or manpower?

Case for

Stable follow-up cases occupy specialist slots; moving them to primary care frees capacity, and AI triage and multidisciplinary clinics raise efficiency.

Case against

Primary care is short-staffed too; diversion may simply move the queue, and without adequate co-care subsidies it creates a downstream bottleneck.

醫委會改革與非本地醫生：保障質素還是削弱專業自主？

支持的理據

醫生比例要由2.25增至2.43，本地培訓追不上；專業自律需要更多問責和透明度。

質疑的理據

專業自主是醫療質素的保障；政府主導改革可能被視為干預，影響士氣；非本地醫生的語言和制度適應亦需時間。

Medical Council reform and non-local doctors: quality or professional autonomy?

Case for

The ratio must rise from 2.25 to 2.43 and local training cannot keep up; self-regulation needs more accountability and transparency.

Case against

Professional autonomy underpins quality; Government-led reform may be read as interference and hit morale; non-local doctors need time to adapt to language and systems.

考生取態建議與紅線

支持「基層為先、精準資助」的方向，把分數花在「分流的承接能力」：基層人手、公私營協作的付費機制、收費透明度和病人信任。紅線：不要主張全面回到低收費，也不要把改革寫成「削減福利」——官方用語是「資源重新配置」「整體承擔不減」。

Recommended stance and red lines

Support “primary care first, targeted subsidy” and spend your marks on whether the system can absorb diverted patients: primary care manpower, payment design for public-private partnership, price transparency and patient trust. Red lines: do not argue for a blanket return to low fees, and do not call the reform a welfare cut — the official framing is reallocation with undiminished overall commitment.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

新加坡

「健康SG」（Healthier SG，2023年）：居民登記一名家庭醫生並制訂個人健康計劃，政府按登記人數向診所支付年費；醫療融資以「3M」為骨幹——保健儲蓄（MediSave）、終身健保（MediShield Life）、保健基金（MediFund）。

登記人數逐年上升，慢病管理轉向家庭醫生；個人以儲蓄分擔部分費用，政府負擔可預測。

✓ 可借鑑

「登記＋按人頭年費」令家庭醫生有誘因做預防而非多看症；香港共同治理網絡可由按次資助改為按登記人數或成效付費。

✗ 不可照搬

MediSave 依賴中央公積金的強制儲蓄；香港沒有醫療儲蓄戶口，照搬等於新設供款，政治難度極高。

Singapore

Healthier SG (2023): residents enrol with one family doctor and agree a health plan; the Government pays clinics an annual fee per enrolled resident. Financing rests on the “3Ms”: MediSave, MediShield Life and MediFund.

Enrolment has grown each year and chronic care is shifting to family doctors; individuals share costs from savings, making public spending more predictable.

✓ Transferable

Enrolment plus an annual per-person fee rewards prevention rather than volume; Hong Kong’s co-care network could move from per-visit subsidy to payment per enrolee or by outcomes.

✗ Not transferable

MediSave depends on CPF compulsory savings; Hong Kong has no medical savings accounts, and copying it would mean a new contribution with very high political cost.

台灣地區

全民健康保險（1995年）屬單一支付者制度，行政成本低、就醫便利；「健保醫療資訊雲端查詢系統」讓醫生查閱病人跨院的用藥與檢查紀錄。

重複用藥和檢查減少；但就醫次數偏高，給付偏低令內、外、婦、兒、急診科人手流失。

✓ 可借鑑

跨院雲端病歷正是「醫健通+」的方向；台灣經驗說明私營醫護上載紀錄需要規定或誘因，單靠自願不足。

✗ 不可照搬

單一支付的社會保險需全民供款；台灣亦證明低自付會推高使用量——與香港收費改革「減少非緊急使用」的方向相反。

Taiwan region

National Health Insurance (1995) is single-payer, with low administrative cost and easy access; the NHI MediCloud lets doctors see patients’ medication and test records across hospitals.

Duplicate prescriptions and tests fell, but visit rates are high and low reimbursement has driven doctors out of internal medicine, surgery, obstetrics, paediatrics and emergency care.

✓ Transferable

Cross-provider cloud records are exactly where eHealth+ is heading; Taiwan shows private providers upload records only with a requirement or an incentive.

✗ Not transferable

Single-payer insurance requires universal contributions, and Taiwan shows low co-payments push utilisation up — the opposite of Hong Kong’s aim of reducing non-urgent use.

韓國

2024年宣布醫學院每年增收約2 000名學生，引發實習及住院醫生大規模離職抗議逾一年，政府其後把2026年度招生名額回復至增加前水平。

急症及手術服務受影響，改革被迫退讓——醫療人手改革若缺乏與專業界別協商，代價極高。

✓ 可借鑑

人手改革要分階段、先協商、配合培訓容量；香港以特別註冊引入非本地醫生、分批擴大學額，屬較漸進的路線，可在答題時以此作對照。

✗ 不可照搬

韓國醫生多在私營體系按服務收費，集體行動的槓桿大；香港公營醫生是醫管局僱員，勞資框架不同，不能直接類比其衝突模式。

South Korea

In 2024 the Government announced about 2 000 more medical school places a year; trainee doctors walked out en masse for over a year, and the Government later reverted the 2026 intake to its previous level.

Emergency and surgical services were disrupted and the reform was reversed: manpower reform without negotiation with the profession is extremely costly.

✓ Transferable

Manpower reform should be phased, negotiated and matched to training capacity; Hong Kong’s special registration of non-local doctors and staged expansion of places is the more gradual route, a useful contrast in an answer.

✗ Not transferable

Korean doctors are mostly private and paid per service, giving collective action great leverage; Hong Kong’s public doctors are HA employees under a different employment framework, so the conflict does not map directly.

日本

大型醫院對沒有轉介信而直接求診的病人收取額外「選定療養費」（初診最少約7 000日圓），以價格引導病人先看診所。

分流效果明顯；但亦被批評變相懲罰沒有固定家庭醫生的人。

✓ 可借鑑

政策目的

Policy objective

把醫療系統的重心由「治病」移向「預防」，在社區處理可以在社區處理的需求，紓緩公立醫院壓力，並為長期醫療開支尋找可持續的融資安排。

To shift the centre of gravity of the health system from treatment to prevention, to deal in the community with what the community can handle, to relieve pressure on public hospitals, and to find a sustainable way of financing health expenditure over the long term.

核心矛盾

Core tension

公營體系承擔絕大部分住院服務但資源有限；私營容量存在但價格高；市民的就醫習慣是有病才求醫，而價格訊號正好鼓勵他們直接使用最昂貴的入口。

The public system carries the overwhelming share of inpatient care on limited resources; private capacity exists but is expensive; and the public's habit is to seek care only when ill — a habit that current price signals actively reward, since the cheapest point of entry is also the most costly to run.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

醫生比例為每千人口2.25名，五年規劃目標2.43名；人均公共醫療經常開支約18,000元（2024-25年度）。公營系統承擔大部分住院服務，但基層醫療以私營為主、收費不透明。2026年1月推行首階段收費改革；「第一個醫院發展計劃」2031年完成後病床約35 000張。醫健通已有超過650萬人登記（佔人口87%）。第三所醫學院（科大）2028年收生。

Current position

There are 2.25 doctors per 1 000 people against a Plan target of 2.43; per-capita public current health spending is about $18,000 (2024-25). The public system provides most inpatient care, but primary care is mainly private and opaque on price. The first phase of the fees reform began in January 2026; when the First Hospital Development Plan completes in 2031 there will be about 35 000 public beds. Over 6.5 million people (87 per cent) are registered with eHealth. A third medical school (HKUST) takes students from 2028.

長期結構

人口老化令慢性病患者基數持續擴大，而慢性病的成本主要來自併發症，即可預防的部分。基層醫療的制度基礎（家庭醫生、電子病歷、轉介鏈）仍在建立中；醫護人手緊絀且流失，公私營薪酬差距是結構性因素。

Long-run structure

An ageing population steadily enlarges the base of chronic disease, and the cost of chronic disease lies chiefly in complications — that is, in the preventable part. The institutional foundations of primary care — family doctors, shared electronic records, a working referral chain — are still being built. Clinical manpower is tight and attrition continues, with the public-private pay gap a structural driver.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1誘因錯置：理性的病人仍然會去急症室

急症室收費低、隨到隨看、不需預約與轉介；社區服務則要預約、要付費、要經家庭醫生。在這組價格與便利訊號下，分流失敗不是市民不理性，而是市民太理性。

1Misaligned incentives: the rational patient still goes to A&E

Emergency departments are cheap, open without appointment and need no referral, whereas community services require booking, payment and a family doctor. Given that combination of price and convenience signals, the failure to divert patients is not irrationality on the public's part — it is rationality.

2守門人缺位，轉介鏈斷裂

沒有普及的家庭醫生註冊制度，社區服務、私家醫生與醫院之間缺乏共用病歷與協作安排；病人在系統之間重複檢查、重複敍述病史。

2No gatekeeper, so the referral chain is broken

Without a general system of family-doctor registration, community services, private practitioners and hospitals lack shared records and agreed protocols. Patients are re-investigated and re-interviewed as they move between them.

3預防的回報滯後，撥款難以證明

慢病管理的效益要十年才顯現，而公共財政的審批周期是一年。結果是預防長期得不到與其價值相稱的資源。

3The returns to prevention lag, so funding is hard to justify

The benefits of chronic-disease management appear over a decade, while the budget cycle is annual. Prevention therefore persistently receives less than its value warrants.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

及早篩查與慢病管理可大幅減少併發症與住院，是少數同時改善結果與降低成本的政策。

站反方？這樣駁

臨床上成立，財政上卻要十年才兌現，而期內必須同時支付兩套系統。若轉型期的額外開支擠壓現有服務，短期受害的正是今天的病人。

FOR 1

Early screening and chronic-disease management substantially reduce complications and admissions — one of the few interventions that improves outcomes and lowers cost at the same time.

Counter

The clinical case holds, but the fiscal return takes a decade while both systems must be funded in parallel. If transitional costs squeeze existing services, the people who bear it are today's patients.

支持 2

公私營協作可即時動用私營容量，不必等待公營設施落成。

站反方？這樣駁

協作把公帑帶進一個政府無法控制價格的市場，長遠有推高整體醫療通脹的風險；而私營容量會流向最有利可圖的服務，未必是最需要的服務。

FOR 2

Public-private partnership mobilises private capacity immediately, without waiting for public facilities to be built.

Counter

Partnership brings public money into a market whose prices government does not control, with a long-run risk of medical inflation; and private capacity gravitates to the most profitable services, not necessarily the most needed.

支持 3

引入非本地培訓醫生可在短期內補充人手，同時維持註冊與監管把關。

站反方？這樣駁

流失的原因是公營體系的工作條件與晉升前景。不處理這一點，新增人手同樣會流走，等於不斷往漏桶注水。

FOR 3

Admitting non-locally trained doctors can supplement manpower quickly while registration and regulatory safeguards are retained.

Counter

Attrition is driven by conditions and prospects inside the public system. Left unaddressed, newly recruited staff will leave in the same way — water poured into a leaking bucket.

反對／制約 1

預防的成效難以量度，容易淪為數人頭的指標遊戲。

站支持方？這樣駁

難量度不等於不可量度。血糖血壓達標率、併發症發生率、急症重複求診率都是現成而且國際通用的指標。真正的問題是財政周期與回報期不匹配，這應該用多年期撥款解決，而不是用「不做」解決。

AGAINST 1

The effects of prevention are hard to measure and the exercise easily degenerates into counting heads.

Counter

Hard to measure is not impossible to measure: control rates for blood glucose and blood pressure, complication rates and repeat attendance are standard indicators. The real mismatch is between the budget cycle and the payback period, which calls for multi-year funding rather than inaction.

反對／制約 2

公私營協作的定價與質素監管複雜，容易被批評為補貼私營市場。

站支持方？這樣駁

複雜是設計問題。按人頭或按成效付費、公開的服務規格與定期審計，都能把風險控制在可接受範圍。而完全不用私營容量的代價是輪候時間——那是市民直接承受的成本。

AGAINST 2

Pricing and quality assurance in public-private partnership are complex, and the arrangement is easily attacked as a subsidy to the private market.

Counter

Complexity is a design problem. Capitation or outcome-based payment, published service specifications and regular audit keep the risk within bounds. The cost of refusing to use private capacity is waiting time, which patients pay directly.

反對／制約 3

調整收費會被視為向病人開刀，社會接受度極低，尤其涉及長者。

站支持方？這樣駁

接受度取決於配套與說法。豁免、自付上限與專款專用同步公布，並只針對非緊急服務，爭議可以大幅收窄。何況維持現狀也有代價——輪候時間是一種隱性收費，只是由最沒有選擇的人支付。

AGAINST 3

Adjusting fees is read as making patients pay and has very low public acceptability, especially where older people are concerned.

Counter

Acceptability depends on the package and the framing. Exemptions, an out-of-pocket cap and earmarking announced together, applied only to non-urgent attendance, narrow the dispute considerably. The status quo is not free either: waiting time is a charge, paid by those with the fewest options.

方案方向

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

資訊

以風險分層的個人化提示取代普遍宣傳；把篩查帶到工作場所、學校與長者中心。

Information

Replace general publicity with risk-stratified personal prompts, and take screening to workplaces, schools and elderly centres.

價格訊號

調整非緊急急症服務的收費，同時以慢病管理的資助抵銷——引導而非懲罰。

Price signals

Adjust charges for non-urgent emergency attendance while offsetting them with subsidies for chronic-disease management: steer rather than punish.

制度

建立家庭醫生註冊名冊與雙向轉介協議；電子健康紀錄全面互通。

Institutions

Establish a family-doctor register with two-way referral protocols, and make electronic health records fully interoperable.

購買服務

以按人頭或按成效付費的方式向私營界別購買慢病管理，而非按次數付費。

Purchasing

Buy chronic-disease management from the private sector on a capitation or outcome basis rather than fee-for-service.

人手

擴大本地培訓、開放有限度的非本地培訓醫生途徑，並改善公營體系的執業條件以減少流失。

Manpower

Expand local training, open a limited pathway for non-locally trained doctors, and improve working conditions in the public system to slow attrition.

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

調整收費的政治阻力極大。

化解

必須同步設立收入與資產豁免、年度自付上限及慢病藥物豁免，並把新增收入明文專用於基層醫療。

Mitigation

Pair it with income and asset exemptions, an annual out-of-pocket cap and exemptions for chronic-disease medication, and earmark the revenue for primary care in explicit terms.

挑戰 2

私家醫生未必願意承擔守門人角色與行政負擔。

化解

以簡化的電子平台降低行政成本，並讓註冊帶來穩定病人來源而非額外工作。

Mitigation

Cut administrative cost with a simple electronic platform and make registration a source of steady patient flow rather than extra work.

強調把關不變、註冊制度不變，新途徑只補充而不取代本地培訓。

The medical profession

Stress that gatekeeping and registration are unchanged and that any new pathway supplements rather than replaces local training.

病人組織

以輪候時間與可負擔性的具體承諾回應，並設立獨立投訴與檢討渠道。

Patient groups

Answer with concrete commitments on waiting times and affordability, plus an independent complaints and review channel.

財政當局

以避免的住院日數與併發症成本作論據，並提出可量度的中期指標。

The fiscal authorities

Argue from bed-days and complication costs avoided, and offer measurable interim indicators.

公眾

一句話訊息：把醫院留給真正需要醫院的人。

The public

One message: keep hospitals for the people who really need hospitals.

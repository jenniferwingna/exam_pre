一句話方向

治理改革有三條線：一是制度問責——部門首長責任制、兩級調查、評核「賞罰分明」、簡化紀律程序；二是城市安全——宏福苑大火後優先修訂三條條例、研究圍標刑事化、政府首次為業主提供招標評標服務，並以AI城市大腦和物聯網提升防災；三是地區與服務——1823設牽頭部門和首長級監察，以AI把「投訴後治理」變成「未投訴已治理」。五年規劃再加一層：「五年規劃統領、年度施政落實、財政預算支持、年度述職匯報」的實施機制。

最可能的考法

有意見認為，政府應更深入介入私人樓宇大型維修工程，以杜絕圍標和保障安全。你是否同意？試分析現況，提出措施，分析執行挑戰及處理方法，並游說業主立案法團支持。

出處：施政報告第八章「秉持改革思維 統籌發展和安全」（§418–443）、§7（以結果為目標、全政府動員機制）、§13；五年規劃第六篇第二十六至二十八章（FYP 6.1–6.26、專欄40–43）及第七篇「強化五年規劃實施保障機制」（FYP 7.1–7.5）

DIRECTION IN ONE LINE

Governance reform runs on three lines. Institutional accountability: heads-of-department accountability, two-tier investigations, appraisal with clear reward and punishment, simpler discipline. Urban safety: after the Wang Fuk Court fire, priority amendments to three ordinances, a study on criminalising bid-rigging, government tender services for owners for the first time, and the AI City Brain and IoT for disaster prevention. District services: lead departments and directorate oversight for 1823, with AI moving from managing complaints to fixing problems before they arise. The Plan adds an implementation chain: five-year plan, annual Policy Address, Budget support and annual reporting.

MOST LIKELY QUESTION

Some say the Government should intervene more deeply in major private building maintenance to stamp out bid-rigging and protect safety. Do you agree? Analyse the situation, propose measures, analyse the challenges and responses, and persuade owners’ corporations to support them.

取態：政府怎樣想、爭議在哪、你怎樣站2026

Stances and fault lines
政府取態

以「行政主導」為原則，強調「以結果為目標」「敢於擔當、善於作為」；樓宇安全明確轉向政府高度介入招標程序並嚴懲圍標；公務員管理轉向績效與問責，同時嚴控編制、以數字化和AI提升效率；地區治理強調「民有所呼，我有所應」。

Government stance

Executive-led governance with a results orientation and officials who take ownership; in building safety, a clear turn to close government involvement in tendering and tough action on bid-rigging; in the civil service, performance and accountability alongside a tight establishment and digital efficiency; in districts, responsiveness to residents’ calls.

政府「高度介入」私人樓宇大維修招標：保障業主，還是越界？

支持的理據

宏福苑事件暴露業主在專業、資訊和組織上的弱勢；政府把關可打擊圍標、保障安全；預審名單有執法機關背景審查。

質疑的理據

政府責任的邊界：工程出事時政府是否要負責；行政成本和人手；市場可能過度依賴政府名單而減少競爭。

Close government involvement in private maintenance tenders: protecting owners or overreach?

Case for

The fire exposed owners’ weakness in expertise, information and organisation; government gatekeeping tackles bid-rigging and protects safety, with law-enforcement vetting of listed firms.

Case against

Where does government responsibility end if works go wrong? Administrative cost and staffing; the market may lean on the official list and lose competition.

提高業主大會門檻：防止操控，還是令維修更難？

支持的理據

少數人以授權票操控大型採購決定，是圍標的關鍵環節；提高出席和投票門檻、完善授權票制度，令決定更具代表性。

質疑的理據

舊樓業主參與度本來偏低，門檻提高可能令必要維修無法通過，失修問題惡化。

Higher thresholds for owners’ meetings: preventing capture or blocking repairs?

Case for

Capture of large procurement decisions through proxies is central to bid-rigging; higher attendance and voting thresholds and better proxy rules make decisions more representative.

Case against

Participation in old buildings is already low; higher thresholds may block necessary repairs and worsen decay.

公務員評核「區分表現」：提升效率，還是打擊士氣？

支持的理據

評核長期「人人良好」，無法反映表現；賞罰分明可鼓勵擔當和創新。

質疑的理據

可能造成內部競爭和主觀評分；縮編下壓力更大；需要公平程序和上訴。

Differentiated appraisal: efficiency or lower morale?

Case for

Ratings have long been uniformly good and meaningless; clear rewards and penalties encourage ownership and innovation.

Case against

It may breed rivalry and subjectivity and add pressure amid cuts; fair procedures and appeals are needed.

考生取態建議與紅線

AO考生尤其要熟悉這一題：支持「以結果為目標、問責與擔當並重」。樓宇維修題取「政府把關＋業主自主」的平衡：政府提供招標服務和名單，決定權仍在業主，並以資助降低參與成本；門檻分類設計。紅線：不要把宏福苑事件寫成對政府的全面批評——談制度漏洞和改革；獨立委員會報告公布前，不要「定責」或推斷調查結果。

Recommended stance and red lines

AO candidates should know this topic best. Support a results orientation with accountability and ownership together. On building maintenance, balance government gatekeeping with owner autonomy: government tender services and lists, decisions still with owners, subsidies to lower the cost of taking part, and thresholds that vary by type of decision. Red lines: do not turn the fire into a blanket attack on the Government — discuss system gaps and reform; and do not assign blame or pre-empt findings before the independent committee reports.

區域比較：可借鑑與不可照搬2026

Comparable places文化相近地區；引用時用「台灣地區」「澳門特區」

北京「接訴即辦」

2019年起以12345熱線為總入口，市民訴求直接派單到街道鄉鎮並限時回應，按「響應率、解決率、滿意率」考核排名；對高頻問題開展「主動治理、未訴先辦」。

基層回應速度提升；但排名考核亦被批評令基層疲於應付個別訴求。

✓ 可借鑑

「未訴先辦」與§434(iii)「未投訴已治理」幾乎一致；以「解決率」而非「轉介率」作指標並公開部門表現，可提升1823的「辦得成」。

✗ 不可照搬

北京以一體化的街道體系落實派單；香港部門按法定職權運作，民政事務處無權指揮其他部門，必須以牽頭部門和上報機制處理。

Beijing: “respond to complaints immediately”

Since 2019 the 12345 hotline is the single entry point; cases go straight to sub-districts with deadlines, ranked on response, resolution and satisfaction; recurring problems are tackled proactively before complaints.

Frontline response improved, though rankings are criticised for exhausting local staff on individual requests.

✓ Transferable

Acting before complaints mirrors §434(iii); measuring resolution rather than referral and publishing departmental results would strengthen 1823.

✗ Not transferable

Beijing relies on an integrated sub-district system; Hong Kong departments act under statutory powers and district offices cannot direct them, hence lead departments and escalation.

新加坡

組屋由市鎮理事會負責管理和維修，資金來自住戶雜費；政府以「家居改善計劃」等資助大型翻新；2022年起實施「定期外牆檢查」，樓齡20年以上的建築物每7年須檢查外牆。

公營屋邨維修由機構而非個別業主承擔，減少圍標空間；外牆檢查制度化。

✓ 可借鑑

「強制定期檢查＋專業機構統籌」：香港強制驗樓計劃可與政府招標服務結合，把大維修由業主「自己搞」轉為有專業機構把關。

✗ 不可照搬

約八成新加坡居民住組屋並由市鎮理事會統一管理；香港私人樓宇業權分散、每幢大廈各自成立法團，不能複製市鎮理事會模式。

Singapore

Town councils manage and maintain public estates from service charges; the Government subsidises major upgrading such as the Home Improvement Programme; since 2022 buildings over 20 years old must have façades inspected every seven years.

Professional bodies, not individual owners, run estate maintenance, leaving little room for bid-rigging; façade checks are institutionalised.

✓ Transferable

Mandatory periodic inspection plus professional co-ordination: Hong Kong’s mandatory inspection scheme could be joined to government tender services.

✗ Not transferable

About four in five Singaporeans live in town-council estates; Hong Kong’s private buildings each have their own owners’ corporation, so the model cannot be copied.

日本

2025年修訂《建築物區分所有法》（2026年起施行），處理業主失聯或長期缺席令大廈難以達到表決門檻的問題，例如容許部分事項以出席業主為基礎表決。

方向與香港相反：日本放寬門檻以解決「決定不了」，香港提高門檻以防止「被操控」。

✓ 可借鑑

對照說明門檻設計要針對病因：香港可「分類設門檻」——大型維修採購提高門檻，日常及緊急維修則簡化，以免拖延必要工程。

✗ 不可照搬

日本的問題是高齡化和業主失聯；香港的問題是圍標和授權票操控，病因不同，不能照搬其放寬方向。

Japan

Japan’s 2025 amendments to its condominium ownership law, in force from 2026, address absentee and untraceable owners who make voting thresholds unreachable, for example by basing some decisions on owners present.

The opposite direction: Japan lowers thresholds so decisions can be made; Hong Kong raises them to stop capture.

✓ Transferable

The contrast shows thresholds must fit the problem: raise them for large procurement, simplify them for routine and urgent repairs.

✗ Not transferable

Japan’s problem is ageing and absent owners; Hong Kong’s is bid-rigging and proxy capture, so its loosening cannot be copied.

韓國

2014年世越號沉船後重組災難管理體制；2022年梨泰院人群擠壓事故後，檢討「無主辦者活動」的責任空白並推動人流管理改革。

重大事故後的制度改革與個人問責同時進行，並牽涉長時間的調查和政治爭議。

✓ 可借鑑

先以獨立調查查明制度漏洞，再按報告全面改革；列出「責任空白」清單（例如私人樓宇工程由誰監督）——與香港先推優先改革、待獨立委員會報告再全面改革的做法相呼應。

✗ 不可照搬

韓國事故後機構反覆重組、政治化嚴重；香港行政主導體制下應聚焦專業調查和具體改革，而非頻繁重組部門。

South Korea

After the 2014 Sewol ferry disaster Korea reorganised disaster management; after the 2022 Itaewon crowd crush it examined gaps in responsibility for events with no organiser and reformed crowd management.

Institutional reform and individual accountability ran in parallel, with long investigations and political dispute.

✓ Transferable

Independent investigation first, comprehensive reform after; list the gaps where no one is responsible — consistent with Hong Kong’s priority reforms pending the committee’s report.

✗ Not transferable

Korea reorganised repeatedly amid heavy politicisation; Hong Kong should focus on professional investigation and concrete reform rather than restructuring departments.

政策目的

Policy objective

提升政府的執行力與問責：讓政策「可操作、可考核、可問責」地落地；在公務員編制縮減下以科技和流程重組維持服務；在城市安全（樓宇、消防、極端天氣）上由事後補救轉向事前防範。

To strengthen delivery and accountability: policies that are workable, measurable and accountable; services sustained through technology and process redesign as the civil service shrinks; and a shift in urban safety — buildings, fire, extreme weather — from remedy after the event to prevention before it.

核心矛盾

Core tension

行政主導要求政府「敢於擔當、善於作為」，但公務員體系的傳統優勢是按規則、按程序、避免出錯；強化問責（兩級調查、賞罰分明）可能令人員更保守，而縮減編制又同時要求更高效率。樓宇安全方面，政府介入私人大廈維修愈深，愈承擔原本屬於業主的責任和風險。

Executive-led governance asks officials to take ownership and act, yet the civil service’s traditional strength is following rules and avoiding error; tougher accountability (two-tier investigations, sharper appraisal) may make officers more cautious just as a shrinking establishment demands more efficiency. In building safety, the deeper the Government goes into private maintenance, the more of the owners’ responsibility and risk it takes on.

現況與結構

Context數字以 2026 年 9 月官方文件為準
2026 現況

公務員編制約19萬個職位，2026-27及2027-28年度每年縮減2%，至2026年4月1日約188 000個職位，本屆任期累計削減超過1萬個職位（2026-27年度財政預算案）。《公務員敍用委員會（調查）規例》2026年7月實施，「部門首長責任制」和「行政責任兩級調查機制」推行中。2025年11月大埔宏福苑大火後，由法官主持的獨立委員會須於2026年10月底前提交報告；政府已優先推行多項樓宇維修及消防規管改革。地區治理以區議會、關愛隊和地區「三會」為「三駕馬車」。

Current position

The civil service establishment of about 190 000 posts is being cut by 2 per cent a year in 2026-27 and 2027-28, to about 188 000 on 1 April 2026, with over 10 000 posts cut over this term (2026-27 Budget). The Public Service Commission (Investigation) Regulation took effect in July 2026, alongside a heads-of-department accountability system and a two-tier investigation mechanism. After the Wang Fuk Court fire in Tai Po in November 2025, a judge-led independent committee is due to report by the end of October 2026, and the Government has prioritised reforms to building maintenance and fire regulation. District governance rests on the “troika” of District Councils, Care Teams and the three district committees.

主要問題

Key problems寫機制，不寫症狀

1跨部門事務無人牽頭

市民投訴涉及多個部門時容易互相推卸；1823「轉介快」但未必「辦得成」。

1No one leads on cross-departmental issues

Complaints involving several departments are easily passed around; 1823 refers quickly but does not always resolve.

2問責與積極性的張力

兩級調查和紀律程序加強後，人員可能傾向「不做不錯」；需要同時有獎勵勇於擔當的機制。

2Accountability versus initiative

Tougher investigations and discipline may encourage “do nothing, do no wrong”; rewards for taking ownership are needed alongside.

3私人樓宇大維修的市場失靈

業主缺乏專業知識、法團易被少數人操控、圍標難以舉證；監管分散在屋宇署、消防處和民政總署。

3Market failure in private building maintenance

Owners lack expertise, owners’ corporations are easily captured, bid-rigging is hard to prove, and oversight is split across the Buildings Department, Fire Services and Home Affairs.

4縮編與服務需求上升

人口高齡化、城市管理日趨複雜，但編制縮減；若流程和科技跟不上，服務質素會下降。

4A smaller establishment, rising demand

Ageing and more complex urban management raise demand while posts shrink; if processes and technology lag, service quality falls.

正反論點與反駁

Arguments and rebuttals中文卷的利弊段＋駁論段
兩邊都站得住：每個論點下面都配了反駁。選了立場之後，對方的論點＋你的反駁就是你的駁論段；而你自己這一邊的論點下面那條反駁，是你要預先化解的弱點。練法：同一條題目寫兩次大綱，一次支持、一次反對。
支持 1

部門首長責任制和兩級調查令責任清晰，市民看到問責，有助重建信任。

站反方？這樣駁

問責清晰不等於治理改善：若調查和紀律程序只針對前線，首長和制度設計的問題仍未處理，信任不會因此恢復。

FOR 1

Heads-of-department accountability and two-tier investigations clarify responsibility; visible accountability helps rebuild trust.

Counter

Clear accountability is not better governance: if investigations target only the front line, leadership and design failures remain and trust does not return.

支持 2

政府介入大維修招標、設預審名單，可打破圍標集團的資訊和組織優勢，保障業主（特別是長者業主）。

站反方？這樣駁

政府介入招標可以打擊圍標，但也可能令業主以為「政府名單就是保證」，放鬆監督；把關者本身亦需要被監察。

FOR 2

Government involvement in major maintenance tenders and pre-qualified lists breaks the information and organisational advantage of bid-rigging rings, protecting owners, especially elderly ones.

Counter

Government involvement can curb bid-rigging but may lull owners into treating the official list as a guarantee; the gatekeeper itself needs oversight.

支持 3

以AI和數碼化配合縮編，可把人手集中在高價值工作，同時控制經常開支。

站反方？這樣駁

AI釋放的人手要有明確去向；若縮編快於科技成熟，結果只是前線工作量增加。

FOR 3

AI and digitalisation alongside a smaller establishment focus staff on higher-value work while containing recurrent costs.

Counter

Staff freed by AI need a clear destination; if cuts outpace mature technology, the result is simply heavier frontline workloads.

反對／制約 1

問責文化過強可能令人員不敢創新，與「以結果為目標」的要求相矛盾。

站支持方？這樣駁

問責與擔當可以並行：評核改革同時嘉獎創新和跨部門合作，並設「容錯」指引，關鍵在設計而非放棄問責。

AGAINST 1

An overly punitive culture may deter innovation, contradicting the results-oriented approach.

Counter

Accountability and initiative can coexist: appraisal reform rewards innovation and collaboration and can include fault-tolerance guidance — the answer is design, not dropping accountability.

反對／制約 2

政府介入私人大廈維修可能被視為提供擔保；工程出事時政府亦可能被追究責任。

站支持方？這樣駁

政府角色可清楚界定為「把關人」而非「承建商」：提供招標服務和名單，合約仍由業主與承建商訂立，責任邊界可以寫清。

AGAINST 2

Government involvement in private maintenance may be seen as a guarantee, and the Government may be blamed when works go wrong.

Counter

The Government’s role can be defined as gatekeeper, not contractor: it provides tender services and lists while contracts remain between owners and contractors, with liability spelt out.

反對／制約 3

縮編若快於科技應用，會加重前線工作量、影響士氣和服務質素。

站支持方？這樣駁

縮編按2%逐年推行，並配合流程重組和AI項目（已落實30個）；部門可按服務優次調配，並非一刀切。

AGAINST 3

If cuts outpace technology, frontline workloads, morale and service quality suffer.

Counter

Cuts proceed at 2 per cent a year alongside process redesign and 30 delivered AI projects, and departments reprioritise rather than cut across the board.

Policy options按政策工具階梯由輕到重

執行挑戰與化解

Implementation challenges五層任務的第三、四層
挑戰 1

牽頭部門權責不對等：牽頭部門無權指揮其他部門。

化解

設由政務司副司長介入的上報機制；把跨部門成效計入部門首長的評核。

Challenge 1

The lead department lacks authority over others.

Mitigation

An escalation route to the Deputy Chief Secretary; count cross-departmental results in heads of departments’ appraisals.

挑戰 2

提高業主大會門檻可能令維修更難通過，舊樓失修加劇。

化解

門檻提高只適用於大型維修的採購決定；配合政府招標服務和資助，降低業主參與成本。

Challenge 2

Higher meeting thresholds may make repairs harder to approve and worsen dilapidation.

Mitigation

Apply higher thresholds only to large-scale maintenance procurement, with government tender services and subsidies lowering owners’ costs of taking part.

挑戰 3

評核「區分表現」引起公務員團體反彈。

化解

清晰準則、上訴機制、兩個評核周期後檢討；獎勵與懲處並重。

Challenge 3

Sharper appraisal provokes civil service unions.

Mitigation

Clear criteria, appeals and a review after two cycles, with rewards as well as penalties.
